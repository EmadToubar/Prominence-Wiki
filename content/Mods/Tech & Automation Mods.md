# Tech & Automation Mods

Several parallel tech trees, each with its own Quest Book chapter. Pick one or two to specialize — see the [[Progression & Level Caps|mid-game stage]] for when to start.

## Create
The showpiece automation mod (6.0.8.1). Everything runs on real, visible mechanical power — shafts, gears, water wheels, contraptions you build by hand and watch turn. Water wheels only spin under genuinely *flowing* water, not a still pool.

## Applied Energistics 2
The endgame storage-and-autocrafting system: one digital ME network that holds, sorts, and auto-crafts from thousands of items instead of a wall of chests. Steep learning curve, enormous payoff — the real answer to "how do I auto-sort my storage."

## Tech Reborn
A complete, self-contained classic tech tree — tools, machines, ore processing — for progressing through "generate power, build bigger machines" instead of Create's contraptions.

## Modern Industrialization
Another full industrial tech tree, heavier on multiblock machines and chemistry-flavored processing chains.

## Powah!
The pack's accessible energy mod — generators, batteries, and cables that wire into whichever tech tree you're actually playing.

## Create addons and companions (grouped)
Create: New Age (bridges kinetic power into electricity), Create Crafts & Additions (Electric Motor/Alternator converting Forge energy ↔ kinetic power), Create: Estrogen (Create-flavored module for the Estrogen mod), Create: Steam 'n' Rails (rail/steam expansion), Create: Copycats+ (disguise blocks), Colorwheel (Iris shader + Flywheel compat).

## Estrogen
The base mod behind Create: Estrogen — its own fluid handling mechanics and a dash ability.

## Other tech trees & AE2 extras (grouped)
Industrial Revolution (another full industrial tech tree) + its EMI Plugin. Alloy Forgery (configurable alloy smelting pre-full-tech-tree). ExtendedAE, MEGA Cells, ME Requester, AE2WTLib (all extend AE2 — QoL content, huge storage cells, auto-restocking, wireless terminals). Applied Botanics (bridges [[Magic & Spells Mods|Botania]] mana into an AE2 network). OnlyExcavators and Just Hammers (3x3-area mining tools). Polymorphic Energistics (AE2 recipes work with the Polymorph system, see [[Quality-of-Life & UI Mods]]).
