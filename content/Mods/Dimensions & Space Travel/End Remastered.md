# End Remastered

End Remastered (by jack_bagel Dev, maxmitchelson Dev, and artists gilvoisard/And; mod id `endrem`, `fabric-end-remastered-5.2.4.jar`) reworks the classic "hunt down a stronghold with Eyes of Ender" loop by splitting the single vanilla Eye of Ender into **17 themed variants**, each tied to a different source: the **Old Eye** (desert pyramid loot), **Rogue Eye** (jungle temple), **Nether Eye** (nether bridge chest), **Cold Eye** (igloo), **Magical Eye** (woodland mansion or evoker drop), **Black Eye** (buried treasure), **Lost Eye** (abandoned mineshaft), **Wither Eye** (wither drop), **Guardian Eye** (elder guardian drop), **Cursed Eye** (bastion treasure), **Exotic Eye**, **Evil Eye**, **Undead Eye** (skeleton horse), **Cryptic Eye**, **Corrupted Eye** (pillager outpost), **Witch Eye/Witch Pupil** (witch drop), each with its own flavor-text lore entry and a matching advancement ("Fortune Seeker," "Welcome To The Jungle," "Arghh, Where be Me Ship?," etc.).

On this server the mod is configured conservatively via `config/endrem.json`: vanilla Eye of Ender usage and throwing are both **disabled** (`ENABLE_EYE_OF_ENDER: false`, `THROW_EYE_OF_ENDER: false`), and eyes don't randomly spawn already-placed in portal frames — so the alternate-eye loot hunt is the primary intended path here rather than a bonus layered on top of vanilla eye-throwing. Both the Cryptic Eye and Evil Eye are left obtainable. As a lightweight, config-driven "reforge the path to the End" mod, it complements the heavier worldgen overhauls in this category ([[Better End]], [[Eldritch End]]) without touching biomes or terrain itself.

## Anticipated questions

**Q: Why can't I craft or throw a normal Eye of Ender?**
A: On this server End Remastered disables vanilla Eye of Ender usage and throwing (`ENABLE_EYE_OF_ENDER: false`, `THROW_EYE_OF_ENDER: false`), so the alternate-eye loot hunt is the intended path to the End rather than vanilla eye-throwing.

**Q: How do I open the End portal then?**
A: You collect from 17 themed Eye variants, each tied to a different source — e.g. the Old Eye from a desert pyramid, Nether Eye from a nether bridge chest, Cold Eye from an igloo, or Magical Eye from a woodland mansion / evoker drop — and each has its own advancement.

**Q: Where do all the eyes come from?**
A: They're tied to structures and mobs across the world — desert pyramids, jungle temples, igloos, woodland mansions, buried treasure, abandoned mineshafts, bastions, and pillager outposts among them; see [[Structures]].
