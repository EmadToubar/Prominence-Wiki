# Deeper and Darker

Deeper and Darker (by Kyanite Mods, mod id `deeperdarker`, `deeperdarker-fabric-1.20-1.3.3-plus-b.jar`) is an entirely new dimension called **the Otherside**, reached by expanding on the vanilla Deep Dark's sculk theme rather than replacing an existing dimension. It adds four biomes — **Deeplands** (the hub biome), **Blooming Caverns**, **Echoing Forest**, and **Overcast Columns** — continuing the sculk/warden aesthetic into fully explorable terrain instead of the small pocket biome vanilla offers.

Its mob roster leans into "corrupted deep-sea/sculk horror" themes: the **Angler Fish**, **Sculk Centipede**, **Sculk Leech**, **Sculk Snapper**, **Shattered**, **Shriek Worm**, **Sludge**, and **Stalker** all populate the Otherside with escalating threat, alongside dimension-specific boats. Server-side config (`config/deeperdarker.json5`) exists for tuning generation and mob behavior. As a standalone new dimension rather than an overworld/Nether/End rework, it sits alongside [[The Bumblezone]] as one of the pack's "extra pocket dimensions" rather than a vanilla-dimension overhaul like [[Better End]] or [[Better Nether (BWNCR)]].
