# Pirate's Doom

Pirate's Doom (by Kyber_6 and Pixel Dream Studios, mod id `piratesdoom`, `piratesdoom-1.0.0-fabric.jar`) is a small pirate-themed Nether content mod — per its own tagline, "because nothing says 'game over' to piracy like a fiery descent into the depths of hell." It's a lightweight codebase (a single Fabric entrypoint class plus a file-loading utility, no dedicated lang file or config in this pack), suggesting its content is largely data/resource-driven rather than a large new systems layer, in keeping with its role as a smaller thematic add-on rather than a full dimension overhaul.

It sits alongside [[Better Nether (BWNCR)|Better Nether]] as Nether-flavored content in this category, giving the Nether a pirates-meet-hellscape theme to complement Better Nether's biome and mob overhaul.
