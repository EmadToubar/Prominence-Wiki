# The Bumblezone

The Bumblezone (by TelepathicGrunt, mod id `the_bumblezone`, `the_bumblezone-7.9.10+1.20.1-fabric.jar`) is a full bee-themed pocket dimension with a genuinely large content footprint — its English lang file alone carries over 1,300 entries, easily the biggest of the smaller-dimension mods in this category. Its biomes read like a honeycomb fever dream: **Pollinated Fields** and **Pollinated Pillar**, **Sugar Water Floor**, **Hive Wall** and **Hive Pillar**, **Crystal Canyon**, **Floral Meadows**, **Howling Constructs**, and the aptly-named **Bumbling Beepartments** — plus its own "Beeography" completion tracker for visiting every biome, mirroring [[Better End]] and [[Better Nether (BWNCR)]]'s biome-completion achievements.

The dimension has real threats, not just cosmetic bee theming: **Beehemoth** and **Queen Beehemoth** anchor a boss-tier "Bee Queen" fight (with a visible Friendship meter), backed by mobs like the **Honey Slime**, **Rootmin**, **Sentry Watcher**, and assorted projectile/hazard entities (Pollen Puff, Dirt Pellet, Stinger Spear, Bee Stinger, Honey Crystal Shard, Electric Ring/Purple Spike/Cosmic Crystal entities). Per the mod's own description, entry is **advancement-gated** — the mod's tagline explicitly points players to "check out the advancements for how to play the mod," meaning access isn't as simple as building a portal from day one. Server config lives at `config/the_bumblezone.json`.

## Anticipated questions

**Q: How do I get into the Bumblezone?**
A: Entry is advancement-gated — the mod itself points players to "check out the advancements for how to play the mod," so access isn't as simple as building a portal on day one.

**Q: Is there a boss to fight?**
A: Yes — the Beehemoth and Queen Beehemoth anchor a boss-tier "Bee Queen" fight (with a visible Friendship meter), backed by mobs like the Honey Slime, Rootmin, and Sentry Watcher; see [[Progression & Level Caps]].

**Q: What's actually in the dimension?**
A: A full bee-themed pocket dimension with biomes like Pollinated Fields, Sugar Water Floor, Crystal Canyon, Floral Meadows, and the Bumbling Beepartments, plus a "Beeography" tracker for visiting them all.
