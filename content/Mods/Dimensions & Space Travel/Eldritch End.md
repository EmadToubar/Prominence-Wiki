# Eldritch End

Eldritch End (by ElocinDev, mod id `eldritch_end`, `Eldritch_End-FABRIC-MC1.20.1-0.3.4.jar`) is a Lovecraft-inspired horror expansion built on top of the End dimension. Its two biomes are the **Hasturian Wastes** and the **Primordial Abyss** — notably, "Hasturian" directly echoes this modpack's own subtitle, *Prominence II: Hasturian Era*, making this one of the more thematically load-bearing mods in the pack rather than incidental flavor content.

It adds a genuinely unsettling bestiary: **The Eldritch Eye**, **The Faceless** (with "Hastur's Image" as a related entry), **Aberration**, **Ominous Eye**, **Tentacle** and **Undead Tentacle**, and **Dendler**, alongside Lovecraftian-flavored gear and loot — a **Necronomicon**, **Eldritch Artifact**, **Corrupted/Corruption Relic**, **Silver Key**, and a full Etyrite armor set (helmet/chestplate/leggings/boots) crafted from a new "Etyr" ore/ingot line. A named boss encounter, **X'al, The Faceless Eye**, appears as a summonable/spawnable threat with an area-damage void-energy attack. It depends on the `necronomicon` and `azurelib` libraries. `config/eldritch_end/` holds its server-side tuning.
