# BetterEnd Crashed Ships

A small, focused patch mod (by MuonR, mod id `betterend-crashed-ships`, `betterend-crashed-ships-1.0.0.jar`) that depends directly on and reworks a single feature of [[Better End]]: its **crashed ships** structure feature. It works via a single mixin (`CrashedShipFeatureMixin`) rather than adding new blocks, biomes, or items of its own — this is a targeted improvement/fix to how crashed ships generate in the End rather than new explorable content in its own right.

Because it's a pure dependent add-on, it only matters if [[Better End]] is installed and its crashed-ship structures are in play; it has no standalone function.
