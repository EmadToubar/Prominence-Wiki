# Dragonfight Mod

Dragonfight Mod (by Someaddons, mod id `dragonfight`, `dragonfight-fabric-1.20.1-4.7.jar`, requires the `cupboard` library) rebuilds the vanilla Ender Dragon encounter into a genuinely harder fight rather than the usual "shoot crystals, hit dragon during perch" pattern. Per this server's `config/dragonfight.json`, the pack runs it noticeably tougher than default: **dragon difficulty is set to 2** (vanilla is 0, mod default is already 2), **mob spawn amount is doubled** (`mobSpawnAmountModifier: 2.0`) so more reinforcements pour in during the fight, phantoms spawn when a crystal is destroyed at range and blazes+phantoms spawn on crystal respawn to punish players who hang back, and the dragon has **explosion damage immunity** enabled (so crystal detonations near it don't chip its health for free). Working in the mod's favor for players, though, **lightning strikes are disabled** entirely (`disableLightning: true`) and its **anti-flight ability is turned off** (`antiflightAbility: false`), so elytra/rocket flight isn't punished mid-fight — a deliberate softening given the pack also leans on flight via [[Ad Astra]] gear and `allow-flight=true` server-wide.

New hazards introduced by the mod include a custom "weakexplosion" death message, a gravity-affecting "dragon's flight magic" debuff that can be inflicted mid-fight (with its own warning/hurt messages), and configurable custom mob-spawn lists tied to crystal destruction/respawn events. It's a pure combat-difficulty layer on the vanilla End fight — no new biomes or blocks — making it a natural pairing with the worldgen-focused [[Better End]] and the eye-hunt rework in [[End Remastered]].

## Anticipated questions

**Q: How is the Ender Dragon fight different here?**
A: Dragonfight Mod rebuilds it into a genuinely harder encounter — on this server dragon difficulty is set to 2, mob spawns are doubled, phantoms spawn when you destroy a crystal from range, blazes and phantoms spawn on crystal respawn, and the dragon has explosion-damage immunity so crystal blasts near it don't chip its health for free.

**Q: Can I still use elytra or rocket flight during the fight?**
A: Yes — the mod's anti-flight ability is turned off and lightning strikes are disabled on this server, a deliberate softening since the pack leans on flight via [[Ad Astra]] gear and server-wide `allow-flight`.

**Q: How tough is it — should I prepare?**
A: Noticeably tougher than vanilla, with doubled reinforcements and a gravity-affecting "dragon's flight magic" debuff that can hit you mid-fight, so come well geared; see [[Progression & Level Caps]].
