# PigPen

PigPen (Darkhax, `pigpen`, v15.0.3) brings "the classic Pig Pen Cipher" into Minecraft as an in-game font, the same substitution cipher used for real-world puzzle boxes and ARGs, for anyone building secret messages, puzzle rooms, or lore text into signs and books. Like its sibling mod [[Runelic]] (same author, same minimal structure — just a font plus ModMenu contact links), it adds no gameplay systems of its own.
