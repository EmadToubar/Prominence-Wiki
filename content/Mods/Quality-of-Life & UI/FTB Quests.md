# FTB Quests

FTB Quests (`ftbquests`, v2001.4.13, by the FTB Team) is the Quest Book itself — Prominence II's structured progression system, guiding players from "punch a tree" through the pack's dozens of integrated mods via chaptered, reward-granting checklists rather than leaving discovery entirely to wiki-diving or [[EMI]] lookups. It's the mod referenced directly in [[Getting Started]] and is functionally the pack's spine: it depends on Architectury, and on [[FTB Library]] and [[FTB Teams]] to function at all.

This server's live quest data (`config/ftbquests/quests/chapters/`) contains **33 chapters**, and their names map almost one-to-one onto the pack's major integrated mods and content pillars: `getting_started`, `main_story`, and `elhasturian_era` alongside dedicated chapters for `applied_energistics_2`, `botania`, `create`, `tech_reborn`, `modern_industrialization`, `industrial_revolution`, `powah`, `the_nether`, `the_bumblezone`, `deeper_and_darker`, `to_the_end`, `dplanets_and_space` (Ad Astra-style space progression), `eenchanting`, `e_spell_books`, `c_bewitchment`, `archon`, `artifact_reforging`, `d_the_art_of_transmogrification`, `simply_swords`, `gear_mariums_soulslike_weaponry`, `reforging`, `gadgets`, `8the_prision`, `the_scarred_night`, `dmastering_fatew`, `4trophy_collection`, `collectibles`/`collectibles_2`, and a seasonal `a_jolly_christmas` chapter. A separate `reward_tables/` directory (runes, wands, tier-4 rings, transmog skins, metal blocks, eyes, spell enchants) backs randomized quest reward pools rather than fixed single-item payouts. The Starter Kit hands every new player an `ftbquests:book` on spawn (see [[Starter Kit]]), meaning the quest book is quite literally the first item a new player holds.

## Anticipated questions
- **What does this mod do?** It's the Quest Book — Prominence II's structured progression system, guiding you from "punch a tree" through the pack's dozens of mods via chaptered, reward-granting checklists.
- **How many chapters are there?** 33, whose names map almost one-to-one onto the pack's major mods and content pillars (Create, Botania, AE2, Tech Reborn, the Nether, To The End, and more).
