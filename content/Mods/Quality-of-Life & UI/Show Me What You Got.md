# Show Me What You Got

Show Me What You Got (`smwyg`, Apace, v1.1.1) lets you post the item currently in your hand into chat as a rich, hoverable link other players can inspect — the classic "check this out" flex, without them needing to be standing next to you. It's a tiny mod with one piece of user-facing text: chat shows `[stale link]` if someone clicks an item link that's no longer valid (e.g. the sender's game session ended or the item changed).
