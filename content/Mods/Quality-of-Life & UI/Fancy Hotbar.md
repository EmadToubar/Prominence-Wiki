# Fancy Hotbar

Fancy Hotbar (`fancy_hotbar`, ElocinDev, v1.0.0) is a purely cosmetic HUD mod that redraws the vanilla hotbar and experience counter using a resource pack, described in its own metadata as "fancy hotbar for your hotbary needs, requires a resourcepack!" `config/fancy_hotbar.json5` has both pieces enabled — `custom_hotbar.enable` and `custom_experience_number.enable` are both `true`, with the XP counter tinted a custom gold color (`xp_color: 16099138`) and no offsets applied to either element's position.
