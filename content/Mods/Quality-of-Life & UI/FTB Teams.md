# FTB Teams

FTB Teams (`ftbteams`, v2001.3.1, by the FTB Team) adds the ability to form parties/teams whose members share [[FTB Quests]] progress and rewards — it's the mod that turns quest completion from a solo checklist into something a group can tackle together, so one player claiming a quest reward doesn't leave teammates locked out. It depends on Architectury and [[FTB Library]], and FTB Quests itself declares a hard dependency on FTB Teams (`>=2001.1.4`) — quests can't function without it.

Team-based kill credit specifically (making sure any team member's kill counts toward a shared kill-quest) is handled by the separate add-on [[Quest Kill Task]], since base FTB Teams only manages team membership/permissions, not quest-objective-specific credit logic.
