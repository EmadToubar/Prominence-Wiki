# Runelic

Runelic (Darkhax, `runelic`, v18.0.4) adds a "Runelic" font system to the game — a fantasy rune alphabet usable as an in-game font, typically for signs, books, or lecterns that want to look mystical rather than legible. It's a tiny, focused utility mod: no gameplay mechanics, just a font resource plus the surrounding Fabric plumbing (Bookshelf dependency, ModMenu links to Darkhax's Discord/Patreon/CurseForge pages). It shares an author and a near-identical structure with [[PigPen]], another Darkhax font/cipher mod in this pack.
