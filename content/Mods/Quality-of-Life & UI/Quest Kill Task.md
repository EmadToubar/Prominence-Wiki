# Quest Kill Task

Quest Kill Task (`questkilltask`, v0.2.0, by MuonR) is a small, focused add-on for [[FTB Quests]] that adds **team-based kill credit**: without it, a "kill 10 zombies" quest task only tracks kills made by the specific player who claimed/started that task. With Quest Kill Task installed, any kill made by any member of the same [[FTB Teams]] party counts toward the shared objective, so a group tackling a quest chapter together isn't forced to grind identical kill counters in parallel.

It's a thin, dependency-driven mod (`fabric-api`, Minecraft 1.20.1, Java 17+) with no meaningful config of its own — it exists purely to patch this one specific gap in FTB Quests' default kill-task behavior.
