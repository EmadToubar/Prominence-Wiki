# Extra Mod Integrations

Extra Mod Integrations (`extra-mod-integrations`, v0.4.7, by Kneelawk) is EMI's broad-spectrum compatibility addon — its stated goal is literally "support for as many mods as possible." Where the other EMI addons in this pack are narrow and single-purpose (loot tables, enchantments, ore locations), this one is the catch-all that renders recipe types the base EMI API doesn't otherwise understand cleanly, filling gaps left by mods that implement unusual or non-standard crafting systems.

It sits alongside two more targeted, mod-specific EMI plugins also present in the pack — `indrev-emi-plugin` for Industrial Revolution and `ae2-emi-crafting` for Applied Energistics 2 (currently disabled) — rather than replacing them; Extra Mod Integrations covers the long tail everything else misses.
