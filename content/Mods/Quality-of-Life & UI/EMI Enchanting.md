# EMI Enchanting

EMI Enchanting (`emi_enchanting`, v0.1.2, by fzzyhmstrs) is an EMI addon dedicated to enchantment information: look up an enchantment and see how to obtain it (enchanting table odds, loot sources), what items it can legally be applied to, and — importantly in a modpack this size, where multiple mods add competing or overlapping enchantments — which other enchantments it's mutually exclusive with. That exclusion data is normally buried in code/data-pack files; surfacing it in EMI avoids the trial-and-error of finding out an enchant conflict only after wasting an anvil combine.

It's a lightweight, config-free plugin (no entry in `config/` beyond what EMI itself provides) from the same author as EMI Loot, and the two are commonly installed as a pair.
