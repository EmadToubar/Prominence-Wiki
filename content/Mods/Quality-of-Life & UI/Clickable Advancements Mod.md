# Clickable Advancements Mod

Clickable Advancements Mod (`clickadv`, Someaddons, v1.20.1-3.8) does exactly what it says: advancement toasts that get posted to chat become clickable links you can open directly, instead of having to dig through the Advancements screen to find what you just earned. The one setting in `config/clickadv.json`, `showAllInLocalChat` (default `true`), broadens vanilla's behavior — normally chat only shows advancements that get broadcast server-wide, but with this on every advancement you personally earn posts locally too, including "hidden text" category-only advancements.
