# Villager Names

Villager Names (Rick South / Serilum, `villagernames`, v8.2) gives every villager and villager-like entity (wandering traders, zombie villagers, etc.) a randomly assigned personal name instead of the plain "Villager" label, using separate male and female name pools. `config/villagernames.json5` has this pack running with `_useCustomNames: true` alongside the built-in `_useMaleNames`/`_useFemaleNames` lists, meaning names are drawn from both the mod's defaults and a custom list editable in `mods/villagernames/customnames.txt`; `nameModdedVillagers: true` extends naming to villager-like entities added by other mods in the pack, not just vanilla ones. Shares its author, dependency stack (Serilum's Collective library), and overall structure with [[Fish On The Line]].
