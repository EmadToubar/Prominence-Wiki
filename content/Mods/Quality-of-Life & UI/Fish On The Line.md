# Fish On The Line

Fish On The Line (`fishontheline`, Rick South / Serilum, v3.5) is a single-mechanic QoL mod: it rings a bell held in your offhand the moment something bites your fishing line, so you don't have to stare at the bobber. The server's `config/fishontheline.json5` keeps the default `mustHoldBellInOffhand = true`, meaning the audio cue only fires if a bell is actually equipped offhand while fishing — no bell, no ring. It ships a ModMenu config-screen integration so the setting is reachable in-game.
