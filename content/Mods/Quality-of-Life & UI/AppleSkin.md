# AppleSkin

AppleSkin (`appleskin`, v2.5.2, by squeek502) is a long-running, well-known QoL mod that adds food-related HUD information Minecraft normally hides: exact hunger/saturation values on food tooltips, a saturation overlay on the hunger bar, and a preview of exactly how much hunger/health a food item in hand would restore before eating it.

This server's `appleskin.json5` has essentially every overlay turned on: tooltip values shown automatically without needing to hold Shift (`showFoodValuesInTooltipAlways = true`), the saturation HUD overlay, the hunger/health-restore-preview overlays for both main-hand and off-hand food, a food-exhaustion progress bar underlay, vanilla icon-shake animation matching, and food debug info in the F3 screen — nothing is left at a partial/minimal config, meaning the pack wants full nutritional visibility surfaced by default rather than opt-in.

## Anticipated questions
- **What does this mod add?** Food HUD info Minecraft normally hides: exact hunger/saturation values on tooltips, a saturation overlay on the hunger bar, and a preview of how much hunger/health a held food would restore before eating.
- **Do I have to hold Shift to see food values?** No — `showFoodValuesInTooltipAlways = true`, so tooltip values show automatically without holding Shift.
- **Does the restore preview work for off-hand food too?** Yes — the preview overlays are enabled for both main-hand and off-hand food.
- **Is this a stripped-down config here?** No — essentially every overlay is on (saturation HUD, exhaustion bar underlay, F3 debug food info), so the pack surfaces full nutritional info by default.
