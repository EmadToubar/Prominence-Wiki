# EMI Ores

EMI Ores (`emi_ores`, v1.2, by Abbie) adds ore-generation information directly into EMI's item lookup: check an ore item and see where it actually spawns in the world — biome restrictions, Y-level ranges, vein characteristics — without alt-tabbing to a wiki or JEI-style worldgen viewer mod. In a pack with dozens of modded ores stacked across vanilla, Tech Reborn, Modern Industrialization, and Industrial Revolution (later reconciled by Almost Unified), knowing *where* to dig for a specific material is exactly the kind of lookup this closes.

It's a minimal addon — this install's `emi_ores-client.json` is a 33-byte stub, so it's running effectively stock/default settings.
