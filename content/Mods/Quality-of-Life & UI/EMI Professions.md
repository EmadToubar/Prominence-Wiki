# EMI Professions

EMI Professions (`emiprofessions`, v1.0.4, by Mrbysco & ShyNieke) is a small EMI addon that adds villager profession information into EMI's item lookups. Looking up a profession-related item (a job-site block like a lectern, smoker, or fletching table) surfaces which villager profession it assigns and what that profession trades, folding villager-trading knowledge into the same lookup workflow players already use for crafting recipes instead of requiring a separate wiki check.

It requires EMI 1.1.10+ and Minecraft 1.20.1+Java 17, and is purely additive — it has no config file of its own in this install and just registers extra recipe-viewer pages on top of the base EMI catalog.
