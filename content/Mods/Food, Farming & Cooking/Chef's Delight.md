# Chef's Delight

Jar: `chefs-delight-1.0.4-fabric-1.20.1.jar` (mod id `chefsdelight`, by Redstone Games). This is the thinnest of the Farmer's Delight add-ons in the pack — its entire `en_us.json` is four lines. It adds exactly two new villager professions, **Cook** and **Chef**, giving Farmer's Delight's cooking loop a dedicated villager trade chain (buy/sell FD ingredients and dishes through a profession built for it) instead of leaving those trades scattered across vanilla professions or absent entirely.

There's no new block, item, or crop here — it's a small, focused compatibility/flavor addition that hard-depends on `farmersdelight` (declared in its `fabric.mod.json`) and exists purely to give the cooking-focused half of a Farmer's Delight village something to do for a living.

## How you'll use it here
This mod is invisible until you get a villager into the **Cook** or **Chef** profession, at which point it becomes your trade route for [[Farmer's Delight]] ingredients and finished dishes. Set it up mid-game once you have a village and a working FD kitchen — it lets you buy the FD staples you haven't farmed yet, or sell surplus cooked food for emeralds. There are no blocks or recipes to craft yourself here; the whole value is the villager economy it plugs into.

## Anticipated questions
**What does this actually add?** Two villager professions, Cook and Chef, giving Farmer's Delight's cooking loop its own dedicated trade chain instead of leaving those trades scattered across vanilla professions or absent.

**Do I need it to cook?** No — cooking works fine without it; this only adds villagers to trade FD ingredients and dishes through.

**How do I get a Cook villager?** Generally the same way as any villager profession — an unemployed villager claims the profession's workstation placed nearby.
