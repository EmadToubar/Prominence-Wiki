# End's Delight

Jar: `ends_delight-refabricated-1.20.1-2.5.jar` (mod id `ends_delight`, by FoggyHillside). A Farmer's Delight add-on (hard-depends on `farmersdelight`) that takes the mod's cooking systems into the End dimension, themed entirely around chorus fruit, shulkers, endermen, and the ender dragon. Its lang file lists 55 items and 8 blocks, including its own dedicated **End Stove** for End-themed cooking.

Highlights of the roster: chorus fruit gets turned into milk tea, bubble tea, wine, popsicles, sauce, and pie; shulkers yield raw/roasted/stir-fried meat, soup, and omelettes; the ender dragon itself is a food source, with raw/roasted/smoked dragon leg and steak, dragon meat stew, and even a "Fried Dragon Egg" and "Liquid Dragon Egg"/"Dragon Egg Shell" line built around the boss's egg; endermen contribute "Enderman Gristle" and gristle stew; and there's a full custom knife line (End Stone Knife, Purpur Knife, Dragon Tooth Knife, Dragon Egg Shell Knife) alongside dedicated blocks like Grilled Shulker, Steamed Dragon Egg, and a Chorus Fruit Crate. It's one of the more elaborate theme add-ons in the category — effectively a full parallel cooking tree for the End, not just a handful of reskinned dishes.

## How you'll use it here
This is late-game cooking — you won't touch it until you already have End access, but once you're out there, chorus fruit, shulkers, endermen and even the ender dragon become a whole parallel menu (milk/bubble tea, shulker soups and omelettes, dragon steak and stew). Because prepared food gives real stat buffs on this server, it's worth cooking your End haul on the dedicated **End Stove** rather than just eating chorus fruit raw. Set up an End Stove out there or bring the ingredients home.

## Anticipated questions
**When do I get to use this?** Late game — the whole roster is built around chorus fruit, shulkers, endermen and the dragon, so you need End access first.

**Do I need Farmer's Delight?** Yes — it hard-depends on `farmersdelight` and extends that cooking system into the End.

**Is there a special workstation?** Yes, its own End Stove for the End-themed dishes.
