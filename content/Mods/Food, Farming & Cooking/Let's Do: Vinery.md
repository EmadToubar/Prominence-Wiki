# Let's Do: Vinery

Jar: `letsdo-vinery-fabric-1.4.41.jar` (mod id `vinery`). The largest of the "Let's Do" content mods by far — its lang file lists 38 items but a huge 144 blocks, more than Brewery and HerbalBrews combined, and it shares the series' furniture/sound plumbing from [[[Let's Do] API]]. Tagline: "In Vino Veritas - it's time for wine! Build your own vineyard, produce wine, and establish your own winery."

Grapes come in Red and White varieties across three biome-flavored strains — regular, Jungle, Savanna, and Taiga grapes — each with seeds, fresh fruit, and their own grapejuice. Apples get folded in too (Apple Press → Apple Juice/Mash → Cider/Wine), and Cherry trees are added as a full wood set (logs, planks-equivalent "beam," boats, chest boats, signs). The wine-making chain is the real depth here: an **Apple Press** and winery blocks turn fruit into wine aged and displayed in per-wood-type **Wine Racks** and **Wine Bottle Storage** (small and big variants exist for Acacia, Bamboo, Birch, Cherry, Crimson, and presumably every other vanilla wood type), plus **Lattices** for growing vines. On top of generic wines it adds a set of named specialty/joke wines — Aegis Wine, Bolvar Wine, Chenet Wine, Chorus Wine, Clark Wine, Cristel Wine, "A Bottle of 'Mojang Noir'," "A Bottle of 'Creepers Crush'" — plus new mobs (Mule and Wandering Winemaker spawn eggs) and winemaker cosmetic armor (apron, boots, leggings) and a Straw Hat. Config lives at `config/vinery.json` and `config/vinery/`.

## How you'll use it here
This is the wine branch and the biggest Let's Do content mod — grow Red and White grapes (with jungle/savanna/taiga strains), run fruit through the **Apple Press** and winery blocks, and age wine in per-wood **Wine Racks** and Wine Bottle Storage. It's a build-heavy vineyard project for mid-to-late game, with Cherry wood sets and winemaker cosmetics to theme a proper winery. It's a separate drinks economy from [[Let's Do: Brewery]]'s beer and [[Let's Do: HerbalBrews]]' tea.

## Anticipated questions
**What do I grow for wine?** Red and White grapes across regular, Jungle, Savanna and Taiga strains; apples also fold in via the Apple Press.

**What actually makes the wine?** The Apple Press and winery blocks turn fruit into wine, aged and displayed in per-wood-type Wine Racks and Wine Bottle Storage.

**Does it need the Let's Do API?** It shares the series' [[[Let's Do] API]] plumbing; its own `fabric.mod.json` doesn't list `doapi` as a hard dependency, but the API ships with the pack anyway.
