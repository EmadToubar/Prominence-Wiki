# Ocean's Delight

Jar: `oceansdelight-fdrf-fabric-1.0.2-1.20.jar` (mod id `oceansdelight`, by Scouter). A Farmer's Delight add-on (hard-depends on `farmersdelight`, note the `fdrf` — "Farmer's Delight Refabricated" — in the filename confirming it targets this pack's specific FD port) themed around ocean/deep-sea food. Lang file lists 21 items and 1 block, making it a compact, single-idea addition rather than a sprawling one.

Its standout ingredient is the **Elder Guardian**: the mod turns Minecraft's toughest ocean boss into a full cooking chain (raw/cooked slice, slab, roll, cabbage-wrapped, and a full guardian soup), alongside a smaller regular-Guardian tail dish line. It rounds out with squid/tentacle food (tentacle on a stick, cut tentacles, squid rings), pufferfish-derived sushi (Fugu Roll/Slice — playing on real-world fugu, which is famously poisonous), sea pickles (braised), kelp (honey fried), seagrass salad, and a stuffed cod dish. Together with End's Delight, it's one of the "boss-monster-as-food" themed add-ons in the category — Ocean's Delight does for the Elder Guardian what End's Delight does for the ender dragon.

## How you'll use it here
This turns your ocean and boss loot into food — the headline is the **Elder Guardian**, which becomes a full chain of slices, slabs, rolls, cabbage wraps and soup, alongside squid dishes, pufferfish sushi (Fugu), braised sea pickles and kelp. Use it after you've raided an ocean monument; on this server cooking those drops into buffed meals beats eating raw fish. Like [[End's Delight]], it's a boss-as-food theme rather than everyday cooking.

## Anticipated questions
**What's the standout dish?** The Elder Guardian chain — raw/cooked slice, slab, roll, cabbage-wrapped and guardian soup — built around the ocean's toughest boss.

**Do I need Farmer's Delight?** Yes — it hard-depends on `farmersdelight` (the `fdrf` build targets this pack's Refabricated FD port).

**When do I use it?** Generally after an ocean monument raid, since the best ingredients come from the Elder Guardian and regular guardians.
