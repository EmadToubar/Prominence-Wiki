# Expanded Delight

Jar: `expandeddelight-0.3.2.jar` (mod id `expandeddelight`, by ianm1647 — the same author as Farmer's Knives). A Farmer's Delight add-on (hard-depends on `farmersdelight`) that fills in crops and pantry staples the base mod is missing, rather than theming around a dimension or mob like the other add-ons. Lang file lists 44 items and 18 blocks.

New crops: asparagus, sweet potato, chili pepper, and peanut, each carried through several dishes (asparagus soup and its creamy/bacon variants; baked sweet potato and sweet potato salad; peperonata from chili peppers; peanut butter, peanut butter sandwiches — including sweet berry and glow berry jelly combos — peanut salad, and honey peanut soup). It also adds general-purpose kitchen staples that read as infrastructure for other cooking mods: cinnamon (stick and ground), salt (mined from a "Salt Rock"), and cheese (wheel, slices, sandwiches, grilled cheese, mac and cheese), plus jams/jellies (sweet berry, glow berry), sweet rolls, and cookies (chocolate, sugar, snickerdoodle). Two new utility blocks tie it together: the **Mortar and Pestle** and the **Juicer**, used to process the new ingredients into condiments and juices (apple, sweet berry, glow berry).

## How you'll use it here
This is where your pantry staples come from — cheese, salt, cinnamon, jams and peanut butter — plus extra crops (asparagus, sweet potato, chili pepper, peanut) that the wider cooking loop leans on. Use its **Mortar and Pestle** and **Juicer** to process these into condiments and juices for [[Farmer's Delight]] recipes. Mid-game it fills out your menu with things like grilled cheese, peanut butter sandwiches and sweet potato dishes. Think of it as infrastructure for the pack's cooking, not a standalone theme.

## Anticipated questions
**What does this add over base Farmer's Delight?** The crops and pantry staples FD lacks — cheese, salt, cinnamon, jams and peanut butter, plus asparagus, sweet potato, chili pepper and peanut.

**What are the Mortar and Pestle and Juicer for?** Processing the new ingredients into condiments and juices used by the cooking recipes.

**Do I need Farmer's Delight?** Yes — it hard-depends on `farmersdelight`.
