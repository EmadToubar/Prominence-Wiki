# Let's Do: HerbalBrews

Jar: `letsdo-herbalbrews-fabric-1.0.12.jar` (mod id `herbalbrews`). The tea-focused entry in the "Let's Do" series, sharing furniture/sound plumbing with [[[Let's Do] API]] and the series' shared visual style, though — unlike Brewery — its own `fabric.mod.json` doesn't list `doapi` as a hard dependency. Its tagline: "Brew your bliss – discover new flowers, craft unique stations, and master the art of tea-making." Lang file lists 9 items and 36 blocks.

New plants: Lavender, Hibiscus, Wild Coffee, Wild Rooibos, and Wild Yerba Mate (each with potted variants and their own leaf/block forms — e.g. Green/Black/Oolong/Mixed Tea Leaf Blocks). These get processed at a dedicated **Brewing Cauldron**, **Copper Tea Kettle**, and **Stove/Tea Kettle** into a full menu of teas and coffees: Black Tea, Green Tea, Oolong Tea, Lavender Tea, Rooibos Tea, Yerba Mate Tea, Hibiscus Tea, plus Coffee and Milk Coffee, most stored/served via a Flask or Jug and dried tea leaves bundled into "Bag of Tea Leaves." A "Herbal Infusion" item and a "Completionist Banner: Herbal Brews" (shared banner mechanic from doapi) round it out, alongside Top Hat and Witch Hat cosmetics. It's the lightest of the three Let's Do content mods but still adds a genuinely separate drink economy (hot beverages) distinct from Brewery's beer/whiskey and Vinery's wine.

## How you'll use it here
This is the tea-and-coffee branch — grow lavender, hibiscus, wild coffee, rooibos and yerba mate, then brew them at the **Brewing Cauldron** and **Copper Tea Kettle** into a menu of teas and coffees served in flasks and jugs. It's the lightest of the three Let's Do content mods, so treat it as a low-investment cozy side project: make hot drinks once you've found the flowers. It's a distinct drink line from [[Let's Do: Brewery]]'s beer and [[Let's Do: Vinery]]'s wine.

## Anticipated questions
**What do I grow for tea?** Lavender, Hibiscus, Wild Coffee, Wild Rooibos and Wild Yerba Mate, processed at the Brewing Cauldron and Copper Tea Kettle.

**Does it need the Let's Do API?** It shares the API's furniture and sound plumbing, but unlike Brewery its own `fabric.mod.json` doesn't list `doapi` as a hard dependency — the [[[Let's Do] API]] is present in the pack regardless.

**How's it different from Brewery and Vinery?** It's the hot-beverage line (teas and coffees), separate from Brewery's beer/whiskey and Vinery's wine.
