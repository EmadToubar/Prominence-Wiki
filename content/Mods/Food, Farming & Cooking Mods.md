# Food, Farming & Cooking Mods

Cooking gives real stat buffs here, not just hunger refill — worth doing early. See [[Getting Started]].

## Croptopia
Adds a huge range of new crops and foods, giving farming much more depth than vanilla's wheat-and-carrots loop.

## Farmer's Delight
The genre-defining cozy farming-and-cooking expansion — new crops, a proper cooking pot, food worth making.

## Farmer's Delight add-ons (grouped)
Chef's Delight (Cook/Chef villager professions), End's Delight, Expanded Delight, Ocean's Delight (themed crop/dish expansions), Farmer's Knives, Animal Feeding Trough.

## Let's Do series (drinks)
Let's Do: Brewery (brew your own beer), Let's Do: HerbalBrews (tea), Let's Do: Vinery (grapes/wine), [Let's Do] API (shared library).
