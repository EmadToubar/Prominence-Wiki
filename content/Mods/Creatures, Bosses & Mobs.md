# Creatures, Bosses & Mobs

Bosses generally have a **recommended player level** — check the quest before engaging. See [[Progression & Level Caps]].

## Bosses of Mass Destruction
Genuinely high-threat optional bosses for when you've outgrown everything else — built to be fought with real endgame gear, not stumbled into.

## Mutant Monsters
Twisted, oversized versions of familiar mobs — a solid step up in danger from vanilla enemies.

## Mobs of Mythology
A whole cast of mythological creatures for variety in what you're fighting.

## Friends & Foes
The mobs that got voted down (or forgotten) in Mojang's old Mob Votes, expanded into fuller additions.

## Naturalist
New ambient wildlife with real behavior — makes the Overworld feel more alive outside of combat.

## Mythic Mounts — rideable flying/ground creatures
The pack's dedicated tamable-mount mod. Confirmed **flying** mounts: **Dragon** (health 40 base / rarest, subterranean spawn, hostile until tamed with gold/diamond/emerald, biggest stats + 9-slot storage), **Griffon** (health 35, common in mountain biomes, tames on rabbit/mutton, peaceful), **Nether Bat** (health 25, Nether-only, hostile until tamed), **Żar-ptak "Firebird"** (health 20, rare village-adjacent spawn, tames on golden apple — note: its in-game display name is Polish for "Firebird," which is why it looks like it starts with "Z"), **Giant Moth** (health 10, fragile). NOT flyers despite appearances: **Courier Bird** (too heavy to fly, runs instead) and **Colelytra**. Also includes non-flying mounts: Acencia (climbing cat), Archelon (giant sea turtle), Geckotoa/Riding Lizard (ground lizards), Dire Wolf, Nightmare (hostile Nether fire-steed).
- **Summoning Staff** (2 Feathers + Ender Pearl / Stick + Name Tag / Stick + Bell) links to a tamed mount on right-click, then recalls it to you instantly from any distance — the fix for a flying mount that's wandered off. Flying mounts also simply won't take off while leashed.
- All mount base stats (health/speed/attack/taming items/spawn biomes) are configured server-side in `MythicMountsConfig.json` and can be looked up precisely rather than guessed.
