# Core RPG & Combat Mods

The beating heart of the pack — the mods that turn Minecraft into an actual RPG. See [[Combat & Stats]], [[Gear Rarity & Reforging]], [[Talent Tree & Fate Talents]] for how these systems play.

## Zenith Renewed / Zenith Attributes Renewed
A Fabric port of the classic mod Apotheosis. Runs enchanting overhauls, spawner mechanics, tougher bosses, and — most importantly — the weapon/armor "affix" system behind [[Gear Rarity & Reforging|gear rarity]] (Common through Mythic) and its reforge bonuses. Zenith Attributes Renewed is the math underneath making the extra stats behave.

## Pufferfish's Skills / Pufferfish's Attributes
The [[Talent Tree & Fate Talents|Talent Tree]] (press N). One point every two levels; Fate Talents near the top define your build. Attributes is the plumbing that lets the tree grant extra stats at all.

## Better Combat
Swaps vanilla's "click and pray" combat for directional swings and proper weapon movesets — the combat system you're playing with the whole time.

## Combat Roll
Adds a stamina-based dodge roll — your i-frame button, core to surviving harder boss fights.

## Tierify
Gives armor, weapons, and tools quality tiers of their own, layered on top of the broader Zenith rarity system.

## Jewelry (RPG Series)
Rings and necklaces from deep-mined gems, each built to boost a specific [[Archetypes & Builds|archetype]]. See [[Accessories & Enchanting]].

## Artifacts
Powerful, uncraftable trinkets found only while exploring.

## Accessories / Accessories Trinkets Compat Layer / Trinkets and Things
The invisible slot system Jewelry and Artifacts plug into.

## Marium's Soulslike Weaponry
Legendary, boss-tier weapons (and the bosses that drop them) — its own Quest Book chapter.

## Simply Swords
A whole armory of unique, flashy swords with their own identities and quest chapter.

## Transmog
Change gear appearance without touching stats.

## Gear-tier & progression mods
**MC Dungeons Armors / MC Dungeons Weapons** bring Minecraft Dungeons gear into Java for mid-game goals. **Advanced Netherite** and **Mythic Upgrades** add tiers above vanilla Netherite. **More Totems** adds Totem of Undying variants themed after different mobs. **Mythic Mounts** adds rideable mounts — see the mount-hunting workflow under [[Creatures, Bosses & Mobs]]. **Dynamic RPG Resource Bars** gives animated health/stamina/mana bars. **SimplySkills** is an extra combat skill tree. **More RPG Classes** adds extra weapons/spells beyond the main archetype mods.

## Archetype / "RPG Series" class mods
**Archers (RPG Series)** + **Archers Expansion** = the Ranger path. **Wizards (RPG Series)** = the Elemental Mage path. **Paladins & Priests (RPG Series)** = the Holy path. **Rogues & Warriors (RPG Series)** = the melee-Haste/Assassin path. **Death Knights** = a dark-magic-and-melee hybrid class. **Spellblade Next** = magical melee for the hybrid Spellblade build. Full breakdown: [[Archetypes & Builds]].

## Combat tweaks (grouped)
Combat Effects (configurable in-fight effects), ReBalance and Resistance Balancer (damage math retuning), Projectile Damage Attribute and RangedWeaponAPI (separate damage stat for ranged weapons), Shield API and Shield Overhaul (shield mechanics/looks), Gun Scaling (scales Soulslike Weaponry guns with ranged-damage stat), Simply Swords Battle Standard Tweaks and Mutant Skeleton Armor Tweaks (balance patches).

## Cosmetic gear: armor trims (grouped)
AllTheTrims, DynamicTrim, BetterTrims (trims grant passive effects), More Armor Trims, Better Trim Tooltips, BetterThanMending.

## Attribute plumbing (grouped, invisible)
AttributeFix, Custom Entity Attributes, Custom Item Attributes, Eternal Attributes, Extra RPG Attributes — widen the stat "slots" gear/talents/mobs use.
