# More Totems

**More Totems** (`moretotems`, jar `more-totems-mod-2.16.0.jar`, by Hyper_Pigeon) adds seven Totem of Undying variants, each themed after a different mob and granting a unique on-activation effect beyond vanilla's plain Regeneration/Absorption/Fire Resistance. Craftable by surrounding a vanilla Totem of Undying with 8 of a themed ingredient in a 3x3 shaped crafting grid: the **Explosive Totem of Undying** (8 Gunpowder, creeper-themed) presumably detonates a controlled blast on save; the **Stinging Totem of Undying** (bee-themed) applies its "Sniper" or sting-related effect; the **Skeletal Totem of Undying** (8 Bone) grants a skeleton-archer-styled buff; the **Teleporting Totem of Undying** blinks the player to safety; the **Ghastly Totem of Undying** (8 Ghast Tears) ties into the mod's "Cephalopod"/ghast-flight theming; the **Tentacled Totem of Undying** leans on the same squid/ghast-adjacent "Cephalopod" effect; and the **Rotting Totem of Undying** applies a zombie-flavored "Necrosis" effect. The mod also registers two summonable helper entities, a "Totem Bee" and "Totem Zombie," suggesting at least the Stinging and Rotting totems summon a temporary ally mob on activation rather than (or in addition to) applying a status effect.

No dedicated config file exists for More Totems under `config/` on this server (a `find` for "totem" in the config directory only turns up unrelated Iron Chests netherite-chest configs and Better Archaeology's separate "totem" items in the quest book) — the mod ships with no exposed server-side tuning, so all seven variants run on their packaged vanilla-recipe defaults. It doesn't appear in any FTB Quests chapter either; the only "totem" hits in the quest chapters belong to the unrelated Better Archaeology mod's Torrent/Soul/Growth Totems.
