# BetterTrims

By Bawnorton — "Gives each armour trim material a unique effect when worn." Turns [[AllTheTrims]]'s cosmetic trim system into a genuine gearing choice: each of ~19 trim materials grants a small always-on passive effect while worn, stacking per matching trim piece on the armor set. This server's config (`bettertrims.json`) has every material enabled except Amethyst and Glowstone Dust, with the tuned effect values fully readable:

- **Silver** — +5% movement speed, +5% jump height, +20% attack damage, +1.5% attack speed, +25% improved vision.
- **Nether Brick** — Blazes ignore you at 1 piece, Wither Skeletons ignore you at 2, Piglins enrage at 4.
- **Platinum** — Illagers ignore you at 1 piece.
- **Gold** — Piglins ignore you at 1 piece (extending the vanilla gold-nugget behavior to armor trims).
- **Prismarine Shard** — Guardians ignore you at 2 pieces; mining fatigue immunity at 4.
- **Diamond** — 2.5% flat damage reduction. **Netherite** — 25% fire resistance. **Iron** — +5.0 mining speed. **Copper** — +5% swim speed. **Redstone** — +10% movement speed. **Lapis** — +30 enchantability. **Emerald** — 12.5% villager trade discount.
- **Quartz** — +5% bonus XP from kills/mining. **Coal** — furnace speed boost + 5-block hostile-detection radius. **Leather** — +0.4 step height. **Slime Ball** — 25% fall damage reduction + 25% extra knockback + bounce. **Ender Pearl** — 7.5% dodge chance (but water damages the wearer). **Chorus Fruit** — 25% dodge chance. **Fire Charge** — extra fire duration on hit. **Dragon's Breath** — 1.25x lingering-cloud radius. **Echo Shard** — reduced sculk-sensor detection range. **Enchanted Golden Apple** — extended/stronger absorption.

## Anticipated questions

**What does BetterTrims change about armor trims?**
It "gives each armour trim material a unique effect when worn," turning [[AllTheTrims]]'s cosmetic trim system into a genuine gearing choice: each trim material grants a small always-on passive effect while worn, stacking per matching trim piece on the set.

**How many trim materials are there, and are any disabled?**
About 19 trim materials. This server's config (`bettertrims.json`) enables every material except Amethyst and Glowstone Dust.

**Which trim is best for offense?**
Silver stands out — +20% attack damage, +1.5% attack speed, +5% movement speed, +5% jump height, and +25% improved vision.

**Which trims make hostile mobs ignore me?**
Several: Gold makes Piglins ignore you at 1 piece, Platinum makes Illagers ignore you at 1, Nether Brick makes Blazes ignore you at 1 (Wither Skeletons at 2, but Piglins enrage at 4), and Prismarine Shard makes Guardians ignore you at 2 (plus mining-fatigue immunity at 4).

**Are there dodge-chance trims, and is there a catch?**
Yes — Ender Pearl gives 7.5% dodge chance but water damages the wearer, while Chorus Fruit gives 25% dodge chance.
