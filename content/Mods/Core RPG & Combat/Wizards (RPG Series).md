# Wizards (RPG Series)

The Elemental Mage archetype mod. Adds three Tier I Tomes obtained from the Spell Binding Table — **Tome of Arcane**, **Tome of Fire**, **Tome of Frost** — each its own ranged-caster sub-path with matching Wands, Staves (including Netherite and gemstone-tier Crystal/Ruby/Smaragdant staves), and full Wizard/Arcane/Fire/Frost robe sets (hat, top, bottom, boots, up to Netherite). A Wizard Merchant villager profession sells related gear.

Each school plays differently: **Arcane** stacks *Arcane Charge* off Arcane Bolt/Missiles/Blast and dumps it with Arcane Beam or a forward Blink for burst; **Fire** layers Scorch, Fireball, a channeled Fire Breath cone, area Meteors, and a persistent Wall of Flames for sustained damage; **Frost** kites with Frost Shard/Frostbolt (which ricochets between targets), locks enemies down with Frost Nova → *Frozen* (immobile, extra-vulnerable to frost follow-up), and can go defensive with Frost Shield or ultimate with Blizzard. The pack's own quest book explicitly calls Fire "high crit chance," Frost "constant/quick damage with crowd control," and Arcane the biggest single hits.

On this server `wizards/tweaks.json` only tunes `arcane_charge_damage_per_stack` (0.15) beyond the defaults — the class is otherwise stock. Per the `e_spell_books` quest chapter, Wizards' three Tomes are the Tier I feed-in for the Tier III **Astral Invocations** hybrid book (Runic Invocations / Astral Magic), which blends all three schools via Astral Power into the hardest, most build-defining spellbook in the pack (39 possible spells).

## Anticipated questions

**What archetype is this, and how do I start?**
It's the Elemental Mage archetype (see [[Archetypes & Builds]]). You start with one of three Tier I Tomes obtained from the Spell Binding Table: **Tome of Arcane**, **Tome of Fire**, or **Tome of Frost**, each its own ranged-caster sub-path with matching Wands, Staves, and robe sets.

**How do the three schools play differently?**
**Arcane** stacks *Arcane Charge* and dumps it with Arcane Beam or Blink for burst (the biggest single hits). **Fire** layers Scorch, Fireball, Fire Breath, Meteors, and Wall of Flames for sustained damage (high crit chance). **Frost** kites with Frost Shard/Frostbolt, locks enemies down with Frost Nova → *Frozen*, and defends with Frost Shield or Blizzard (constant/quick damage with crowd control).

**Where do I buy Wizard gear?**
A **Wizard Merchant** villager profession sells related gear.

**Is the Wizard class rebalanced on this server?**
Barely. `wizards/tweaks.json` only tunes `arcane_charge_damage_per_stack` (0.15) beyond the defaults — the class is otherwise stock.

**What do the three Tomes lead into?**
They're the Tier I feed-in for the Tier III **Astral Invocations** hybrid book, which blends all three schools via Astral Power into the hardest, most build-defining spellbook in the pack (39 possible spells).
