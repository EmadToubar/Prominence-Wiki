# AllTheTrims

By Bawnorton (also author of [[DynamicTrim]] and [[BetterTrims]]) — "allows any item to be a trim material and makes all armour trimmable." Vanilla only lets a handful of ingots/gems act as trim materials on standard armor; this mod removes both restrictions at once, letting any item in the game (including modded items) act as a trim material, and letting any armor piece (including modded armor) accept a trim.

This is the foundation the other two trim mods in this pack build on: [[BetterTrims]] attaches passive gameplay effects to specific trim materials, and [[DynamicTrim]] handles rendering those trims correctly on non-vanilla armor models. This server's config (`allthetrims.json`) has debug logging off and includes an anti-reupload watermark message baked in by the modpack author, confirming AllTheTrims ships as a Prominence-II-integrated dependency rather than a drop-in vanilla-only mod.

## Anticipated questions

**What two restrictions does AllTheTrims remove?**
Vanilla only lets a handful of ingots/gems act as trim materials, and only on standard armor. AllTheTrims removes both at once: **any item** (including modded items) can act as a trim material, and **any armor piece** (including modded armor) can accept a trim.

**Does AllTheTrims itself add gameplay effects to trims?**
No. It's the foundation the other two trim mods build on — [[BetterTrims]] attaches the passive gameplay effects to specific trim materials, and [[DynamicTrim]] handles rendering trims correctly on non-vanilla armor models.

**Who made it?**
Bawnorton, who also authored [[DynamicTrim]] and [[BetterTrims]].

**Is this a plain vanilla-compatible drop-in?**
Not quite — the server's `allthetrims.json` has debug logging off and carries an anti-reupload watermark baked in by the modpack author, confirming it ships as a Prominence-II-integrated dependency.
