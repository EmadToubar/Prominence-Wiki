# Death Knights

A dark-magic-and-melee hybrid archetype explicitly "inspired by World of Warcraft," sitting outside the pack's main five archetypes ([[Archetypes & Builds]]). Adds three Tier II spellbooks (per the `e_spell_books` quest chapter) built around a shared theme of **Runeblades** and **Runeaxes** (Iron/Diamond/Netherite/Ruby/Aeternium, plus one-handed and Great two-handed variants) and full themed armor progressions — Hood/Tunic of Death's Call as the base set, then Frozen Champion, Plaguebringer, Crimson Guard, Icebound Knight, Blightbringer and Crimson Knight sets. It also adds its own runeblade-specific jewelry (Ring/Necklace of the Eternal Winter, Signet of Death, Ring of Bloodthirst, etc.) and a custom **Decaying** enchantment that boosts Unholy and Blood Spell Power damage.

The three specs are genuinely distinct kits, not palette swaps:
- **Tome of Vampiric Knowledge (Blood, Tank)** — self-sustain via lifesteal. Death Strike and Marrowrend deal hybrid physical+blood damage; Death Strike heals 10% max HP (or grants equivalent absorption at full health); Marrowrend stacks a *Marrowshield* Resistance buff that's consumed by incoming hits; Blood Boil applies *Blood Plague*, a DoT that heals the Death Knight when they damage an infected target.
- **Tome of Unholy Teachings (Unholy, single-target DPS)** — a disease-stacking rotation. Festering Strike applies/stacks a random disease (Festering Wound → Atrocious/Grievous Plague, which reduce enemy damage and healing respectively); Death Coil detonates disease stacks on the target and spreads a stack to nearby uninfected enemies; Epidemic detonates stacks on *all* nearby targets at once; Death Grip is a pull-and-slow.
- **Tome of the Eternal Winter (Frost, multi-target DPS)** — "easy to understand but the hardest of all three to master" per the quest text. Obliterate/Frost Strike deal physical+frost damage and can shatter icicles off frozen targets; Remorseless Winter is a constant frost-damage aura with a freeze chance; Agonizing Breath is a frontal frost cone.

Depends on **Necronomicon** and **Eternal Attributes** (both also in this pack), and requires Java 21+ bytecode support. `attribute.name.death_knights.blood_power` / `unholy_power` are the mod's two custom spell-power attributes that gear can roll.

## Anticipated questions

**Where does the Death Knight fit among the pack's archetypes?**
It's a dark-magic-and-melee hybrid explicitly "inspired by World of Warcraft," sitting **outside the pack's main five archetypes** ([[Archetypes & Builds]]). It adds three Tier II spellbooks built around Runeblades and Runeaxes.

**What are the three specs and how do they play?**
**Tome of Vampiric Knowledge** (Blood, Tank) — self-sustain via lifesteal, with Death Strike healing 10% max HP and Marrowrend stacking a consumable *Marrowshield* Resistance buff. **Tome of Unholy Teachings** (Unholy, single-target DPS) — a disease-stacking rotation using Festering Strike, Death Coil, Epidemic and Death Grip. **Tome of the Eternal Winter** (Frost, multi-target DPS) — Obliterate/Frost Strike, a Remorseless Winter aura, and Agonizing Breath cone; called the hardest of the three to master.

**What gear does it add?**
**Runeblades and Runeaxes** (Iron/Diamond/Netherite/Ruby/Aeternium, in one-handed and Great two-handed variants), full themed armor sets (Death's Call base, then Frozen Champion, Plaguebringer, Crimson Guard, Icebound Knight, Blightbringer, Crimson Knight), runeblade-specific jewelry, and a custom **Decaying** enchantment that boosts Unholy and Blood Spell Power damage.

**What does it depend on?**
It depends on **Necronomicon** and **[[Eternal Attributes]]** (both in this pack) and requires Java 21+ bytecode support.

**Which custom attributes does its gear roll?**
`blood_power` and `unholy_power` — the mod's two custom spell-power attributes, defining its Blood and Unholy trees respectively.
