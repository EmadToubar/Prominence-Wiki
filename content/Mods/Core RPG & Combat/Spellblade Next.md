# Spellblade Next

Real name "Spellblades and Such" — the magical-melee hybrid mod behind the pack's **Spellblade** archetype. Adds elemental Spellblades and Claymores (Arcane, Frost, Fire, each with an upgraded named variant — Crystal Cutlass, Glacial Gladius, Flaming Falchion), matching casting Orbs, and a Runeblaze Rifle for a gun-caster variant. Its core hook, per its own in-game tooltip, is the **Spellblade passive**: "Upon casting a spell with this Spellblade, you gain Strength and Haste equal to a quarter of your power relating to the spell's school, for up to however long the cooldown of the spell is" — i.e. casting temporarily buffs your melee.

It's also where the pack's two Tier II "tank oath" books actually live (despite being grouped with Paladins in the quest book): **Oath of Defiance** (Defiant Stance passively stacks self-heals off incoming damage, active version pounds the shield for AoE armor-based damage; Bolster stacks armor; Shield Shatter throws the shield) and **Oath of Vengeance** (built around a stacking *Fervor* resource — Overpower, Soul of Vengeance, and staff-based spins like Cudgel Strike / Vaulting Slam / Monkey King's Reckoning). Beyond those, it adds several Tier III evolution books referenced in the quest chapter: **Phoenix Rites** (fire, self-burning "Phoenix Curse" DoT engine, Phoenix Dive), **Echoes Unleashed** (arcane, stacking "Unleash" that repeats your spells), and **Deathchill Tome** (frost, "chill faster / heat up slower" passive with a heavy debuff). A **Thesis of Awakening** mechanic lets you dedicate to a single spell for +50% spell power/haste on it, and **Tabula Rasa** + **Spell Oils** let you rebind spells onto arbitrary casting items.

The mod also ships its own light invasion/boss content: a **Hexblade** module that can spawn a **Magus** boss and a **Hexblade Portal**, an optional **Glass Ocean** area with weather changes on phase-end and a horde-spawn toggle (all present but tuned down on this server — `spawnmodifier` 0.6, `magisterXP` 0, `horde` off). Depends on **Extra RPG Attributes** for its "power converted to attack damage" style attributes (Fire/Frost/Arcane Power → Attack Damage and back), visible directly in its lang file.
