# Archers Expansion

Archers Expansion is a Spell Engine add-on that bolts three full archer *subclasses* onto [[Archers (RPG Series)]] — it explicitly depends on both `archers` and `more_rpg_classes`, tying it into the pack's broader [[Archetypes & Builds|archetype]] system rather than just adding loose items. Each subclass is its own armor set + spellbook + four unique spells:

- **Deadeye** (spellbook: *Bounty List*) — a precision/mobility kit: **Trick Shot** (a bouncing arrow that ricochets between targets), **Disabling Shot** (jumps the caster back while slowing/damaging the target), **Fast Shot** (passive Ranged Haste stacking per shot), and **Infiltrator's Arrow** (an invisible shot that also grants the caster invisibility).
- **Tundra Hunter** (spellbook: *Tundric Hunting*) — a cold/control kit: **Arctic Volley** (8-arrow frost spread), **Enchanted Crystal Arrow** (stuns and freezes), **Frozen Pact** (deals damage scaled to a target's accumulated "Frozen ticks," then weakens its attack), **Frozen Shot** (passive stacking frost), and **Winter's Grip** (a totem that freezes nearby enemies solid on death).
- **War Archer** (spellbook: *War Archery Instructions*) — a raw-damage/suppression kit: **Dual Shot** (fires two arrows at once), **Pin Down** (roots the target in place), **Point-Blank Shot** (knockback that scales inversely with range), and **Smoldering Arrow** (passive burn damage).

Each subclass has its own Netherite-upgradeable armor line (Deadeye Tunic/Hood/Leggings/Boots, Tundra Tunic set, Bowman set for War Archer), and each has its own advancement for collecting the full spell set ("Bounty Hunting," "Cold Blooded Hunter," "Load, Hold and Fire!"). It `suggests` (soft-depends on) [[Better Combat]] for its melee-adjacent interactions but doesn't require it. Together with the base Archers mod this is what the category note calls "the Ranger path" — Archers supplies the weapons and the generic marksman kit, Archers Expansion supplies the build-defining subclass choice on top of it.
