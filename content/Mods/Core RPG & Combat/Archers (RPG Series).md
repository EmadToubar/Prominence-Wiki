# Archers (RPG Series)

Archers ("Draw, Release, Conquer — Master the art of Archery!") is the Ranger half of the "RPG Series" archetype line — same author (Daedelus) as [[Better Combat]] and [[Combat Roll]] — and it's built on top of the Spell Engine framework, the same skill-book system the other class mods ([[Wizards (RPG Series)]], [[Paladins & Priests (RPG Series)]], [[Rogues & Warriors (RPG Series)]]) use. Picking up an **Archery Manual** puts you on the Path of Archery; the manual's skill tree grants: **Power Shot** (a passive that stacks a "Hunter's Mark" debuff, +5% damage taken per stack, on marked targets), **Entangling Roots** (a root-and-slow AoE), **Barrage** (rapid multi-arrow fire), and **Magic Arrow** (a piercing magical shot that hits every enemy in its path).

Gear-wise it adds a full bow/crossbow lineup — Composite Longbow, Mechanical Shortbow, Royal Longbow, Rapid/Heavy Crossbow, up through Netherite and Crystal/Ruby tiers — plus Archer and Ranger armor sets (cloth and Netherite-Ranger variants) and a parallel line of thrown **Spears** (Flint through Netherite, plus Aeternium and Ruby tiers). Numbers are server-tuned in `config/archers/items.json`: pull times and damage scale with tier, e.g. the base Composite Longbow is 30-tick pull / 8 damage, the Netherite Heavy Crossbow is 35-tick pull / 15 damage, and melee spears range from the Flint Spear (4 damage, -3 attack speed) up to the Netherite Spear (7 damage). An **Auto-Fire Hook** enchant (anvil-applied) auto-releases a fully charged shot for you.

The mod also seeds new structures into vanilla villages — **Archery Range** buildings (large and small variants) in desert, savanna, plains, taiga, and snowy villages, each staffed by a new **Archery Artisan** villager profession working from an **Archery Artisan Table**. Config tweaks on this server: crossbow power-enchant scaling at `0.08`/level, quick-charge at `0.1`/level, and Infinity is explicitly enabled for crossbows (not a vanilla behavior).
