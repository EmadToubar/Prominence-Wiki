# Extra RPG Attributes

Jar/mod id `extraspellattributes`, by cleannrooster (Forg) — "For use with RPG series mods." Despite the generic-sounding jar name, this is a purpose-built defensive/conversion attribute pack for the archetype mods: it adds power-conversion attributes (Attack Damage ⇄ Fire/Frost/Arcane/Healing Power, used by [[Spellblade Next]]'s hybrid melee-caster builds) alongside a set of standalone defensive stats — **Reabsorption** (regenerates Absorption after 4 seconds without being hit), **Glancing Blow** (chance to take 35% less damage from entity-sourced hits), **Spell Suppression** (chance for incoming spells to deal 50% less damage), **Serenity/Spellbreak** (chance for an already-suppressed spell to deal *zero* damage, capped 75%), **Endurance/Defiance** (flat damage-taken reduction with diminishing returns), and **Determination** (a % of health lost is regenerated back over 4 seconds).

It backs six enchantments that grant these stats directly (Suppressing, Defiance, Warding, Spellbreaking/"Evasion" is Glancing Blow's enchant name, Battle Rouse) and ten trinket items in its own "Extra RPG Attributes Trinkets" item group — rings, amulets, and bracers (Netherite Diamond Ring/Amulet, Gold Quartz Ring/Amulet, Turtle Bracer, Amethyst Bracer, Ring of Undying, Banner of Undying, etc.) that presumably grant these attributes when worn as accessories. [[AttributeFix]]'s config lists several `extraspellattributes:` entries (`endurance`, `spellsuppression`) among its 194 tracked attributes, confirming this mod's stats are part of the pack's wider attribute-safety net.
