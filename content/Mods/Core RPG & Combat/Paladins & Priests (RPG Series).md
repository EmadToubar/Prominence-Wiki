# Paladins & Priests (RPG Series)

The Holy archetype mod, adding two Tier I spellbooks: the **Paladin Libram** (melee tank/support, crafted with the also-added Claymore, Great Hammer and Mace weapon lines up through Netherite/Aeternium/Ruby tiers) and the **Holy Book** (pure caster support). Full matching armor progressions exist for both — Paladin/Crusader plate sets and Priest/Prior robe sets — plus dedicated Holy Wands/Staves and Iron→Aeternium/Ruby Kite Shields. A Monk villager profession (with its own Monk Workbench) sells related gear.

Spells split cleanly by role: the Paladin side gets **Judgement** (AoE strike + stun), **Battle Banner** (party-wide attack speed/knockback resistance buff), and **Divine Protection** (blocks a set number of incoming hits); the Priest side gets **Heal/Flash Heal**, **Holy Shock** (heal ally + smite enemy in one cast), a channeled **Holy Light** beam that heals allies and burns enemies simultaneously, **Circle of Healing** (AoE heal + absorption), and **Barrier** — a summoned dome that blocks projectiles, magic, and intruding enemies. Config (`paladins/tweaks.json`) tunes Battle Banner's numbers directly: +40% attack speed, +40% ranged/spell haste, +50% knockback resistance, plus a 0.3 knockback strength for Barrier.

Per the pack's own quest book (`e_spell_books` chapter), the **Paladin Libram is a Tier II hybrid** — explicitly described as "a combination of a Warrior and a Priest," craftable from Tier I books rather than found directly. The same quest groups it alongside two other Tank-flavored Tier II books, *Oath of Defiance* and *Oath of Vengeance* (both actually belong to [[Spellblade Next]]), as the pack's three tank-oriented hybrid paths.

**What two spellbooks does the mod add?**
The **Paladin Libram** (melee tank/support) and the **Holy Book** (pure caster support), both Tier I, with full matching armor progressions and dedicated Holy Wands/Staves and Kite Shields.

**What Paladin spells are available?**
**Judgement** (AoE strike + stun), **Battle Banner** (party-wide attack speed/knockback resistance buff), and **Divine Protection** (blocks a set number of incoming hits). See [[Combat & Stats]].

**What Priest spells are available?**
**Heal/Flash Heal**, **Holy Shock** (heal ally + smite enemy in one cast), a channeled **Holy Light** beam, **Circle of Healing** (AoE heal + absorption), and **Barrier** (a dome blocking projectiles, magic, and enemies).

**What does the config tune?**
`paladins/tweaks.json` sets Battle Banner to **+40% attack speed, +40% ranged/spell haste, +50% knockback resistance**, plus a 0.3 knockback strength for Barrier.

**Is the Paladin Libram Tier I or Tier II?**
The pack's quest book treats it as a **Tier II hybrid** — "a combination of a Warrior and a Priest," craftable from Tier I books — grouped with *Oath of Defiance* and *Oath of Vengeance* (both from [[Spellblade Next]]) as the three tank-oriented hybrid paths.

**Who sells the gear?**
A **Monk** villager profession with its own **Monk Workbench**. See [[Archetypes & Builds]].
