# Rogues & Warriors (RPG Series)

The melee half of the archetype line, adding two Tier I books: the **Rogue Handbook** (Haste/Assassin utility kit) and **Warrior's Codex** (bruiser/protector, "a great middle ground for slower Physical Damage builds" per the pack's quest text). New weapon families back both — Daggers and Sickles for Rogue, Double Axes and Glaives for Warrior, all running the full material ladder from starter tiers through Netherite, Aeternium and Ruby. Matching armor sets exist too: Rogue/Assassin (up to Netherite) and Warrior/Berserker (up to Netherite). An Arms Dealer villager profession (with its own Arms Station workbench) sells related gear.

Rogue spells are stealth/burst tools: **Slice & Dice** is a stacking self-buff (attacks build stacks, up to a configured max, each adding damage), **Shock Powder** stuns nearby enemies, **Shadowstep** repositions you behind a target, and **Vanish** grants full stealth that breaks on any action or damage taken. Warrior spells are more control/sustain-oriented: **Shattering Throw** (weapon throw that also shreds target armor), **Demoralizing Shout** (AoE damage-reduction debuff plus minor damage), **Charge** (speed + knockback resistance, cleanses movement-impairing effects), and **Whirlwind** (channeled spin-to-win AoE).

`rogues/tweaks.json` shows this server has hand-tuned the numbers: Slice & Dice at 0.1 damage/stack up to 10 stacks, Stealth at -50% movement speed with a 0.1x visibility multiplier, Shattered Armor at -30%, Demoralizing Shout at -30% damage, Charge at +50% speed/+50% knockback resist — and it layers in **ReBalance**-style enchant scaling too: Sharpness (+8%/level), Smite (+12%/level) and Bane of Arthropods (+12%/level) are all explicitly re-enabled and rebalanced here rather than left at vanilla math.
