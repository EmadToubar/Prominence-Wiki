# More Armor Trims

By Masik16u — "Adds new armor trims." Simply adds 12 brand-new trim *patterns* (as opposed to [[AllTheTrims]], which widens what can serve as trim *material*): Storm, Ram, Myth, Greed, Beast, Fever, Wraith, Nihility, Horizon, Origin, Twilight and Parasite, each with its own smithing template item and template registered exactly like vanilla's Coast/Wild/Sentry patterns. Ships its own advancement, "Full Card Collection," for applying every smithing template at least once.

Purely cosmetic pattern content — the material-based passive effects layered on top of trims come from [[BetterTrims]], and the extra rendering support for non-vanilla armor comes from [[DynamicTrim]].

## Anticipated questions

**What does More Armor Trims add?**
12 brand-new trim *patterns* — Storm, Ram, Myth, Greed, Beast, Fever, Wraith, Nihility, Horizon, Origin, Twilight and Parasite — each with its own smithing template item, registered exactly like vanilla's Coast/Wild/Sentry patterns.

**How is it different from AllTheTrims?**
This mod adds new trim *patterns*; [[AllTheTrims]] instead widens what can serve as trim *material*.

**Do these trims have gameplay effects?**
No — it's purely cosmetic pattern content. The material-based passive effects layered on top of trims come from [[BetterTrims]].

**What renders non-vanilla armor trims?**
The extra rendering support for non-vanilla armor comes from [[DynamicTrim]].

**Is there an advancement for it?**
Yes — "Full Card Collection," earned for applying every smithing template at least once.
