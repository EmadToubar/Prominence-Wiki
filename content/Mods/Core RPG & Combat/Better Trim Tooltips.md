# Better Trim Tooltips

By Andrew6rant (also co-author of [[DynamicTrim]]) — "Condenses the tooltips of Trimmed Armor items." A pure client-rendering/UX mod (mixin-based, client environment only) with no config or gameplay content: it exists purely to keep armor tooltips readable once [[BetterTrims]] and [[More Armor Trims]] are stacking passive-effect text and extra pattern names onto every trimmed piece — without it, a fully-trimmed item in this pack's armor system could have a noticeably bloated tooltip.

## Anticipated questions

**What does this mod actually do?**
It "condenses the tooltips of Trimmed Armor items" — a pure client-rendering/UX mod by Andrew6rant (also co-author of [[DynamicTrim]]) that keeps trimmed-armor tooltips readable.

**Does it change any gameplay?**
No. It's mixin-based, client-environment only, with no config or gameplay content — it only affects how tooltips are drawn.

**Why is it needed in this pack?**
Because [[BetterTrims]] and [[More Armor Trims]] stack passive-effect text and extra pattern names onto every trimmed piece. Without this mod, a fully-trimmed item could have a noticeably bloated tooltip.
