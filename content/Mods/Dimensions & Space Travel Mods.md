# Dimensions & Space Travel Mods

## Ad Astra
Full space travel: build rockets, launch off Earth, land on the Moon, Mars, Mercury, Venus, and Glacio (plus their orbits). Needs genuine [[Tech & Automation Mods|tech progression]] to get a rocket off the ground — see the in-game "Planets & Space" quest chapter. Ad Astra: Giselle Addon adds more content on top; Ad Astra Cable Fix prevents pipe-related crashes.

## Better End
A full overhaul of the End dimension — new biomes, mobs, structures — turning the usual flat island + dragon fight into an actual dimension worth exploring.

## Better Nether
The Nether's equivalent overhaul — new biomes and mobs replacing the vanilla nether wastes and basalt deltas. (Correction: earlier notes in this vault mislabeled this as "Better Nether (BWNCR)" — the `bwncr`-prefixed jar is actually an unrelated sound-muting utility, [[Bad Wither No Cookie Reloaded]]. Real Better Nether ships in its own separate jar.)

## Deeper and Darker
An entirely new dimension, the Otherside, reachable beyond the Deep Dark.

## The Bumblezone
A bee-themed dimension with its own advancement-gated entry trigger.

## More dimension content (grouped)
BetterEnd Crashed Ships, Eldritch End (H.P. Lovecraft-inspired End content), End Remastered, Dragonfight Mod (harder Ender Dragon fight), Pirate's Doom (pirate-themed Nether content).
