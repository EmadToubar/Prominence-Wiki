# Building & Decoration Mods

## Chipped / Chipped-Express
Chipped adds a huge library of matching decorative block variants; Chipped-Express lets you autocraft those blocks through AE2 or Create instead of clicking a crafting table 200 times. See [[Tech & Automation Mods]].

## Supplementaries
A big grab-bag of functional, good-looking "vanilla+" blocks — signs, lanterns, benches, dozens of small useful pieces.

## Handcrafted
A full furniture mod — tables, chairs, cabinets — for making builds feel lived-in.

## Twigs / Stoneworks
Twigs adds new building materials and fresh vanilla-block takes; Stoneworks does the same specifically for stone types.

## Decorative Blocks / Building Wands / Build Guide
Decorative Blocks (new decorative sets), Building Wands (place a whole shape at once), Build Guide (client-side shape projection — circles, spheres — works even without the mod on the server).

## Convenient Decor / Macaw's Holidays / Promenade
Convenient Decor (more decoration pieces), Macaw's Holidays (250+ Christmas + 80+ Halloween decorations), Promenade (pathway/planter village decor).

## Thaumon
Thaumcraft-inspired decoration blocks, purely aesthetic — no actual Thaumcraft magic attached.

## Small decoration & building utilities (grouped)
Dark Paintings, DarkUtilities, Grass Overhaul, Oxidized (more copper uses), Connectible Chains, Double Doors, Derelict, Windchimes, Amendments, Satisfying Buttons, KleeSlabs, Fast Paintings, Reacharound, Quartz Elevator, Open Block Elevator Mod, Magnum Torch (wide-area mob-spawn prevention), Immersive Lanterns, Connectivity Mod, Kibe, Block Swap.
