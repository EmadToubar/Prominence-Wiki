# Magic & Spells Mods

See [[Damage Types & Runes]] and the Mage path in [[Archetypes & Builds]] for how these systems play.

## Botania
A gorgeous, flower-and-mana-powered magic-tech mod. Less "cast fireballs" and more "build an increasingly elaborate garden that automates your whole base" — one of the pack's best long-term magic/tech hybrid goals, with its own Quest Book chapter. Can bridge into [[Tech & Automation Mods|Applied Energistics 2]] via Applied Botanics.

## Spell Engine
The data-driven library that actually runs spellcasting — spell books, staffs, and scrolls all bind to it. Running under the hood of every spell any caster build uses.

## Spell Power Attribute
The stat plumbing behind Spell Engine — why "spell power" and "spell haste" exist as gear stats at all.

## Runes
Craft Runes to use as spell ammunition. These are the items that map to the pack's three [[Damage Types & Runes|damage families]] — Elemental (Arcane/Fire/Frost), Primordial (Healing/Soul), Decaying (Unholy/Blood).

## Sortilege
Simple magic staffs plus tweaks to vanilla enchanting/brewing — a gentler, earlier-game entry point into magic before the archetype mods.

## Archon
Sorcery, necromancy, and its own bosses — a denser, darker magic mod for players who've outgrown the basics.

## Invocations Mod
A spellbook containing 24 powerful invocable spells in one package — a broad magical toolkit without committing to a full caster build.

## Bewitchment / Bewitchment Tweaks
A dedicated witchcraft mod (its own Quest Book chapter) with rituals and familiars. Bewitchment Tweaks is a small balance patch (adds invisibility to the Specter Bangle).
