# Storage & Inventory Mods

For real auto-sorted digital storage, the pack's actual answer is **Applied Energistics 2** — see [[Tech & Automation Mods]]. There is no dedicated "auto-categorize into separate physical chests" mod installed (no Sophisticated Storage equivalent); AE2's Storage Bus + item filters is the closest thing, or a classic vanilla hopper/comparator sorter.

## Iron Chests: Restocked
A tiered line of chests bigger than vanilla's double chest.

## Traveler's Backpack
Wearable, upgradeable backpacks with real customization. Higher tiers unlock more main storage slots plus a dedicated multi-slot **weapon/tool rack** for quick-swapping gear, and accept **Upgrades** (e.g. Crafting Upgrade, Void Upgrade with item filters) installed into their own upgrade slots. Genuinely one of the best early storage upgrades available.

## Nether Chested
A chest built from Nether materials holding 8x the usual capacity.

## Lootr
Makes chest loot personal — every player who opens a loot chest gets their own copy of the contents, so nobody misses out because a friend looted it first.

## Carry On
Pick up and carry single-block tile entities (chests, furnaces, etc.) in your empty hands instead of breaking and replacing them.

## Inventory Sorter / Visual Workbench
Inventory Sorter adds one-click sorting **within a single inventory** (not across multiple chests). Visual Workbench keeps items visible inside crafting tables as you work.
