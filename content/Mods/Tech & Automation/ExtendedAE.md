# ExtendedAE

ExtendedAE (v1.0.2) is a substantial quality-of-life extension for [[Applied Energistics 2]], shipping higher-capacity and more convenient versions of AE2's core network devices: an Extended Pattern Provider, Extended Interface, Extended Drive, and Extended IO Port that push past AE2's stock throughput limits, plus an Extended Molecular Assembler, Extended Inscriber, and Extended Charger for beefier crafting and item-charging setups. It also adds a Wireless Connector for linking ME subnetworks without physical cable runs, an Ingredient Buffer for staging crafting inputs, and a Crystal Fixer for automating AE2's crystal-growth process.

With 147 lang keys, it's one of the larger AE2 addons in the pack — closer to "AE2, but every device has a bigger sibling" than a narrow single-feature plugin.
