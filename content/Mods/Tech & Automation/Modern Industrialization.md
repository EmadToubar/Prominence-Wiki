# Modern Industrialization

Modern Industrialization (v1.8.6) is a second full industrial tech tree, distinguishing itself from [[Tech Reborn]] and [[Industrial Revolution]] by leaning heavily into multiblock machines and chemistry-flavored processing chains — Distillation Towers (server-capped at 9 blocks tall via `maxDistillationTowerHeight`), Electric Blast Furnaces, and cable/hatch-based multiblock assembly instead of single-block machines. It runs its own EU-style energy tier system with its own cable network, separate from Forge Energy, that other mods bridge into rather than the other way around.

This server explicitly enables Modern Industrialization's cross-mod hooks: `enableAe2Integration = true` wires it into [[Applied Energistics 2]]'s storage/crafting system, and `enableFtbQuestsIntegration = true` powers its dedicated Quest Book chapter (`modern_industrialization.snbt`, 307 quests, icon `modern_industrialization:guidebook`), positioned last at slot 7 of 7 in the book's order — the pack treats it as the most involved, latest-game tech tree of the bunch. Its questline opens with a Bronze Age framing ("Engineering School?!", "Are we Blacksmiths?", "Bronze Ingots", "Your First Bronze!") before escalating into "Mechanical Press, but more Modern!" — a nod to Create's own Mechanical Press, since MI expects players to already have overlapping tech trees running in parallel.
