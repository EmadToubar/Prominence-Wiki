# Polymorphic Energistics

Polymorphic Energistics (v0.1.1) is a thin compatibility patch: it adds Polymorph support to [[Applied Energistics 2]]'s crafting and pattern system. Polymorph (see [[Quality-of-Life & UI Mods]]) normally lets recipe-viewer mods substitute interchangeable ingredients into a recipe on the fly; without a dedicated bridge, AE2's own autocrafting patterns don't recognize that flexibility. This mod wires the two together so AE2 patterns can take advantage of Polymorph's ingredient substitution instead of requiring one rigid pattern per exact-item variant.

It ships no lang file, no config, and no standalone content of its own — it's pure glue between two systems that are each documented in their own right elsewhere in this vault.
