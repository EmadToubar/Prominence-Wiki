# Industrial Revolution

Industrial Revolution — tagline "Every other industrial mod ever" — is a third full industrial tech tree in the pack, built around progressive machine tiers (Electric Furnace and Pulverizer each go Mk1 through Mk4, then a top-tier "Factory" variant) and dedicated Mining Rigs that automate resource gathering. The Mk-tier scaling is steep and server-verified: an Mk1 Electric Furnace costs 4 FE per operation and holds 1,000 FE, while the Factory tier costs 64 FE per operation but accepts up to 16,384 FE/t input and holds 100,000 FE — each tier roughly doubling processing speed alongside power draw. Its generator lineup (coal, two solar tiers, heat, gas, biomass, and a steam turbine) tops out at 4,096 FE/t for the Heat Generator and Steam Turbine, giving it genuine late-game power output to match its machine tiers. This server has all of Industrial Revolution's custom ores enabled in world generation — tin, nikolite, lead, silver, tungsten, plus sulfuric acid lakes and sulfur crystal deposits.

Industrial Revolution has its own Quest Book chapter (`industrial_revolution.snbt`, 260 quests, icon `indrev:mining_rig_mk4`) at position 6 of 7, right before [[Modern Industrialization]] — placing it as a later-game tech tree alongside Create and MI rather than an early option like [[Tech Reborn]] or [[Powah!]]. Its own guidebook framing opens with "Welcome to Industrial Revolution!" and walks through Mining Rig drill heads and machine color-coded configuration modules.
