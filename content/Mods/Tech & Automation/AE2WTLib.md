# AE2WTLib

AE2WTLib (v15.2.1) is a shared library mod that adds Wireless Terminal support to [[Applied Energistics 2]] with infinite-range capability, and functions partly as plumbing that other AE2 addons in this pack build on rather than a standalone feature mod. Its headline item is the Quantum Bridge Card, which lets a Wireless Terminal attune to a Quantum Bridge for unlimited range instead of AE2's normal falloff-based wireless distance; a Magnet Card adds an auto-pickup upgrade to the Wireless Crafting Terminal with three modes (off, pickup-to-inventory, pickup-to-ME), toggleable via hotkey. A terminal-cycling hotkey lets players flip between multiple carried wireless terminals without opening an inventory menu.

## Anticipated questions

**Q: What does AE2WTLib do?**
A: It adds Wireless Terminal support to [[Applied Energistics 2]] with infinite-range capability, and serves as plumbing other AE2 addons in the pack build on. See [[Storage & Applied Energistics — Getting Started]].

**Q: How do I get unlimited wireless range?**
A: The Quantum Bridge Card lets a Wireless Terminal attune to a Quantum Bridge for unlimited range instead of AE2's normal distance falloff.

**Q: Can I auto-pick-up items into my network wirelessly?**
A: Yes — the Magnet Card adds an auto-pickup upgrade to the Wireless Crafting Terminal with three modes (off, pickup-to-inventory, pickup-to-ME), toggled via hotkey.

**Q: Does it tie into AE2?**
A: Entirely — it's an AE2 addon; it also adds a hotkey to cycle between multiple carried wireless terminals without opening a menu.
