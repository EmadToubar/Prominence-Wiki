# Industrial Revolution EMI Plugin

A small, purely functional addon (v1.0.1) that teaches EMI — the pack's recipe viewer — how to display [[Industrial Revolution]]'s machine and Mining Rig recipes correctly. Without it, IndRev's custom multi-input/energy-cost recipes either wouldn't show up in EMI at all or would render with missing items. Every config option is a straightforward on/off toggle, all left enabled on this server: `addMachineRecipes`, `addMiningRigRecipes`, `addChargedVersions` (so charged item variants show correctly), `hideNotImplementedItems`, and `addMissingItems` to patch in anything EMI would otherwise render blank.

There isn't gameplay content here — it's pure recipe-viewer glue, doing for Industrial Revolution what similar EMI plugins do for other tech mods in the pack.
