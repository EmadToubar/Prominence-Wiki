# Just Hammers

Just Hammers (v20.1.5) adds a parallel area-of-effect mining tool line to [[OnlyExcavators]]: Hammers in Iron, Gold, and Diamond tiers, each available as a plain Hammer, an Impact Hammer, a Reinforced Hammer, and a combined Reinforced Impact Hammer, plus a top-tier Destructor Hammer variant. Two socketable upgrade items — the Impact Core and Destruction Core — appear to modify a hammer's behavior when applied, though the exact mechanical effect of each isn't documented in the mod's own strings.

Server config leaves both of its settings at default: `allowBreaking = false` (hammers cannot break otherwise-unbreakable blocks) and `disableFancyDurability = false` (the mod's custom durability display stays enabled). There's little else to it beyond the tool set itself.
