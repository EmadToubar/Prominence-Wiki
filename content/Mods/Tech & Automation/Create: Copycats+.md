# Create: Copycats+

Create: Copycats+ (v3.0.7) is a disguise-block mod purpose-built for [[Create]]'s kinetic contraptions: it adds "Copycat" versions of ordinary building shapes — blocks, boards, beams, corner slices, half-layers, half-panels, fences, fence gates, doors (including folding and sliding variants), pressure plates, and panes — that visually take on the texture of any block right-clicked onto them while keeping their own shape and behavior. Unusually for a facade mod, Copycats+ goes past pure decoration into functional-block disguises too: Copycat Cogwheel, Copycat Fluid Pipe, and Copycat Glass Fluid Pipe let players skin *working* Create machinery — a spinning cogwheel or a fluid pipe run — to match a build's material palette instead of standing out as bare brass or copper.

With 67 distinct block variants in its lang file, it's the pack's dedicated answer to "I want this functional Create part to not look like Create."
