# Estrogen

Estrogen (v5.0.8) is a standalone whimsical/joke-flavored mod that layers a full item and fluid processing chain onto a "Girl Power" theme, and serves as the base mod that [[Create: Estrogen]] hooks into. Consumables — Estrogen Pills, Crystal Estrogen Pills, Estrogen Chip Cookies, and refillable Estrogen Patches (worn in the accessory "Thighs" slot) — grant a temporary Girl Power Effect buff, each with its own server-tuned duration (this pack sets pills, patches, and cookies to a uniform 6,000 ticks, i.e. 5 minutes). The fluid side is a small processing chain in its own right: Horse Urine is cauldron-processed into Filtrated Horse Urine, which combines with other inputs toward Liquid Estrogen and Gender Fluid — the same fluids [[Create: Estrogen]]'s Centrifuge is built to process.

The mod rounds out with a moth-themed decorative furniture set (Rosy Maple Wool, Bed, and Carpet, plus quilted variants), novelty blocks (Cookie Jar, Dreamcatcher, Dream Block, Bottle of Dreams, a "Block of :3"), and a small permanent-Dash minigame system that's disabled on this server (`minigameEnabled = false`, `permaDashAmount = 0`) — the dash ability itself isn't part of the enabled feature set here.
