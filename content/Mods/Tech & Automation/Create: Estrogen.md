# Create: Estrogen

Create: Estrogen (v1.0.1) is a small integration module that connects the standalone [[Estrogen]] mod to [[Create]]'s kinetic system. Its centerpiece is the Centrifuge, a machine block with no internal inventory of its own — it has to be fed and drained by placing fluid containers directly around it, taking input from the bottom face and delivering output from the top. The Centrifuge also has a hard kinetic requirement: it only functions at Create's maximum rotation speed of 256 RPM, meaning it needs the same top-tier gearing (large cogwheel ratios, a windmill, or a well-geared water wheel setup) that any other max-speed Create machine does.

Centrifuging recipes process Estrogen's fluid items — turning things like Estrogen Pill Packages, Used Filters, and Incomplete Estrogen Patches/"Incomplete UwU" items into their finished forms. The mod also adds one decorative block, the Rosy Maple Seat, matching Estrogen's moth-themed decoration set.
