# Alloy Forgery

Alloy Forgery (v2.1.2) adds a configurable, data-driven alloy-smelting multiblock that predates needing a full tech tree: rather than a single dedicated machine block, players build a Forge out of common building materials (bricks, stone bricks, deepslate bricks, end stone bricks, polished blackstone, or prismarine bricks) around a matching Forge Controller. Each material choice acts as a distinct Forge "tier" — recipes can require a minimum controller tier, and the in-game tooltip warns players outright with "This recipe requires a Forge Controller of Tier %d" if they've under-built. Fuel capacity scales with the structure, and an "Invalid Multiblock Configuration" message flags builds that don't match the required shape.

Because it's purely data-driven, Alloy Forgery is mostly a vehicle other mods and modpacks use to add early-access alloy recipes (bronze, electrum, and similar) that don't require unlocking an entire industrial mod first — a low-investment way to get basic alloys before committing to [[Tech Reborn]], [[Modern Industrialization]], or [[Industrial Revolution]].
