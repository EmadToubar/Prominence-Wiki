# Hellion's Sniffer+

Hellion's Sniffer+ (v0.3.0, by HellionGames) undersells itself with the tagline "basic improvements to the Sniffer from the 1.20 update" — in practice it adds a whole new underground biome built around the Sniffer's ancient-plant theme. Its **Timeless Grotto** biome (a cave-type biome tagged into the overworld's cave generation) grows a dedicated **Stone Pine** wood set (log, planks, stairs, slabs, doors, fences, signs, boats, and a chest boat), plus **Ivy** and **Fiddlefern**/**Tall Fiddlefern** vegetation and moss patches, giving Sniffer-adjacent exploration its own distinct underground look rather than reusing existing stone/wood palettes.

The mod ships no configuration file in this pack, so the Timeless Grotto generates under its default worldgen rules. It's a content addition rather than a creature mod in the traditional sense — the Sniffer itself is vanilla 1.20, so this mod's job is purely to give the biome it's thematically tied to some genuinely new blocks and flora to discover.
