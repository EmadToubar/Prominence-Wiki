# Mythic Mounts

Mythic Mounts (v7.2) is the pack's dedicated tamable-mount mod, and it's entirely config-driven — every creature's health, speed, attack, diet, taming items, and spawn conditions live in `MythicMountsConfig.json`, which this server has read directly to confirm the numbers below. Its roster splits cleanly into flyers, ground mounts, and two look-alikes that turn out not to fly at all.

**Flying mounts.** The **Dragon** is the rarest and most dangerous: 40 base health, hostile until tamed (only a 5% taming chance per attempt, using gold ingot, diamond, or emerald), a 9-slot inventory, and `specialAbilitiesEnabled` set true. It only spawns subterraneously, capped at just **1 per 256-chunk region** with a mere **10% roll chance** even then — making it the single hardest mount in the mod to find. The **Griffon** is the common alternative: 35 health, tamed peacefully with rabbit or mutton (no hostility flag), spawning at a generous 70% roll chance across mountainous biomes (windswept hills, windswept gravelly hills, windswept forest, snowy slopes, and jagged peaks). The **Nether Bat** (25 health) is Nether-exclusive, spawning only in Crimson and Warped Forests, hostile until tamed on porkchop. The **Żar-ptak** — Polish for "Firebird," and its actual in-game display name, which is why it visually starts with "Ż" rather than "F" — is a rare village-adjacent spawn (20 health, only a 2% roll chance, capped at 5 per 256-chunk region) tamed exclusively with golden apples. The **Giant Moth** is the most fragile flyer at just 10 health, spawning only in Dark Forests and tamed with any flower item.

**Look-alikes that don't fly.** The **Courier Bird** and **Colelytra** both read as flying candidates but aren't: the Courier Bird's own in-game lore states these tropical, parrot-like birds have "grown too hefty to fly" and instead sprint on powerful legs, making them popular messenger mounts; the Colelytra — a colorful beetle native to Mushroom Islands that lives peaceably alongside mooshrooms — has no flight mechanic mentioned anywhere in its lore or config.

**Ground mounts.** Rounding out the roster: **Acencia** (a leopard-like climbing cat of snowy forests, tamed on mutton/salmon), **Archelon** (a giant sea turtle of deep lukewarm oceans, tamed on cod/salmon, capped at 2 per 256-chunk region), **Geckotoa**/Giant Geckotoa (a subterranean riding lizard, capped at 1 per region), **Riding Lizard** (a smaller desert-dwelling lizard, hostile until tamed), **Dire Wolf** (old-growth forest predator, tamed on bone), and **Nightmare** (a hostile fire-steed of basalt deltas and soul sand valleys, only a 3% taming chance using bone, coal, or charcoal — notably, this pack's Nightmare comes from Mythic Mounts, not from [[AdventureZ]]'s same-named mob, which this pack has deliberately disabled to avoid the overlap).

**The Summoning Staff** is the mod's key utility item: crafted from 2 Feathers + an Ender Pearl on the top row, a Stick + Name Tag on the middle row, and a Stick + Bell on the bottom row. Right-click a tamed mount once to link the staff to it, then right-click again from anywhere to instantly warp that mount to the rider's side — though per its own in-game description, the staff "will fail if the linked mount is too far away," so it has a hard range limit rather than unlimited reach. One more universal quirk applies across the whole mod: flying mounts refuse to take off while leashed with a lead.

## Anticipated questions

**Q: What's the rarest, hardest mount to get?**
A: The **Dragon** — hostile until tamed with only a 5% taming chance (gold ingot, diamond, or emerald), spawning only subterraneously at 1 per 256-chunk region with a 10% roll chance even then.

**Q: What's a good beginner flying mount?**
A: The **Griffon** — 35 health, tamed peacefully with rabbit or mutton, spawning at a generous 70% roll chance across mountainous biomes (windswept hills/gravelly hills/forest, snowy slopes, jagged peaks).

**Q: How does the Summoning Staff work?**
A: Right-click a tamed mount once to link the staff to it, then right-click again to warp it to your side — but it "will fail if the linked mount is too far away," so it has a hard range limit. Recipe: 2 Feathers + an Ender Pearl (top row), a Stick + Name Tag (middle), a Stick + Bell (bottom).

**Q: Why won't my flying mount take off?**
A: Flying mounts refuse to take off while leashed with a lead — remove the lead first.
