# Caracal mob

Caracal mob (v1.20-2.3.3, by AqUpd, Maity and u/SociopathyKathy) is a small, focused addition: a single new tameable-cat-family creature, the Caracal, modeled after the real African/Asian wild cat. Per its own biome tag data (`caracal_spawning_biomes.json`), it's configured to spawn naturally in exactly one biome type — **Savanna** — making it a rare, regionally-flavored find rather than a common companion animal like vanilla cats or wolves.

The mod ships no configuration file in this pack, so it runs on its stock behavior and spawn rules. Its lang file confirms a small but complete behavior set (hiss, scream, purr, death, eat, and beg-for-food sound cues), suggesting cat-like AI — likely tameable and capable of begging for food from the player, consistent with vanilla ocelot/cat behavior patterns.
