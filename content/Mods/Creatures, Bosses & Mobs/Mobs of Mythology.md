# Mobs of Mythology

Mobs of Mythology (v2.1.2, by Pixel Dream Studios, steficy, AzureDoom, karelien and Bandit-Bytes) adds six creatures drawn from folklore rather than a single mythology: the **Automaton** (a hostile construct, 100 HP / 16 attack, always-hostile toggleable), the **Chupacabra** (16 HP / 6 attack, common spawn weight 20), the **Kobold** and armed **Kobold Warrior** (20 HP scavengers that can optionally steal items, weights 4 and 6, the warrior adding 6 armor and 5.5 attack), the **Drake** (30 HP / 5 attack, spawn weight 10, a small draconic mob rather than a boss), and the **Sporeling** — a mushroom-person with two color variants (red and brown) that each carry a huge bank of joke one-liners triggered on interaction (puns about fungi, "spore-tacular" wordplay, and a couple of stoner jokes about being "blazed").

This server's `mobs_of_mythology.properties` keeps all six enabled at their default health/damage/spawn-weight values, with `shouldKoboldsSteal` left off (kobolds don't pickpocket the player) and the Sporeling as the most common spawn of the group (weight 16, well above the Drake's 10 or Chupacabra's 20-vs-population). None of the six are wired into the pack's boss-gating system, so they read as roaming mid-tier wildlife/threats rather than structured encounters — a lighter-weight complement to the pack's more heavily systemized bosses like Bosses of Mass Destruction or AdventureZ.

## Anticipated questions

**Q: Are any Mobs of Mythology creatures bosses?**
A: No — none of the six are wired into the pack's boss-gating system, so they read as roaming mid-tier wildlife/threats rather than structured encounters. See [[Progression & Level Caps]].

**Q: Which of these mobs is the most dangerous?**
A: The **Automaton** — a hostile construct with 100 HP and 16 attack, the toughest of the six; most others (Chupacabra, Kobold, Drake) sit around 16–30 HP.

**Q: Do Kobolds steal my stuff?**
A: Not on this server — `shouldKoboldsSteal` is left off, so kobolds don't pickpocket the player even though the mod supports it.

**Q: What's the deal with the Sporeling?**
A: It's a mushroom-person with red and brown variants that fire off a huge bank of joke one-liners on interaction; it's also the group's most common spawn (weight 16).
