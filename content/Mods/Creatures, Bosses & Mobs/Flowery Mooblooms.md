# Flowery Mooblooms

Flowery Mooblooms (v2.0.2, by Faboslav) is a small, official addon for [[Friends & Foes]] — it doesn't register its own mod namespace at all, instead dropping its textures and variant data straight into `assets/friendsandfoes/` and `data/friendsandfoes/moobloom_variants/`. It gives the Moobloom (Friends & Foes' flower-topped cow) one extra coat variant per overworld flower: Allium, Azure Bluet, Blue Orchid, Cornflower, Dandelion, Lilac, Lily of the Valley, Orange Tulip, Oxeye Daisy, Peony, Pink Tulip, Poppy, Red Tulip, Rose Bush, Sunflower, and White Tulip — sixteen recolors in total, each a straightforward reskin rather than a new mechanic.

Because it's a texture/variant-only addon with no config file of its own, its only tunable setting is Friends & Foes' own `moobloomSpawnWeight` (4, in this pack's `friendsandfoes.json`) and group size (2–4) — Flowery Mooblooms just widens the pool of what a spawned Moobloom can look like.
