# More Axolotl Variants Mod

More Axolotl Variants Mod (MAVM, v1.2.6, by AkashiiKun) expands vanilla's five axolotl colors with ten additional variants: **Lucia**, **Wilder**, **Golden**, **Green**, **Skulk**, **Black**, **Red**, **Glowxolotl**, **White**, and **Cyanide**. It's built on a small companion library, **More Axolotl Variants API**, which this pack also has installed as a hard dependency. A couple of the names hint at biome/theme tie-ins beyond simple recoloring — "Skulk" suggests a Deep Dark-flavored variant and "Glowxolotl" suggests a glow-ink-sac-style luminous effect — consistent with the mod's general approach of using vanilla's existing axolotl-variant system to slot in extra colors alongside the original Leucistic/Wild/Gold/Cyan/Blue set.

The pack also carries a **disabled** `axolotl-item-fix-1.1.7.jar.disabled`, but that's a different, unrelated mod ("Axolotl Item Fix") — not part of this mod and not currently active.
