# Friends & Foes

Friends & Foes (v3.0.9, by Faboslav) is the pack's implementation of the mobs Mojang put to a community vote and then never added — the **Glare**, **Copper Golem**, **Rascal**, and **Moobloom** from various Minecraft Live mob votes, plus a set of original "vanilla-like" additions: the **Crab**, **Iceologer**, **Illusioner**-adjacent **Player Illusion**, **Mauler**, **Tuff Golem**, and **Wildfire**. Rather than porting the mob-vote losers as-is, Faboslav expanded each with fuller mechanics and structures: Copper Golems can generate as a standalone structure or inside Ancient Cities and oxidize lightning rods over time; Crabs spawn in small groups (2–4) and can be ridden on shoulders like parrots; Glares are small light-seeking mobs that can grief certain blocks if enabled; Iceologers and Illusioners get their own cabin/shack/training-ground structures and can appear in raids; Maulers are aggressive desert/badlands/savanna predators (spawn weights 8/16/32 respectively — most common in savanna); Tuff Golems can spawn inside strongholds; and Wildfires are guardians of a dedicated "Citadel" structure.

This server's `friendsandfoes.json` has every one of these features enabled, including all the bonus generated structures (Copper Golem area + Ancient City variant, Iceologer cabin, Illusioner shack and training grounds, Citadel, and — via the Beekeeper Hut addon config living in this same file — a beekeeper villager profession and beekeeper area structure). Two dedicated addon jars extend Friends & Foes further in this pack: **Flowery Mooblooms** (one Moobloom recolor per flower type) and **Beekeeper Hut** (the beekeeper structure/profession itself), both by the same author and both reading their settings from this shared config file rather than one of their own.

## Anticipated questions

**Q: What mobs does Friends & Foes add?**
A: The mob-vote mobs Mojang never shipped — Glare, Copper Golem, Rascal, and Moobloom — plus originals like the Crab, Iceologer, Mauler, Tuff Golem, and Wildfire.

**Q: Can I ride a Crab?**
A: Yes — Crabs spawn in small groups of 2–4 and can be ridden on your shoulders like parrots.

**Q: Where do Maulers spawn?**
A: In desert, badlands, and savanna, with spawn weights of 8/16/32 respectively — most common in savanna, where they're aggressive predators. See [[Progression & Level Caps]].

**Q: Where do I find the Copper Golem or the Wildfire?**
A: Copper Golems generate as a standalone structure or inside Ancient Cities (and oxidize lightning rods over time); Wildfires guard a dedicated "Citadel" structure.
