# Twigs

Twigs (by Ninni) adds 269 lang entries' worth of new base building materials rather than furniture — the kind of small natural-feeling blocks vanilla is missing. Highlights from its lang file: **twig** (a stick-like decoration item), **bamboo thatch** (block/stairs/slab), **pebbles**, four seashell colors (bronzed, opaline, roseate, tangerine — some of which "play" a sound when used, per the lang key `bronzed_seashell.plays`), **petrified lichen**, **paper lanterns** (multiple color/style variants), standalone **lamp** blocks, and potted **azalea flowers**. It's a materials-first mod: the new items read as texture/detail pieces meant to be scattered into builds (path edges, shorelines, forest floors) rather than a full furniture catalog.

No dedicated config file ships for Twigs on this server, so it runs with default settings. It complements [[Stoneworks]] (stone variety) and [[Decorative Blocks]] (structural trim pieces) by covering the smaller, more naturalistic end of the building-material spectrum.
