# Promenade

Promenade (Hugman) bills itself as "Fancy and simplistic animals, biomes, structures and more!" and, unusually for this category, actually spans decoration blocks, new stone types, world generation, and mob spawns together — 211 lang entries. Decoration-side it adds **leaf piles** and **flower piles** for every vanilla leaf/flower type (oak, spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak, mangrove, azalea leaves; dandelion, poppy, blue orchid, allium, tulips, oxeye daisy, cornflower, lily of the valley, wither rose piles) — good ground-clutter detail for naturalistic builds — plus a new stone type, **Blunite**, with the standard cut-stone family (stairs, slab, wall, column, polished variants).

It also touches world content: `config/promenade/` has separate `animals.json`, `monsters.json`, `biomes.json`, and `world_features.json` files. On this server, `animals.json` sets spawn weights for **capybaras** (10) and **ducks** (10), and `monsters.json` sets weights for **lush creepers** (15) and **sunken skeletons** (20) — these read as Promenade-added mob variants tied to specific biome themes, spawning alongside vanilla mobs rather than replacing them.

**Does it add mobs?** Yes — it touches world content via `config/promenade/`. **Is it client- or server-side?** Its worldgen and spawn behavior are server-side, driven by the JSON files in `config/promenade/` (`animals.json`, `monsters.json`, `biomes.json`, `world_features.json`).
