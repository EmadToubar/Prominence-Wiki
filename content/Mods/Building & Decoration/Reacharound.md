# Reacharound

Reacharound (by spAnser) lets you place a block against the far side of another block you're looking through — practically, it means you can place blocks (stairs, slabs, etc.) facing correctly without having to walk around to the other side first. The pack config (`reacharound.json`) leaves it `enabled: true` with `mode: 0` and shows an in-world placement indicator (a `{ }`/`|  |`-style bracket, animated over 5 ticks) so you can see where the reacharound placement will land before committing. Purely a build-convenience QoL mod.

## Anticipated questions
**What does it do?** It lets you place a block against the far side of another block you're looking through — so you can place stairs, slabs, and the like facing correctly without walking around to the other side first.

**Does it add any blocks or items?** No — it's purely a build-convenience QoL mod with no content of its own.

**How do I know where the block will land?** It shows an in-world placement indicator (a bracket-style marker, animated over 5 ticks) so you can see where the reacharound placement will go before committing. The pack leaves it `enabled: true` with `mode: 0` in `reacharound.json`.
