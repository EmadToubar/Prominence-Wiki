# Convenient Decor

Convenient Decor (Mim1q) is smaller than its name suggests as a pure decoration mod (114 lang entries) — a chunk of its content is actually functional wearables and tools rather than static blocks. Its `config/convenientdecor.json5` file, gated behind a warning that changes **must match on both client and server** and require a full restart, exposes feature toggles: a **watering can** can permanently hydrate farmland with a right-click (on), an **umbrella** attracts lightning during thunderstorms (on) and slows falling / reduces fall damage when held up (on), **rain clothes** grant extra hearts while raining/storming (on), and there are dedicated integration toggles for the Origins mod so umbrellas and rain hats/coats interact with Origins' rain/sunlight power (both on).

So on this server: watering cans are a real farming convenience item (not just decorative), and umbrellas/rain clothes are functional weather-gear with real mechanical effects, not purely cosmetic. All six of these are enabled by default here.

## How you'll use it here
Despite the name, the useful items here are functional gear, not static decor. Craft a watering can to permanently hydrate farmland with a right-click (a real farming shortcut), carry an umbrella to slow your fall / reduce fall damage when held up (and note it attracts lightning in thunderstorms — a hazard, not a bonus), and wear rain clothes for extra hearts while it's raining or storming. All six of these features are enabled here, so treat them as working equipment when kitting out a base or farm.

## Anticipated questions
**Is the watering can just decorative?** No — it permanently hydrates farmland on right-click, so it's a genuine farming convenience item, and it's enabled by default on this server.

**Should I hold an umbrella out in a thunderstorm?** Be careful — the umbrella attracts lightning during thunderstorms (enabled here). It also slows falling and reduces fall damage when held up, so it's genuinely useful, just risky in a storm.

**Do these need to match on client and server?** Yes — the config is gated behind a warning that changes must match on both client and server and require a full restart.
