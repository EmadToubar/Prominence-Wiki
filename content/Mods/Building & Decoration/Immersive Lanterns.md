# Immersive Lanterns

Immersive Lanterns (by Toni) gives lanterns two upgrades: dynamic (moving) light when carried, and Accessories-mod support so a lantern can be worn/equipped rather than just held or placed, complete with swinging physics as you move. The pack's client config leaves swinging physics on (`Lantern Bounciness = 1.0`, default) and keeps lanterns on the right hand / front of the player (`Left-Handed Lanterns = false`, `Back Lanterns = false`). It's a cosmetic/QoL mod with no server-side gameplay effect — the swing physics are flagged in the mod's own lang file as having no performance impact.

## Anticipated questions
**What does it add?** Two upgrades to lanterns: dynamic (moving) light when a lantern is carried, and Accessories-mod support so a lantern can be worn/equipped rather than only held or placed — with swinging physics as you move.

**Is it client- or server-side?** It's a cosmetic/QoL mod with no server-side gameplay effect; the swing physics are flagged in the mod's own lang file as having no performance impact.

**Do I hold the lantern or wear it?** Either — it can still be held or placed, but Accessories support lets you equip it. The client config keeps lanterns on the right hand / front of the player by default (`Left-Handed Lanterns = false`, `Back Lanterns = false`).

**Can I turn off the swinging?** The pack leaves swinging physics on by default (`Lantern Bounciness = 1.0`), and it's a client config value, so it's a personal visual choice rather than a server setting.
