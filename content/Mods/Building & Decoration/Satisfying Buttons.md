# Satisfying Buttons

Satisfying Buttons (by Toni) is a purely client-feel mod: it adds a darkened hover overlay and a soft click sound to vanilla GUI buttons and widgets, nothing more. The pack's `client.json5` keeps the defaults — animation and sound both enabled, a 300 ms hover-darken fade, and a quiet secondary "unhover" sound (volume 0.5, pitch 1.0). It has nothing to do with in-world button blocks despite the name; it's UI polish, filed here only because it's a tiny single-mechanic mod.
