# Supplementaries

Supplementaries (MehVahdJukaar, Plantkillable) is a large "vanilla+" grab-bag mod — 607 lang entries — that mixes decoration with genuinely functional redstone/utility blocks: globes, notice boards, spinning pedestals, bunting banners, clocks, sconces, and dozens of similar small pieces that feel like they should have been in vanilla. Its own advancement flavor text is self-aware about this ("Spin a globe at ludicrous speed", "Use a Wrench to rotate a block", "Drink from a Chalice"), giving a sense of the range — decorative furniture-scale objects through to small interactive gadgets.

Two config files tune it on this server. `supplementaries-client.json` controls per-block visual/behavior toggles: the **globe** shows a random world and displays coordinates, the **notice board** centers its text, **buntings** are *not* set to fast mode, the **clock block** uses 24-hour format, and the **pedestal** spins (speed 2.0) with fancy renderers on. `supplementaries-common.json` tunes gameplay-affecting values — quiver skeleton spawn chance (3%, difficulty-scaled), a flag's "unbound" radius (64 blocks) and explosion radii (2.0 / 5.15 for different explosive types), and a red-merchant spawn multiplier. No Patchouli guidebook ships with it; blocks are best discovered via JEI/creative search.

## Anticipated questions
**What does it add?** A large "vanilla+" grab-bag (607 lang entries) that mixes decoration with genuinely functional redstone/utility blocks — globes, notice boards, spinning pedestals, bunting banners, clocks, sconces, and dozens of similar small pieces.

**Is it client- or server-side?** Both. `supplementaries-client.json` controls per-block visual/behavior toggles (the globe showing a random world with coordinates, notice-board centered text, 24-hour clock, spinning pedestal, etc.), while `supplementaries-common.json` tunes gameplay-affecting values like quiver-skeleton spawn chance (3%), flag radii, and a red-merchant spawn multiplier.

**How do I find all these blocks?** No Patchouli guidebook ships with it, so blocks are best discovered via JEI / creative search.

**Is it just decoration?** No — it ranges from decorative furniture-scale objects through to small interactive/redstone gadgets, as its own self-aware advancement text ("Spin a globe at ludicrous speed", "Use a Wrench to rotate a block") suggests.
