# Thaumon

Thaumon (jdlenl, shizotoaster, nageih, isengrimus) is explicitly "Thaumcraft-inspired decoration blocks for modern Minecraft" — 141 lang entries of aesthetic-only content evoking the old Thaumcraft look (arcane research towers, eldritch libraries) without any of Thaumcraft's actual magic systems attached. The lang file reads like a themed block set: **arcane stone tiles**, **ancient stone** (window panes, pillars, tile stairs, brick stairs, polished variants), **eldritch stone** (slab, stairs, brick stairs), **silverwood** (an original wood type — slab, fence, window pane, plus "empty" and "dusty" bookshelf variants), **greatwood** (fence gate, bark — a second original wood type), **crystal lamp** and **crystal stand**, **amberglass pane**, and a **research notes** item that's presumably just a lore/decoration book rather than a functional item.

Because it's purely aesthetic, Thaumon slots in as a theme kit for anyone building a wizard-tower, library, or arcane-laboratory style structure — matching Prominence II's broader magic-system mods (see [[Magic & Spells Mods]]) visually without being mechanically tied to them. No config or Patchouli book ships for it.

## Anticipated questions
**What does it add?** 141 lang entries of Thaumcraft-inspired decoration blocks — arcane stone tiles, ancient stone (window panes, pillars, tile/brick stairs, polished variants), eldritch stone, two original wood types (silverwood and greatwood, with fences, panes, bookshelf variants), crystal lamp and crystal stand, amberglass pane, and a research-notes item.

**Does it come with any actual magic?** No — it's purely aesthetic, evoking the old Thaumcraft look (arcane research towers, eldritch libraries) without any of Thaumcraft's magic systems attached. Even the research-notes item is presumably just a lore/decoration book.

**How do I use it in builds?** As a theme kit for wizard-tower, library, or arcane-laboratory style structures — a way to match Prominence II's magic-system mods visually without being mechanically tied to them.

**Is there a config or guidebook?** No — no config file and no Patchouli book ship for it, so it runs on defaults.
