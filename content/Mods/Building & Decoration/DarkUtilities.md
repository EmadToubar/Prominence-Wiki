# DarkUtilities

DarkUtilities (Darkhax) is "a broad assortment of new utility blocks and items" (125 lang entries) that leans more functional/redstone than decorative, despite living in the Building & Decoration category. Its standout feature set is **plates** — pressure-plate-style trigger blocks with much more specific behavior than vanilla's: Blank Plate, Vector Plate (and Fast/Extreme/Ultra Vector Plate tiers, implying detection-speed or range variants), Damage Plate, Maim Plate, Player Damage Plate, and a whole family of status-effect plates (Flame, Slowness, Fatigue, Darkness, Hunger, Weakness, Poison, Wither, Alert, Levitation, Misfortune, Slow Fall, Ominous, Frost). Alongside these are **mob filter** blocks — Undead, Animal, Arthropod, Player, and more — which read as detection/filtering redstone components for selectively triggering on specific mob categories walking over them.

Together these make DarkUtilities a small redstone-trap/detection toolkit — useful for building mob farms with selective triggers, PvP-arena hazard plates, or automated player-detection systems — layered in with the more purely-cosmetic mods in this category. No config file ships for it on this server.

## How you'll use it here
Reach for DarkUtilities when building traps, farms, and arenas rather than pure decoration. Lay Vector Plates (and the Fast/Extreme/Ultra tiers) to shove mobs or items along a floor toward a collection point, use Damage/Maim/status-effect plates (Poison, Wither, Slowness, Levitation, and the rest) as arena hazards or kill floors in a mob farm, and pair them with the mob-filter blocks (Undead, Animal, Arthropod, Player) to trigger only on the mob category you want. Together they make selective mob-sorting and player-detection redstone practical.

## Anticipated questions
**What's the difference between the Vector Plate tiers?** The Blank/Fast/Extreme/Ultra naming implies increasing push speed or range — use a higher tier where you need mobs or items moved faster/farther across the plate.

**Can I trigger a plate only when a specific mob walks over it?** Generally yes — that's what the mob-filter blocks (Undead, Animal, Arthropod, Player, etc.) are for: detection/filtering components that let you fire on a chosen mob category, useful for selective mob-farm triggers or player-detection systems.

**Is there a config to tweak these?** No config file ships for DarkUtilities on this server, so the plates and filters behave at their defaults.
