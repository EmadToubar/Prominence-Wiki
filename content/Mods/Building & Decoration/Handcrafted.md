# Handcrafted

Handcrafted (Alex Nijjar, Kekie6 — "Epic Furniture mod!") is a dedicated furniture mod with 392 lang entries. Its catalog is a fixed furniture set — bench, chair, corner trim, couch, counter, cupboard, desk, dining bench, drawer, fancy bed, nightstand, pillar trim, shelf, side table, table — reproduced across essentially every wood type in the modpack, plus stone "trim" pieces (e.g. andesite corner/pillar trims) for matching stonework builds. This is the mod that gives Prominence II builds a "lived-in" interior feel — tables and chairs to sit at, cupboards and drawers for storage-flavor decoration, nightstands and fancy beds for bedrooms — rather than relying on vanilla's bare furniture-less block palette.

No config file for Handcrafted was found under `config/`, so it runs on its shipped defaults, and there's no Patchouli guidebook — like most mods in this category it's discovered through creative search/JEI. It pairs naturally with [[Supplementaries]] and [[Chipped]] for filling out interiors: Handcrafted for sit-at furniture, the others for smaller shelf-decoration and wall pieces.

## How you'll use it here
This is your go-to for making interiors feel lived-in. Furnish a dining room with a **table**, **chairs**, and **dining benches**; build a kitchen or shop counter from **counters** and **cupboards**; line a study with **shelves**, a **desk**, and **drawers**; and finish a bedroom with a **fancy bed** and matching **nightstand**. Every piece comes across essentially every wood type in the pack, so you can keep a room's furniture consistent with its build palette, and the stone "trim" pieces (like andesite corner and pillar trims) let you carry that detailing onto stonework exteriors.

## Anticipated questions
**Is the furniture functional or just decorative?** It's decoration-focused — chairs, tables, cupboards, and drawers dress a room rather than adding real storage slots. Pair it with [[Supplementaries]]/[[Chipped]] for shelf pieces and functional storage flavor.

**Does it come in the wood type I'm building with?** Almost certainly — the full furniture set is reproduced across essentially every wood type in the modpack, plus some stone trims; check the recipe viewer for your specific wood.

**Is there a guidebook for it?** No Patchouli guidebook and no config here — it runs on defaults, so you discover pieces through creative search / the recipe viewer.
