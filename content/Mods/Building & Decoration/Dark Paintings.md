# Dark Paintings

Dark Paintings (Darkhax) is a small, single-purpose mod adding "a variety of new paintings to the game" — 100 lang entries, which for a paintings mod means roughly that many individual painting artworks (each painting typically needs just a title/author lang entry). It's a pure wall-art expansion: more canvas options for interior decoration than vanilla's fixed painting pool, no new mechanics. Pairs with the same author's [[DarkUtilities]], also present in this modpack.

No config file ships for it here, and there's nothing to tune — paintings are placed the same way vanilla paintings are (canvas + item frame-like placement), just from a much larger art pool.

## How you'll use it here
Use Dark Paintings to fill wall space in interiors with far more art variety than vanilla's fixed pool — roughly 100 individual artworks to choose from. Place them exactly like vanilla paintings (on a wall, item-frame-style), and use them to give galleries, halls, and living spaces distinct character instead of the same handful of default canvases.

## Anticipated questions
**How do I pick which painting appears?** It's placed the same way as vanilla paintings — the artwork shown depends on the available wall space, just drawn from a much larger art pool.

**Is there anything to configure?** No — no config file ships for it here and there's nothing to tune; it's a pure wall-art expansion with no new mechanics.
