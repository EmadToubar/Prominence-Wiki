# YUNG's Better End Island

**YUNG's Better End Island** (jar `YungsBetterEndIsland-1.20-Fabric-2.0.6.jar`, by YUNGNICKYOUNG & Acarii) redesigns the central End island — the Ender Dragon arena itself — "making the Ender Dragon fight much more fun and interesting" than vanilla's flat obsidian-pillar circle.

Server config (`config/betterendisland-fabric-1_20.toml`) sets two flags: `useVanillaSpawnPlatform = false`, meaning the small stone/obsidian platform players first arrive on when entering the End has been replaced with this mod's own arrival structure rather than vanilla's, and `resummonedDragonDropsEgg = false`, meaning re-summoning and re-killing the Ender Dragon after the first kill will **not** drop another dragon egg — closing off repeat-egg farming via dragon resummon crystals.

## Anticipated questions

**Q: What does this change?**
A: It redesigns the central End island — the Ender Dragon arena itself — to make the dragon fight more fun and interesting than vanilla's flat obsidian-pillar circle. See [[Bosses & Recommended Levels]].

**Q: Is the arrival platform different?**
A: Yes — `useVanillaSpawnPlatform = false`, so the small platform you first arrive on when entering the End is replaced with the mod's own arrival structure.

**Q: Can I farm dragon eggs by re-summoning the dragon?**
A: No — `resummonedDragonDropsEgg = false`, so re-killing a re-summoned dragon won't drop another egg.
