# YUNG's Better Witch Huts

**YUNG's Better Witch Huts** (jar `YungsBetterWitchHuts-1.20-Fabric-3.0.3.jar`, by YUNGNICKYOUNG & Acarii) adds "new and improved witch huts to swamps" — replacing the vanilla single-room stilt hut (one cauldron, one crafting table, a witch) with an expanded structure with more rooms and loot.

Server config (`config/betterwitchhuts-fabric-1_20.toml`) sets `disableVanillaWitchHuts = true`, so every swamp hut generated on this world is this mod's redesigned version rather than the vanilla one. No further tuning is configured.

## Anticipated questions

**Q: What's improved about witch huts?**
A: The vanilla single-room stilt hut (one cauldron, one crafting table, a witch) becomes an expanded structure with more rooms and loot. See [[Finding Structures & Exploration]].

**Q: Will vanilla witch huts still generate?**
A: No — `disableVanillaWitchHuts = true`, so every swamp hut on this world is this mod's redesigned version.

**Q: Any other tuning?**
A: No — no further tuning is configured.
