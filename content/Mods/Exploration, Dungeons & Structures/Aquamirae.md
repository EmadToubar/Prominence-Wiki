# Aquamirae

By Obscuria — "Ship graveyard with terrible deep sea creatures." Aquamirae builds a sprawling sunken **ship graveyard** structure into the ocean, guarded by a genuinely dangerous deep-sea bestiary: the **Anglerfish**, **Eel**, **Maw**, **Spinefish**, and **Maze Rose** patrol the wrecks, while the **Golden Moth** and the ghostly **Captain Cornelia** (both rendered in colored name text client-side, marking them as notable/unique spawns) sit above the ordinary mob tier. The graveyard centers on a maze structure guarded by the **Mother of the Maze** — a boss-tier mini-dungeon guardian — and a **Pillagers Patrol** roams nearby, tying the wrecks into the pack's wider pillager content. A **Poisoned Chakram**-wielding **Tortured Soul** rounds out the enemy roster.

Surviving and looting the graveyard requires actual preparation: Aquamirae adds an **Oxygen Tank** item for extended underwater exploration, and drops a full progression of gear — a **Three-Bolt Suit** (diving-suit-style full armor set: helmet/chestplate-equivalent/leggings/boots) and an even more powerful **Abyssal set** ("of the Blinding Abyss" — Tiara, Brigantine, Leggings, Boots, Heaume), plus unique weapons like the **Coral Lance**, **Dagger of Greed**, **Angler's Fang**, **Fin Cutter**, **Poisoned Blade**, **Remnant's Saber**, and **Divider**. Loot containers include **Pirate Pouches** and **Treasure Pouches**, and there are two custom music discs ("Forsaken Drownage" by Krossy, "Horizon" by Bertsz) found in the wrecks. No server-side config file overrides Aquamirae's defaults.

## Anticipated questions

**Q: What do I need before diving the ship graveyard?**
A: Bring the **Oxygen Tank** for extended underwater exploration; Aquamirae also drops a **Three-Bolt Suit** diving armor set and the stronger **Abyssal set** ("of the Blinding Abyss") as you progress through the wrecks.

**Q: What's the main boss here?**
A: The graveyard centers on a maze guarded by the **Mother of the Maze**, a boss-tier dungeon guardian. The ghostly **Captain Cornelia** and **Golden Moth** are notable unique spawns above the ordinary mob tier.

**Q: How dangerous is it?**
A: Very — the wrecks are patrolled by a dangerous deep-sea bestiary (Anglerfish, Eel, Maw, Spinefish, Maze Rose, Tortured Soul) plus a Pillagers Patrol. Gear up first; see [[Progression & Level Caps]] for threat scaling.

**Q: Are there unique rewards worth the risk?**
A: Yes — unique weapons like the **Coral Lance**, **Angler's Fang** and **Dagger of Greed**, loot in Pirate/Treasure Pouches, and two custom music discs ("Forsaken Drownage", "Horizon") found in the wrecks.
