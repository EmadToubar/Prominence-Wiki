# YUNG's Bridges

**YUNG's Bridges** (jar `YungsBridges-1.20-Fabric-4.0.3.jar`, by YUNGNICKYOUNG) is the odd one out in the YUNG's suite — it doesn't overhaul a vanilla structure, it adds one that never existed before: naturally-generating bridges spanning ravines, rivers, and other terrain gaps across the world, giving exploration a bit more connective tissue between landmasses instead of always needing to bridge, boat, or fly across.

No config file exists under `prominence2/config/` for this mod, so it runs at its shipped defaults. Like every other YUNG's mod it hard-depends on [[YUNG's API]] (`yungsapi >= 1.20-Fabric-4.0.1` per its `fabric.mod.json`) for shared structure-placement code.

## Anticipated questions

**Q: What does YUNG's Bridges add?**
A: Naturally-generating bridges spanning ravines, rivers, and terrain gaps — it adds a new structure rather than overhauling a vanilla one. See [[Finding Structures & Exploration]].

**Q: How does that help exploration?**
A: It gives exploration more connective tissue between landmasses instead of always needing to bridge, boat, or fly across gaps.

**Q: Is it configured or dependent on anything?**
A: It runs at shipped defaults (no config file) and hard-depends on [[YUNG's API]] for shared structure-placement code.
