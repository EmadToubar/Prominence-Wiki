# YUNG's Better Strongholds

**YUNG's Better Strongholds** (jar `YungsBetterStrongholds-1.20-Fabric-4.0.3.jar`, by YUNGNICKYOUNG & Acarii) is a "complete redesign of Minecraft's strongholds," rebuilding the maze of samey corridors and libraries into a more coherent, larger layout leading to the End Portal room.

Unlike most of its siblings, this mod's config (`config/betterstrongholds-fabric-1_20.toml`) has no `disableVanilla...` toggle — since strongholds are a singular structure type rather than one of several variants, this mod's generator simply is the stronghold generator once installed. What it does tune is decoration/hazard density: cobweb replacement chance is 10% in normal rooms and 30% in spawner rooms (making the stronghold's silverfish spawner rooms noticeably webbier and more dangerous), torch spawn rate 10%, and lantern spawn rate 20%.

## Anticipated questions

**Q: What changes about strongholds?**
A: The maze of samey corridors and libraries becomes a more coherent, larger layout leading to the End Portal room. See [[Finding Structures & Exploration]].

**Q: Is there a disable-vanilla toggle like the other YUNG's mods?**
A: No — strongholds are a singular structure type, so this mod simply *is* the stronghold generator once installed.

**Q: Are the silverfish spawner rooms more dangerous here?**
A: Yes — cobweb replacement is 30% in spawner rooms (vs 10% in normal rooms), making them noticeably webbier and more dangerous; torches spawn at 10%, lanterns at 20%.
