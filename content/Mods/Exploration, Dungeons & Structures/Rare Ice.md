# Rare Ice

By shedaniel (author of Cloth Config / REI, better known for tooling than content mods) — a tiny, single-purpose world-flavor mod: it scatters a genuinely rare variant of ice into the world for players to stumble across ("Can you find them?"). Server config (`config/rare-ice.properties`) sets the spawn probability to 3 (in whatever base unit the mod uses — low either way) and disables inserting items into the ice block (`allowInsertingItemsToIce=false`), so it functions purely as a rare decorative/collectible find rather than a functional storage or crafting block.
