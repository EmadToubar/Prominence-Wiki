# Winterly

By PinkGoosik and VioletSomik — a lighter, more decoration-focused winter-expansion mod that complements Frostiful's survival-mechanics depth. Generates new cold-biome terrain features by default: underground icicles, Cryomarble deposits, and frozen grass/flowers. Also adds gift boxes (server-configured to hold up to 3 items) for a holiday-flavor loot/gifting mechanic. One feature is explicitly **disabled** on this server: seasonal `mobDecorations` (cosmetic winter dressing on mobs, meant to only trigger during an in-game "winter" period) is turned off (`config/winterly.json`: `"enabled": false`), so mobs do not get winter cosmetic reskins here.
