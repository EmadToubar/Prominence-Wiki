# Geophilic

By bebebea_loste (also responsible for Explorify) — described as "a subtle-ish overhaul of vanilla Overworld biomes." Rather than adding wholly new biomes the way Regions Unexplored does, Geophilic reworks the look and feel of existing vanilla biomes for more visual variety on the ground, aiming to stay recognizably vanilla rather than exotic. No server-side config override is present.

## Anticipated questions

**Q: Does Geophilic add new biomes?**
A: No — it's a subtle overhaul that reworks the look and feel of existing vanilla Overworld biomes for more visual variety, staying recognizably vanilla. For wholly new biomes see [[Regions Unexplored]].

**Q: Can I search for a particular biome?**
A: Yes — use [[Nature's Compass]] to locate the nearest instance of a biome.
