# Simply Houses

A small, self-descriptive structure mod by Aurelj, DiamondTown, and Zephyrusj that does exactly what its name says — generates simple houses scattered around the world as extra light points of interest, without adding new blocks, items, or mechanics of its own (the jar's assets are just a logo, no lang file or config). It runs on shipped defaults with no server tuning.

## Anticipated questions

**Q: What does Simply Houses add?**
A: Simple houses scattered around the world as extra light points of interest. See [[Finding Structures & Exploration]].

**Q: Does it add new blocks or items?**
A: No — no new blocks, items, or mechanics of its own; the jar's assets are just a logo.
