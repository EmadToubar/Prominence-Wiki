# YUNG's Better Jungle Temples

**YUNG's Better Jungle Temples** (jar `YungsBetterJungleTemples-1.20-Fabric-2.0.5.jar`, by YUNGNICKYOUNG & Tera) redesigns the vanilla jungle temple — normally a cramped, vine-choked stone box with one lever puzzle and a couple of dispenser traps — into a larger structure with more rooms, loot, and puzzle mechanics consistent with the rest of the YUNG's "Better" line.

Server config (`config/betterjungletemples-fabric-1_20.toml`) sets `disableVanillaJungleTemples = true`, so vanilla jungle temples are fully replaced rather than generating alongside this mod's version. No other tuning is present — the mod runs at its shipped defaults otherwise.

## Anticipated questions

**Q: What's improved about jungle temples?**
A: The cramped vanilla box (one lever puzzle, a couple of traps) becomes a larger structure with more rooms, loot, and puzzle mechanics consistent with the rest of the YUNG's line. See [[Finding Structures & Exploration]].

**Q: Will vanilla jungle temples still appear?**
A: No — `disableVanillaJungleTemples = true` fully replaces them with this mod's version.

**Q: Is anything else tuned?**
A: No — beyond that toggle it runs at shipped defaults.
