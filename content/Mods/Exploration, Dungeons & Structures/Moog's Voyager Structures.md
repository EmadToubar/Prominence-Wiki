# Moog's Voyager Structures

**Moog's Voyager Structures** (jar `MoogsVoyagerStructures-1.20-5.0.4.jar`, by FinnDog) adds — per its own description — "life, fun and variety to your Minecraft world! Explore awesome new structures made with vanilla blocks and entities, and discover loot, enemies, and villagers along the way." It's explicitly built to "keep the vanilla feel of the game" rather than introduce a distinct art style, and its jar contains **285 `.nbt` structure files** — a scale comparable to Philip's Ruins in this pack.

It declares a hard dependency on `moogs_structures` in its `fabric.mod.json`, i.e. on **[[Moog's Structure Lib]]**, which must be present for it to load. This server also carries a small companion jar, `lootintegrations_moog-1.8.jar`, which patches Moog's structure loot tables to integrate with other installed mods' items rather than staying vanilla-only — a detail worth knowing if a Moog structure's chest loot looks more varied than expected.

## Anticipated questions

**Q: Where do these structures generate?**
A: Across the Overworld — new structures built with vanilla blocks and entities, keeping the vanilla feel. See [[Finding Structures & Exploration]] for tracking them down.

**Q: What's inside them?**
A: Loot, enemies, and villagers, per the mod's own description. Chest loot can look more varied than expected because the companion `lootintegrations_moog-1.8.jar` patches its loot tables to include other installed mods' items.

**Q: How much content does it add?**
A: Its jar contains 285 `.nbt` structure files — a scale comparable to [[Philip's Ruins]].

**Q: Does it need anything else to work?**
A: Yes — it hard-depends on [[Moog's Structure Lib]] (`moogs_structures`), which must be present for it to load.
