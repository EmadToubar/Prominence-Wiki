# Swampier Swamps

By andrew0030 — overhauls the Swamp biome and the Wild Update's mangrove-swamp additions with several small but interconnected systems, all live at their shipped defaults (`config/swampier_swamps.json5`):

- **Moist Fertile Farmland**: farmland within 3 blocks of water can turn into a more fertile variant, and crops there grow 6x faster than vanilla.
- **Swamp Gas**: an explosive, poisonous gas hazard (explosion strength 6, applies negative effects like Poison to anything that walks through the cloud) that also works as furnace fuel (4000-tick burn time).
- **"Swallow Me Waldo" frogs**: a named frog that can swallow and effectively kill entities, dealing double its 10-base damage by default — capable of taking down very tough mobs if left unchecked.
- **Growing Lily Pads**: Small → Regular → Large Lily Pads grow over time and spawn more around themselves; they also sink under large entities standing on them and eventually break (rather than just going collision-less).
- **Bald Cypress trees**: can grow from 2x2 Oak sapling plantings (like vanilla's Jungle-tree mechanic), enabled in all biomes on this server.
- Altered brewing recipes: the Leaping potion now uses Frog Legs instead of Rabbit Paws, and a new Luck potion recipe uses Rabbit Paws.
