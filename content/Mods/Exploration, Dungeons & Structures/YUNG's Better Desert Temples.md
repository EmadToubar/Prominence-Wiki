# YUNG's Better Desert Temples

**YUNG's Better Desert Temples** (jar `YungsBetterDesertTemples-1.20-Fabric-3.0.3.jar`, by YUNGNICKYOUNG & Tera) redesigns the vanilla desert pyramid — which in vanilla is little more than a decorative shell around one TNT-trapped loot room — into a larger, more elaborate temple with expanded rooms and puzzle elements.

Server config (`config/betterdeserttemples-fabric-1_20.toml`) sets `disableVanillaPyramids = true`, so vanilla desert pyramids no longer generate at all — every desert pyramid players find is this mod's version. It also enables `applyMiningFatigue = true`, meaning parts of the temple apply a Mining Fatigue effect to discourage brute-forcing through walls instead of solving the temple's traps/puzzles legitimately.

## Anticipated questions

**Q: What changes about desert temples?**
A: The vanilla pyramid (a shell around one TNT-trapped loot room) is redesigned into a larger temple with expanded rooms and puzzle elements. See [[Finding Structures & Exploration]].

**Q: Will I still find vanilla pyramids?**
A: No — `disableVanillaPyramids = true`, so every desert pyramid is this mod's version.

**Q: Anything stopping me digging straight to the loot?**
A: Yes — `applyMiningFatigue = true` applies Mining Fatigue in parts of the temple to discourage brute-forcing through walls instead of solving the traps/puzzles.
