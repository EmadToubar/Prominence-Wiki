# ChoiceTheorem's Overhauled Village (CTOV)

"Enhances and creates new villages and pillager outposts that perfectly fit into your Minecraft world" — CTOV is the most extensive of the pack's village mods in raw structure count. It replaces vanilla village generation with heavily biome-themed variants, each shipped in Small/Medium/Large size tiers: Beach, Christmas, Dark Forest, Desert, Desert Fortified, Jungle, Jungle Tree, Badlands (Mesa), Badlands Fortified, Japanese-style Mountain, Alpine Mountain, Mushroom, Plains, Plains Fortified, Savanna, Savanna Native-American-style, Snowy, Swamp, Swamp Fortified, and Taiga — around 20 distinct village themes times 3 size tiers. It depends on `lithostitched` for its structure-set plumbing.

Like Towns and Towers, this overlaps in purpose with the pack's other village mods but at a different layer: CTOV supplies the actual new village *structures/themes*, while Towns and Towers reworks vanilla's existing village/outpost pool and biome coverage, Integrated Villages changes village behavior and Create-machine integration, and Villagers Plus adds new professions and trades that populate whichever villages generate.

## Anticipated questions

**Q: How is CTOV different from the pack's other village mods?**
A: CTOV supplies the actual new biome-themed village *structures* (around 20 themes in Small/Medium/Large tiers), while [[Towns and Towers]] reworks vanilla's village/outpost pool, [[Integrated Villages]] changes village behavior, and [[Villagers Plus]] adds professions and trades.

**Q: What village types can I find?**
A: Heavily biome-themed variants — Beach, Desert, Jungle, Badlands, Mountain (Japanese and Alpine styles), Mushroom, Plains, Savanna, Snowy, Swamp, Taiga and more, several with Fortified variants.

**Q: How do I locate one?**
A: Use [[Explorer's Compass]] to point to the nearest village structure.
