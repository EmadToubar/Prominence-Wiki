# When Dungeons Arise

**When Dungeons Arise** (jar `DungeonsArise-1.20.x-2.1.58-fabric-release.jar`, by Aureljz, DiamondTown & Zephyrusj) is one of the biggest single contributors of hand-built content in the pack: its `data/dungeons_arise/structures/` folder ships **42 distinct structure families**, most with several NBT variants for different mob loadouts or loot tiers (e.g. the Undead Pirate Ship has separate captain, ranged-skeleton and skeleton-juggernaut variants; the Wishing Well has five potion-effect variants — speed, regen, wither, resistance, haste — plus a no-effect fallback). Notable finds include the Coliseum, the Illager Castle/Fort/Hall/Windmill/Corsair/Galley set, the airship-themed Heavenly Rider/Conqueror/Challenger, the mythology-flavored Typhon and Ceryneian Hind bosses, the Foundry, Mining System and Scorched Mines, the Shiraz Palace, Keep Kayra, Plague Asylum, Bandit Village/Towers, the Mushroom Village/House/Mines trio, Greenwood Pub/Towers, a Bathhouse, Jungle Tree House, Monastery, Lighthouse, Aviary, Merchant Campsite, Small Blimp, Small Prairie House, Fishing Hut, Infested/Abandoned Temples, and a Mechanical Nest.

Many structures award **Explorer Map** filled-map items (e.g. "Illager Castle Explorer Map") tied to advancement triggers, nudging players toward specific landmarks rather than pure random wandering. No dedicated config file exists under `prominence2/config/` for this mod, so it's running with the pack's default spawn rarity and structure toggles — unlike the YUNG's suite, nothing here has been hand-tuned for this server.

## Anticipated questions

**Q: How much does it add?**
A: 42 distinct structure families, most with several NBT variants for different mob loadouts or loot tiers — one of the biggest hand-built content contributors in the pack. See [[Finding Structures & Exploration]].

**Q: What are some notable finds?**
A: The Coliseum, the Illager Castle/Fort/Hall set, airship-themed Heavenly Rider/Conqueror/Challenger, mythology bosses Typhon and Ceryneian Hind, the Foundry, Shiraz Palace, Keep Kayra, Plague Asylum, and many more.

**Q: How do I locate its structures?**
A: Many award Explorer Map items (e.g. "Illager Castle Explorer Map") tied to advancement triggers, nudging you toward specific landmarks rather than pure wandering. See [[Bosses & Recommended Levels]] for the boss-bearing structures.

**Q: Is its rarity tuned on this server?**
A: No — no dedicated config file exists, so it runs at default spawn rarity and structure toggles, unlike the hand-tuned YUNG's suite.
