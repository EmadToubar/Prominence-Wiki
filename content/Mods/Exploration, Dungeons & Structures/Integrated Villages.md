# Integrated Villages

"Adds integration and drastically improves Minecraft Villages" — rather than adding new village *structures* like CTOV or Towns and Towers, Integrated Villages changes village *mechanics*, and depends on `integrated_api`, `create`, and `supplementaries` to do it. Two settings are server-tuned in `config/integrated_villages-fabric-1_20.toml`, and both are meaningful: **`disableVanillaVillages = true`** — meaning stock vanilla village generation is turned off entirely on this server, so every village players find is generated by one of the pack's overhaul mods (CTOV / Towns and Towers) instead of vanilla's own village pool — and **`activateCreateContraptions = true`**, which lets Create-mod kinetic contraptions (the mod's real machinery, e.g. water wheels) function properly within generated villages.

## Anticipated questions

**Q: Why do all my villages look modded?**
A: Because `disableVanillaVillages = true` on this server — stock vanilla village generation is off, so every village you find comes from an overhaul mod like [[ChoiceTheorem's Overhauled Village]] or [[Towns and Towers]].

**Q: Do Create machines work in villages?**
A: Yes — `activateCreateContraptions = true` lets Create kinetic contraptions (e.g. water wheels) function properly within generated villages.
