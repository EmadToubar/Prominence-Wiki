# River Redux

By SuperCoder79 (runs on the `terrablender` biome-injection framework, shared with Regions Unexplored) — "Improves Minecraft's river biomes" by splitting the single vanilla river biome into four distinct variants: **Carved River**, **Gravelly River**, **Sandy River**, and **Tropical River**, each presumably with terrain/vegetation suited to the surrounding biome it cuts through. A small, focused mod with no server-side config override.

## Anticipated questions

**Q: What does River Redux change?**
A: It splits the single vanilla river biome into four variants — Carved River, Gravelly River, Sandy River, and Tropical River — each suited to the biome it cuts through.

**Q: How do I find a specific river variant?**
A: Since these are biomes, [[Nature's Compass]] can locate the nearest one; see also [[Finding Structures & Exploration]].

**Q: Is it configured on this server?**
A: No — it's a small, focused mod with no server-side config override.
