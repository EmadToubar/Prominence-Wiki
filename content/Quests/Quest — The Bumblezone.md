# The Bumblezone

A dimension chapter for **The Bumblezone** — a self-contained world of bees and honey. It's a large, exploration-and-collection chapter (find dozens of structures, gear, and essences) rather than a boss gauntlet, and it culminates in earning the **favor of the Queen Bee**. Mostly optional side content, though its enchanting garden is genuine end-game utility. See [[Dimensions & Space Travel Mods]] and [[Creatures, Bosses & Mobs]].

## Walkthrough (highlights, in rough order)

- **World of Bee and Honey** — Go to the Bumblezone (the entry quest).
- **Hive's Air Pollen-tion** — The air is full of pollen; introduces Pollen Puff, Honey Bucket, Bee Bread, Honeycomb Brood.
- **Bee Weaponry** — Craft bee gear from stingers/honey: **Stinger Spear**, **Crystal Cannon**, **Honey Crystal Shield**.
- **The Bee Armor** — Full set (helmet/chestplate/leggings/boots); nice-looking, not very powerful.
- **Honey Compass & Sempiternal Sanctums** — A **Honey Compass** leads to important sites. Find the six colored **Sempiternal Sanctums** (Blue, Green, Purple, Red, White, Yellow), each tied to an **Essence** (Calming, Life, Knowing, Raging, Continuity, Radiance).
- **Structure hunt** — A long list of "Find" quests: Cell Maze, Stinger Spear Shrine, Subway, **Queen Bee's Throne (Throne Pillar)**, Pollinated Stream, Honitel (honey hotel), Slime Ranch, Pirate Ship, Overgrown Flower, Ice Monolith, Candle Parkour, Bee House, Ancient Hoops, Dance Floor, Hive Temple, Honey Fountain, Cannon Range, Honey Cave Room, Battle Cubes, Ancient Shrine, Cherry Veteran Tree.
- **One of Us! One of Us!** — Meet the **Queen Bee** at her Throne, trade/do tasks, and earn **Essence of the Bees** so the bees stop attacking you. ("Now you are one of her kin.")
- **The Crystalline Flower (Lv 65)** — Endgame enchanting: place a **Crystalline Flower** on **Glistering Honey Crystals** in the **Hanging Gardens**, offer items to grow it, and choose **specific enchantments** to put into a book. **Requires Level 65 to use.** See [[Accessories & Enchanting]].
- **Music discs** — A big set of bee-themed discs to collect ("Bee Jams").

## Tips

- Grab the **Honey Compass** early — most of the structure and Sanctum finds key off it.
- The real reason to come here late is the **Crystalline Flower** (Lv 65): targeted, chosen enchantments are extremely valuable — but you can't use it until level 65.
- Earning the Queen's favor (Essence of the Bees) stops bee aggression, making everything else far easier.

## Anticipated questions

- **Why are the bees attacking me?** Until you earn the Queen Bee's favor (Essence of the Bees) via her Throne, they're hostile.
- **What's the main endgame reward here?** The Crystalline Flower in the Hanging Gardens — it lets you pick specific enchantments into a book, but requires Level 65.
- **How do I navigate this dimension?** Use the Honey Compass; it points to the Sanctums and key structures.
