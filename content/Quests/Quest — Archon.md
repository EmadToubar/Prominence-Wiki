# Archon

**[[Archon]]** is a runes-and-mana sorcery mod: you infuse mana into blocks to make magic materials, gear, spell tomes, and summon elemental bosses. This chapter ("Are you ready to become a Sorcerer?") teaches the full rune workflow. See [[Magic & Spells Mods]]. Archon uses its own mana pool, separate from [[Botania]].

## Walkthrough

1. **First Magic Steps** — Craft the **Channeler**, a wand that infuses mana into blocks to create magic materials. Hold the Channeler in your off-hand and a block of your chosen metal to work it. Experiment with materials (e.g. **Sorcerer's Metal**, **Pixie Leaves** from leaves, **Spry Dust** from mana-infused sand).
2. **Boost your mana** — Find **Ancient Scrolls** to raise mana capacity or regen. Build **Mana Catalysts** — tiered auras that boost nearby players' mana capacity (+50 mana / 16-block radius, up to +200 / 34-block radius). The **Mask of Power** helmet gives 4 mana/sec while worn.
3. **Find a Spire** — Spires are tall towers in forest/mountain biomes with loot (watch for leeches).
4. **Gather essences** — **Earth Essence** (deep underground), **End Essence** (End islands), plus you must **defeat the four Elementals** — Earth, Sky, Fire, Water — to harvest their essences.
5. **Spellcasting** — Build the **Scripture Table** to craft spell tomes (alchemy-style). Each **Wand** casts only its element's spells: **Fire** (Nether), **Sky** (villages), **Water** (ocean structures/fishing), **End** (End Cities/Strongholds), **Earth** (temples/mineshafts).
6. **Bosses** — Use the **Pedestal** to summon bosses with the right ingredients (from your Grimoire): shift-click removes ingredients; right-click the ingredients on directly (no GUI).

## Notable weapons & rewards
The Channeler crafts a suite of magic weapons/armor, each with an ability:
- **Blade** — teleport 8 blocks forward; **Scepter** — teleport 10 blocks with implosion damage.
- **Coat** — fire resistance + mana-scaling implosion; **Sword** — slowness cloud; **Bow** — Ghast fireball; **Stave** — wither skulls; **Mace** — ground slam launching mobs (spares pets); **Axe** — brief resistance + projectile deflection.
- **Grim Reaper's Toys** — the **Crusher** converts kills to mana (20/mob, 30/player, 50/boss); the **Scythe** reaps souls; the **Staff of Undead** summons minions from a core.
- **Harvesters** (Fire/Breezy/Soaking/Warping/Rocky) increase element-related mob loot; **XP Pouches** store 550 → 2920 XP.
- **Magical Farmer boots** fertilize crops with mana when you crouch.

## Anticipated questions
- **How do I make magic materials?** Off-hand the **Channeler** and hold a metal block, then infuse mana into blocks.
- **Why won't my wand cast a spell?** Each elemental **Wand** only casts spells of its own element, found in that element's structures.
- **How do I summon a boss?** Put the Grimoire's listed ingredients on a **Pedestal** by right-clicking them onto it — there's no GUI.
