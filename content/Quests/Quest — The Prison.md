# The Prison

A dimension chapter built on the **Mine Cells** mod (Dead Cells crossover). You enter a randomly-spawning Prison dungeon, fight the giant eye **Conjunctivius**, and unlock the Cell Crafter weapon system. Conjunctivius doubles as a [[Quest — Main Story|Void's Invasion]] herald (Rec. Lv 30), so this chapter pairs naturally with the mid-game campaign. See [[Exploration, Dungeons & Structures Mods]] and [[Creatures, Bosses & Mobs]].

## Walkthrough

- **Enter the Prison** — The entrance spawns **randomly throughout the Overworld**; find it to enter the Prison dimension.
- **Defeat Conjunctivius** — Spawns in **The Lair**, found underground within the Prison. To damage it you must **first kill its tentacles**. (For the Main Story version you first need a Vine Rune from the Promenade — see [[Quest — Main Story|Void's Invasion]].)
- **Visit the Promenade** — Take the **elevator on the last floor** of the Prison to reach the **Promenade of the Condemned**. Bring a **Waystone or Pocket Wormhole** so you can get home.
- **Cell Crafter system**:
  - **Craft a Cell Crafter** — Makes various melee and ranged weapons for the Mine Cells armory.
  - **Loot a Monster Cell** — Drops from monsters in the Prison dimension; needed as crafting material.
  - **Craft Cell-Infused Steel** — Some Cell Crafter weapons require it.
- **Quick Healing! (Health Flask)** — Cell-crafted from **Guts + Monster Cells**; heals **10% of max health** and grants regeneration for a few seconds — a very useful sustain item.

## Tips

- Bring **fast travel back** (Waystone / Pocket Wormhole) before going deep — the Promenade is far from home.
- Against **Conjunctivius**, focus the **tentacles first**; the body takes no damage until they're gone.
- The **Health Flask** is worth prioritizing early in the chapter for its cheap, repeatable healing.

## Anticipated questions

- **Where's the Prison entrance?** It spawns randomly around the Overworld — you have to find it.
- **Why can't I hurt Conjunctivius?** Kill its tentacles first; only then can you damage the eye.
- **What's the Cell Crafter for?** Making Mine Cells melee/ranged weapons (and the Health Flask) using Monster Cells, Guts, and Cell-Infused Steel.
- **How do I leave the Promenade?** Bring a Waystone or Pocket Wormhole in with you — it's suggested before you take the elevator.
