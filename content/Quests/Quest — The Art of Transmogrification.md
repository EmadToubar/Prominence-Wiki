# The Art of Transmogrification

A short cosmetic chapter covering **[[Transmog|transmogrification]]** — magic that changes the *shape* of any item (weapons and armor included) without altering its function. It's purely cosmetic and gives **no** stat benefit. See [[Accessories & Enchanting]].

## Walkthrough

1. **The Art of Transmogrification** — Craft a **Transmogrification Table**. This magic is powered by **Amethyst**, so you'll need some each time you transform an item.
2. **Transmog an item** — No items are consumed, but to change an item's appearance into another you must **own the desired item** (the one whose look you're copying).
3. **The Illusion of Nothing** — To *hide* an armor piece or weapon entirely, use a **Void Fragment** as the transmoggable material.

## Tips
- Because nothing is consumed, you can freely restyle gear as your look changes — just keep some Amethyst on hand.
- Great for keeping a favorite endgame weapon's stats while showing off a rarer skin (or hiding a slot completely).

## Anticipated questions
- **Does transmog change my stats?** No — it's purely cosmetic, no benefit beyond appearance.
- **What powers it?** Amethyst, consumed each time you transform.
- **Do I lose the item I want to look like?** No — you just need to *own* the desired item; nothing is consumed.
- **Can I hide a piece entirely?** Yes — transmog it using a **Void Fragment**.
