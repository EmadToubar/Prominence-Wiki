# Enchanting

This chapter teaches the pack's deep **enchanting overhaul** (built on the Eterna/Quanta/Arcana stat system) — from a basic Enchanting Table up to an Enchanting Library holding every enchant, plus tomes, charms, and infusions. See [[Accessories & Enchanting]] and [[EMI Enchanting]].

## The Enchanting Table stats

The table shows three bars:
- **Eterna** — the table's level (and cost). Your required XP level is **2× Eterna** (50 Eterna = 100 levels).
- **Quanta** — pure randomness; the more you add, the more chaotic results get. **Not recommended to max.**
- **Arcana** — rarity and number of enchants. **Don't max it** if you want plain enchants like Protection or class enchants like Sunfire — lower Arcana for those.

## Bookshelf progression

You build power by surrounding the table with increasingly exotic bookshelves. The rough ladder:

1. **Seashelves / Hellshelves** — your first stat shelves; you need ~15 total (a mix is fine) to get started.
2. **Infused shelves** — infuse the above with **Crystaline/Heartforged Seashelves** or **Blazing/Glowing Hellshelves** (need ≥2 of the higher ones). Following the chapter's counts gets you to ~30 Eterna.
3. **Echoing / Deep Dark shelves** — need Ancient City loot (**Echo Shards**); make ~8. This is where players "learn to love or hate" the grind.
4. **Draconic Endshelves** (Ender Dragon Heads from End ships) and **Endshelves** (End Rods) — the last mile; make several spares.

Set up an **infusion setup** to upgrade shelves and infuse **Tomes** and **Charms**.

## Tomes, charms & the Library

- **Tomes** — scrapping/extraction tomes pull enchants off items; gear-specific tomes get enchants only possible on certain gearsets (e.g. Feather Falling). **Read a tome before using it** — some destroy the item. Infused tomes work with an **Anvil enchanted with Splitting** to break a big enchanted book into its components.
- **Anvil enchants** — **Obliteration** and **Splitting** (the latter is one of the only ways to extract *some* enchants from a weapon/book).
- **Enchanting Library** — the payoff: stores *all* your enchants (and curses) in one block, mineable, and **AE2-compatible** (face a Storage Bus at it). A bigger/fancier version exists ("Library of Alexandria").
- **Charms** — crafted from 3 of the same potion for longer effects; they have durability. Make them last with **Unbreaking + Mending**, a **Hardening Catalyst**, or **Infusion** (learned later). The final quests show how to make charms **Unbreakable**.

## Odds & ends flagged in-quest
- **Special Cobweb** removes curses that can't be removed normally.
- **Infused Dragon's Breath** — take it from the dragon (bring bottles), buy from Alchemist Villagers (higher-tier trade), or loot dungeons.
- **Ghast Tears** — from Journeyman Alchemist trades or Nether dungeons.
- **Getting levels** — Looting, Scavenger, Soul Devourer, and **Knowledge of the Ages** (converts items to XP — **don't use on bosses**), plus the **Bag of Souls** armor enchant. Bulk-**haunt** items with an Encased Fan over soul fire for some materials.

## Anticipated questions
- **Why am I getting bad/random enchants?** Too much **Quanta** (chaos) or **Arcana** (rarity). Lower Arcana for plain enchants like Protection/Sunfire.
- **How much XP do I need?** 2× your Eterna value in levels.
- **How do I move an enchant off a bad weapon?** An Anvil enchanted with **Splitting** + extraction tomes.
- **Best endgame storage for enchants?** The **Enchanting Library** — it holds everything and hooks into an [[Applied Energistics 2|AE2]] Storage Bus.
