# Mastering Fate

A very small chapter centered on **Fate Talents** — the powerful capstone choices in the talent system. Its readable quest text in this install is sparse (essentially a single node referencing a "Knowing" essence), so the concrete detail below is drawn from the related systems note rather than invented quest steps. Treat this chapter as a pointer toward the deeper mechanic explained in [[Talent Tree & Fate Talents]].

> Note: this chapter's quest text is too thin to build a full step-by-step walkthrough from. The framing below is general and flagged as such.

## What this chapter is about

- **Fate Talents** are the defining, build-shaping talents you fate (commit to) at higher levels — e.g. the archmage/element fates, Shadow Techniques, S'kellak's Blessing, Warrior's Devotion, A Bard's Tale, and the Knowledge of Runes and Stars (Astral) fates referenced throughout the build guides in [[Quest — New Mechanics|New Mechanics]].
- Choosing a Fate steers your class identity (Mage element, Rogue Haste, Tank endurance, etc.). Reset options use the same Knowledge Scroll system as the rest of the talent tree.

## Tips

- Because this chapter is content-light, use it as a signpost: the actual Fate choices and their effects are documented in [[Talent Tree & Fate Talents]] and applied per-build in [[Quest — New Mechanics|New Mechanics]].
- Don't fate blindly — your Fate should match the stat priorities of the build you're playing ([[Archetypes & Builds]]).
