# The Hasturian Era

The expansion campaign that follows [[Quest — Main Story|Void's Invasion]]. **You must finish the entire Void's Invasion campaign to unlock this storyline.** Lore: eons ago S'kellak imprisoned the primordial C'thulhu, but its half-brother — a tall hooded, tentacled monster, **Hastur, The King in Yellow** — broke free. With the realms at their weakest after the Void's invasion, Hastur returns to claim them. This chapter is partly live and partly **coming in v4.0** (the Cinderstone Depths dimension and Kingdom of Vaaz), so expect some quests to be teasers.

## Walkthrough

- **The Hasturian Era (unlock)** — Requires finishing the Void's Invasion campaign, which also **unlocks 51–65 leveling**. Read the lore intro on Hastur's escape and return.
- **Missive from S'kellak** — A cinderstone missive confirms the enemy is Hastur, warns the Overworld is unsafe (Hastur twists minds), and tells you to enchant your weapon with **Smite** because his followers are **undead**. It directs you to signal a beacon to travel to Vaaz.
- **Gu'mas, The Fallen Archmage** (Required Level ~60–65) — The former Hex leader, killed by the Champions of Vaaz and resurrected by Hastur. He kidnaps/kills anyone trying to reach the Cinderstone Depths. **Use Smite to shred his barrier** if you lack the required spell power. Defeat the Resurrected Magus, Gu'mas.
- **Kingdom of Vaaz** — Use the **Beacon of Hope** in the Overworld to travel to Vaaz and meet S'kellak.
- **Path to Corruption: Infusions** — **Corruption Infusions** add corruption to armor (fuels corruption-scaled active talent abilities). Template drops in End cities; use in a Smithing Table with the item + a material: Aberration Limb (+10), Aberration Heart (+20), X'al the Faceless Eye (+35).
- **Path to Etyr: Infusions** — **Etyr Infusions** add Corruption Resistance that counteracts your corruption. Negative effects scale off your *Total* Corruption (which accounts for Etyr). Template in End cities; infuse an Etyr Ingot (+10) via Smithing Table.
- **Journey to Vaaz** — The Beacon of Hope breaks; with no way to reach S'kellak you'll need another route. This questline is flagged as **coming in v4.0**, along with the Cinderstone Depths and Kingdom of Vaaz. In the meantime it jokingly tells you to "drink away your sorrows."
- **Blog link quest** — Points to the official 4.0 development blog (link on the wiki / above the quest).

## Tips

- This is **level 60+ content** — max out toward Lv 65 and gear up (artifacts, infusions) before starting.
- **Smite** is essential again here: Hastur's followers are undead, and it breaks Gu'mas's barrier.
- Balance **Corruption** (powers your abilities) against **Etyr** (resistance) — going all-in on corruption without Etyr means heavier negative effects.

## Anticipated questions

- **Why is this chapter locked?** You must complete the whole Void's Invasion campaign first; doing so also unlocks 51–65 leveling.
- **Why can't I finish the Journey to Vaaz?** Part of this chapter (Cinderstone Depths, Kingdom of Vaaz) is unreleased content slated for v4.0.
- **How do I beat Gu'mas if my spell power is too low?** Enchant with Smite — it shreds his barrier.
- **Corruption vs Etyr?** Corruption boosts corruption-scaled abilities but its downsides scale with your Total Corruption; Etyr adds resistance that offsets that total.
