# The Scarred Night

A **seasonal Halloween event** chapter (available roughly **October 20 – mid-November**). Framed as "The Crows' Guide to Destroy the Scarecrows," it's a short, story-driven questline that teaches pumpkin-carving mechanics and ends with a boss, **Sir Scar Crow** — plus a Mythic version for endgame players. See [[Creatures, Bosses & Mobs]].

## Walkthrough (Crow's Guide, page by page)

- **I feel something in the air...** — Play during the Halloween event window to start.
- **Getting ready to Carve (Page 1)** — Get a **Pumpkin**.
- **The Tools (Page 2)** — Get **Shears**, then place the pumpkin and use shears on it to **carve** it.
- **Carving! / Lights up! (Pages 3–4)** — Combine your carved pumpkin with a **torch** to make a **Jack o' Lantern** (light is what brings scarecrows to life).
- **What We Know (Page 5)** — Lore on the leader: it has a metal hook on its left arm; you'll need to try different **face carvings** until the right one works.
- **Work Ain't Easy (Page 6)** — Make a **Botanist Workbench** to re-cut Jack o' Lanterns into better designs.
- **Fancy Carving (Page 7)** — Carve a fancy design at the workbench.
- **Summoning the Bastard (Page 8)** — Build the specified structure and try carved Jack o' Lanterns on it until the leader appears.
- **Sir Scar Crow (boss)** — A cowardly ranged boss that flees and attacks from distance while summoning undead. Defeating him rewards **his weapon, The Scarred Night** (stolen back from him by the Crows).
- **Sir Scar Crow (Mythic)** — Endgame version. Requires **finishing the Void's Invasion campaign and unlocking Mythic Bosses**. Same limited seasonal window. Has a 20% chance to drop the **"Pumpmourne"** appearance.

## Tips

- This chapter is **time-limited** — only playable during the Halloween event window each year.
- Expect trial and error on the carving face; that's intended per the guide ("try all carvings until it works").
- Come back for the **Mythic** fight only after clearing Void's Invasion and unlocking Mythic Bosses.

## Anticipated questions

- **Why can't I access this chapter?** It's a seasonal Halloween event (roughly Oct 20 – mid-Nov); outside that window it's unavailable.
- **How do I summon Sir Scar Crow?** Build the guide's structure and place carved Jack o' Lanterns on it, trying different carvings until he appears.
- **What's the reward?** His weapon, The Scarred Night; the Mythic version can also drop the "Pumpmourne" appearance (20%).
- **What does the Mythic version require?** Finishing Void's Invasion and unlocking Mythic Bosses, during the event window.
