# A Jolly Christmas!

A **seasonal holiday event** chapter. It's short and reward-focused: defeat campaign bosses during the event to claim themed **Christmas Presents**, capped by a special reward. There's no new mechanic to learn — it re-uses existing [[Quest — Main Story|Void's Invasion]] bosses as the "unlock" conditions. See [[Creatures, Bosses & Mobs]].

## Walkthrough (present unlocks)

- **Christmas Present — Beginners Hope / First Bossfight** — Claimed by defeating **one of the first two bosses**.
- **Christmas Present — Merry Magus** — Defeat **Magus** to claim (drops an Ominous Eye reference item as flavor).
- **Christmas Present — Gu'mas 'n' Gingerbread** — Defeat **Gu'mas** to claim.
- **Christmas Present — Under the Tentacle Twig** — Defeat **Conjunctivius** to claim.
- **Christmas Molten Star** — A capstone reward ("You earned this, bud!").
- **Merry Christmas!** — Closing note: the Prominence Team thanks you for playing and wishes you a Merry Christmas.

## Tips

- This is a **time-limited holiday event** — the presents are a seasonal bonus layered on top of bosses you may already be fighting for the main campaign.
- Since the unlocks are tied to existing campaign/side bosses (Magus, Gu'mas, Conjunctivius), progress here comes naturally as you advance the story during the event window.
- Watch for a nut-allergy joke — the rewards are gifts, treat them as flavor loot.
