# Marium's Soulslike Weaponry

**[[Marium's Soulslike Weaponry]]** ("Soul Weaponry") is a boss-and-gear chapter built on soul materials, a chain of summonable bosses, and an arsenal of themed weapons and firearms. It all starts with a single soul sand. See [[Core RPG & Combat Mods]].

## Material base

1. **Lunar Source** — mine **Moonstone** and **Verglas** in the deepslate layers (uncommon). These are core to soul equipment.
2. **Soul Crafting** — smelt **Soul Sand** into **Lost Souls**, then craft **Soul Infused Ingots** — the main soul-equipment ingredient.
3. **Building blocks** — Infused Blackstone, Soulfire Stain, Soul Lamp (decorative, seen in the bosses' structures).

## Boss progression (Lord Souls)

Craft a **Boss Compass** to locate them. Each boss frees/kills a fallen figure and yields a **Lord Soul** used to awaken gear:
- **Champion's Grave** — the champion boss.
- **Accursed Lord** ("Fall of a Kingdom") → Lord Soul Red + Withered Demon Heart.
- **Draugr Boss** ("Fallen Puppet") → Lord Soul Dark + Essence of Eventide.
- **Returning Knight / Moonknight** ("Retribution") → Lord Soul White + Essence of Luminescence, via the Altar Block.
- **The Wither** ("The Abyss Stares Back") → Lord Soul Void + a chaotic shard.
- **Ender Dragon** ("Essence of the Dragon") → Lord Soul Purple.
- **Chaos Monarch** — summoned from the Blackstone Pedestal with the shard; drops the **Chaos Crown** and **Chaos Robes**.

## Weapons & gear

- **Moonlight swords** — craft **Bluemoon** blades ("Imitation Moon"), then insert a powerful soul to awaken them into **Moonlight Shortsword/Greatsword**.
- **Firearms** (use **Silver Bullets**): **Hunter Pistol** (quick reload), **Blunderbuss** (shotgun blast), **Hunter Cannon** (10 bullets → one cannonball, long reload), **Gatling Gun** (rapid fire, eats bullets).
- **Signature weapons**: **Soul Reaper** (summon the dead), **Holy Moonlight Sword** (transforms between forms), **Lich Bane** (true magic damage above ⅓ target HP), **Comet Spear**, **Galeforce** bow, **Bloodthirster** (lifesteal), **Darkin Blade / Darkin Scythe** (evolves from red/blue souls), **Leviathan Axe** (freeze + ice explosion), **Withered Wabbajack** (random gamble effects), **Mjolnir** (lightning), **Freyr Sword** (fights on its own).
- **Armor sets**: **Soul Robes** (dark sorcerer set), **Soul Ingot armor** (durable), and **Forlorn** armor (drops only from Evil Forlorns in the Soul Sand Valley).
- **Moonstone tools** (stronger than diamond) and the **Moonstone Ring** (launch magical waves).
- Novelty: kill Chunguses for **Chungus Emeralds** (9 → the Big Chungus music disc).

## Anticipated questions
- **Where do I start?** Smelt soul sand into Lost Souls and mine Moonstone/Verglas in the deepslate layers.
- **How do I awaken the moonlight swords?** Craft the Bluemoon versions, then insert a powerful soul (a boss's Lord Soul).
- **How do I find the bosses?** Craft the **Boss Compass**.
- **Where's Forlorn armor?** Only from killing **Evil Forlorns** in the Soul Sand Valley.
