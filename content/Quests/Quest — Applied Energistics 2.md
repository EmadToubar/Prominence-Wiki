# Applied Energistics 2

This chapter walks you through the basics of **[[Applied Energistics 2]]** — the endgame digital storage-and-autocrafting system covered in [[Tech & Automation Mods]]. By the end you should have basic AE power generation and a working ME storage system started. It assumes you can mine Sky Stone (Iron Pickaxe or better) and have a little early-game power on hand (even a Wooden Crank works to start).

## Walkthrough

The questline is roughly ordered as: **charge crystals → find meteorites → make processors → build a network → set up storage.**

1. **Preparing to Find Meteorites** — Craft a **Charger** and use it to make a **Meteorite Compass** from a normal compass. The compass can be charged very early with a Wooden Crank. It points toward the nearest Meteorite within ~2,700 blocks: spinning rapidly means Sky Stone is in your chunk, spinning slowly means you're out of range and should explore more.
2. **Your First Meteorite** — Bring an Iron Pickaxe or better (Sky Stone is hard). Mine to the **center** to collect Certus Quartz Crystals, Certus Quartz Blocks, and the **Inscriber Presses** from the Sky Chest — the presses are the whole reason you came.
3. **Charge Certus & make Fluix** — Toss Certus Quartz Crystals into the Charger to make **Charged Certus Quartz**. Craft **Fluix Crystal** by dropping Charged Certus, Nether Quartz, and Redstone Dust into water.
4. **Processors** — Use the **Inscriber** (each op needs 1k AE charged) with the presses to make Calculation, Logic, and Engineering Processors. **Silicon** comes from smelting Certus Quartz Dust.
5. **Power the network** — Options in the chapter: the **Vibration Chamber** (1–10 AE/t from solid fuel), the **Energy Acceptor** (converts external power, 2 FE = 1 AE), and the novelty **Crystal Resonance Chamber**. Add **Energy Cells** (200,000 AE) and **Dense Energy Cells** (1.6M AE) for buffer storage.
6. **Storage & access** — Build **Storage Cells** (items, fluids, or spatial), an **ME Chest** for direct access, and eventually an **ME Controller** (the routing hub — without it only 8 devices work, and you can only have one per network). A **Drive Bay** holds 10 cells in a tiny footprint. **Portable Cells** work like a backpack early-game.

## Notable rewards & tips
- The **Crystal Growth Accelerator** (consumes 8 AE/t) massively speeds growing Certus/Fluix crystals from seeds in adjacent water — build one early to escape the crafting bottleneck.
- **Fluix ME Glass Cable** is the cheapest cable, carries power + up to 8 channels, and dyes to any of 16 colors.
- Grab **all** the Inscriber Presses on your first meteorite trip; they're not consumed and you never want to hunt for a second one.

## Anticipated questions
- **Do I need AE2 to beat the pack?** No — it's a parallel long-term goal. Treat it as the answer to "how do I stop drowning in chests."
- **Why won't my network work past 8 devices?** You need an **ME Controller**; without one the network caps at 8 interacting devices.
- **Where do processors come from?** Only the **Inscriber** + presses, and presses only come from meteorite Sky Chests.
- **Exact recipes/costs?** Check **JEI/EMI** in-game — this guide is grounded in the quest text, which describes function more than exact recipes.
