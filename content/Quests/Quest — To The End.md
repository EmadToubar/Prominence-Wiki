# To The End

The dimension chapter for the End, heavily expanded by Better End and Eldritch End. Reaching it is a mini-quest of its own — vanilla eyes of ender don't work here. Once inside, it's a huge exploration checklist of biomes, structures, ores, and mobs, plus the **Obsidilith** boss (also a [[Quest — Main Story|Void's Invasion]] herald). Many of the Void campaign's late dimensions (Primordial Abyss, Hasturian Wastes) are reached from here. Content is from [[Dimensions & Space Travel Mods]] and [[Exploration, Dungeons & Structures Mods]].

## Walkthrough

- **Getting to the End / Entering The End** — You need **12 (at most) of the 16 unique eyes** — normal Eyes of Ender do **not** work. Specific eyes are found in structures or **traded** for at the chapter's Eye Trading node (no guarantee of the one you want; trades restock after a day). Enter the portal with twelve unique eyes.
- **Free the End! (Obsidilith / the Dragon)** — "Kill the Dragon and free the End of its reign." **Obsidilith** (creature of rock and hate) is summoned at its Arena via an **Obsidian Heart**. (The corrupted Ender Dragon "Jean" is fought via Main Story.)
- **Biome tour** — Long list of "Visit" quests: Blossoming/Blossoming Spires, **Hasturian Wastes**, Dark Amaranth Forests, **Shadow Forest** (bring light; kill Shadow Walkers), Umbrella Jungle, **Primordial Abyss** (aberration heart region), Crystal Mountains (Aurora Crystals), **Chorus Forest** (farm Chorus for **Galena**), Ice Starfield (Ancient Emerald Ice), Amber Lands, Sulphur Springs, Lantern Woods, End Highlands, Glowing Grasslands, Megalake Grove, Umbra Valley, Painted Mountains, and more.
- **Structures** — **End City** (top priority — best loot), End Gate Fortress, **Shipwrecks** (End-City-tier loot), **End Village** (ender pearls, smithing templates, ingots), **Eternal Portal** (restore it with six **Eternal Crystals** placed on surrounding pedestals to earn the **Eternal Light**), Heavenly Rider / **Heavenly Challenger**, Mega Ship, Phantom Citadel.
- **Mob quests** — End Slimes, **Aberrations** (stack corruption), **Dendlers**, Endermen (→ **Mutant Enderman**), **Shulkers**, Phantoms.
- **Materials to collect** — **Galena Chunks** (highest chance of a **Mythic** roll when reforging — you'll want a ton; see [[Gear Rarity & Reforging]]), Chorus Fruit, Purpur, Aurora Crystals, Tenanea / Helix / Lucernia / Umbrella / Dark Amaranth woods, Umbralith, Lumecorn, Ancient Emerald Ice.
- **Rewards & gear** — **Tier 4 Trinkets** (kill a Shulker / get any Tier IV trinket), **End's Delights** cooking (knife-kill End mobs for powerful foods), **Icarus Wings** (Elytra-like, go in the cape slot, no durability/fireworks; Nether Star variant costs no hunger — best with no armor), and the **Explorer's Compass** (costs Trophies but makes finding structures trivial).

## Tips

- **Stockpile Galena** in the Chorus/Chorus Forest areas — it's the top reforging material for pushing gear to Mythic.
- Make **End Cities your first priority** for gear and templates.
- Buy the **Explorer's Compass** early in the chapter; it saves hours of hunting the biomes/structures above.
- In the Shadow Forest, **bring light** — something follows you there.

## Anticipated questions

- **Why won't my eyes of ender open the portal?** You need the 12 *unique* eyes (of 16), found in structures or traded at the Eye Trading node — normal eyes don't count.
- **What's Galena for?** It has the highest chance of a Mythic reforge roll — farm it in the Chorus Forest.
- **What should I look for first in the End?** End Cities (best loot) and an Explorer's Compass to speed up everything else.
- **Are Icarus Wings better than an Elytra?** They have no durability, use the cape slot, and don't need fireworks — but more armor weighs them down; the Nether Star version also costs no hunger.
