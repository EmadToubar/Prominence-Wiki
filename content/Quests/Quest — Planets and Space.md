# Planets & Space — The Astral Realm

The space-progression chapter, built on **Ad Astra** (+ Giselle addon) and tied tightly to tech mods. It's a long-term parallel goal: build steel and machines, craft rockets tier by tier, and travel from the Moon out to other solar systems. Expect heavy overlap with [[Tech & Automation Mods]] — Ad Astra machines run on power from any tech mod. See also [[Dimensions & Space Travel Mods]].

## Walkthrough (roughly in order)

- **Materials base** — Craft **Steel Plates/Rods** (used in nearly every recipe), **Iron Plates/Rods**, and **Gas Tanks**. Obtain Steel by smelting Iron in a **Blast Furnace**; compress iron/steel via the **Tech Reborn Compressor** (overclockable).
- **Power** — Start with a **Coal Generator**. Ad Astra machines accept power from any mod; the chapter recommends **Tech Reborn Glass Fiber Cables**.
- **Life support** — Build an **Oxygen Loader** to fill **Oxygen Tanks/Cans** (craft 5 and fill them), and craft a **Space Suit** (later upgradeable with Jet Propulsion). Make a couple of **Fans**.
- **Fuel** — Find an **Oil Well**, build a **Fuel Refinery**, and add a **Fuel Loader** to load rockets automatically. Later, the **Cryo Freezer** makes **Cryo Fuel** (far more efficient — one bucket launches a rocket).
- **Rockets (Nasa Workbench)** — All rockets share the same recipe layout; only the material tier changes. Each needs **4 Fins** and a **Steel Engine** (built from **Engine Frames**).
  - **Tier 1** → the Moon / low orbit (Space Stations).
  - **Tier 2** → **Mars** (needs **Desh**, found only on the Moon).
  - **Tier 3** → **Mercury** and **Venus** (needs **Ostrum**).
  - **Tier 4** → other solar systems / **Glacio** (needs **Calorite**).
- **The Moon** — Visit it; kill **Star Crawlers** and **Corrupted Lunarians** (pick them off from range), find **Cheese**, Lunar Towers, Lunarian Villages, and Moon Dungeons. Mine **Desh**.
- **Mars** — Ice Caps ("water on Mars?"), Canyon Creek, **Martian Raptors**, and the **Mars Temple**. Mine **Ostrum**.
- **Mercury & Venus** — Venus holds riches; Mercury is mostly uneventful (kill Magma Cubes). Mine **Calorite**.
- **Glacio (Tier 4 system)** — Kill **Pygros**, **Moglers**, and **Glacian Rams** (milk them), collect **Ice Shards**; visit Pygro Towers/Villages. Unique sun.
- **Space Stations** — Buildable in any orbit; bring enough **Steel / Ostrum / Desh** (amount varies by planet). Water freezes there. A **Zip Gun** (fill with oxygen) propels you in low-gravity orbit.
- **Infinite oxygen setup** — **Oxygen Distributor** kept running by a **Water Pump** over an infinite water source, both supplied with power.
- **Shortcut quest** — You can also just **craft a Waystone** ("No rockets needed!") for return travel, and craft a **Flag** to plant on the Moon.

## Tips

- This chapter is really a **tech chapter** — get Steel, power, and the Tech Reborn compressor going before chasing rockets.
- Each rocket tier is gated by a planet-specific metal: **Desh (Moon) → Ostrum (Mars) → Calorite (Venus/Mercury)**. Plan mining trips accordingly.
- **Cryo Fuel** dramatically cuts fuel needs — build the Cryo Freezer once you can.
- Bring a **Waystone** to any planet/station so you can hop home without a return launch.

## Anticipated questions

- **Do Ad Astra machines need Ad Astra power?** No — they run on power from any tech mod; Tech Reborn Glass Fiber Cables are recommended.
- **What gates each rocket tier?** Planet-specific metals: Desh (Moon) for Tier 2, Ostrum (Mars) for Tier 3, Calorite for Tier 4.
- **How do I breathe off-world?** Space Suit + filled Oxygen Cans/Tanks (via an Oxygen Loader), or an Oxygen Distributor + Water Pump for a permanent base supply.
- **Easiest way home?** Craft a Waystone and bring it with you — no return rocket needed.
