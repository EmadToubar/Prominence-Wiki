# New Mechanics

The big **tutorial and build-guide chapter** ("How does Combat & Gearing Up work?"). It's mostly *reading* — the chapter itself says to read carefully, because this is the information you need to understand combat and build your character. Although it's an early-game tutorial, it extends all the way to end-game builds, and more quests appear as you progress. Treat it as the pack's manual for [[Combat & Stats]], [[Damage Types & Runes]], and [[Archetypes & Builds]].

## Walkthrough

### Changed combat & defense
- **New Combat Mechanics (Better Combat)** — Each weapon has its own attack animation/time, consecutive attacks form **combos**, and you can **hold** the attack button to keep swinging.
- **New Defensive Mechanics (Shield Overhaul)** — Shields gain new behavior; a **shield bash** while blocking stuns targets for 1 second (bosses are immune to the stun).

### Understanding stats (read these in order)
The chapter explains stats as the numbers everything scales from. Grounded highlights:
- **Core Stats** — used by everyone (e.g. Health by default, Haste/Armor from gear).
- **Physical Damage** — main melee stat. **Haste** — physical attack speed (Rogue focus). **Armor** reduces physical damage, **Toughness** is the "anti one-shot" stat effective on big hits. **Health** = max damage before death; **Stamina** reduces passive Energy/hunger loss.
- **Powers by tier** — **Elemental** (tertiary): Arcane, Fire, Frost (obtain each rune); Lightning for some artifacts/Valkyries. **Primordial** (secondary): Holy (Priests/Paladins), Soul (hybrids like Soulfire/Soulfrost). **Decaying**: Blood (Blood DK), Unholy (Unholy DK).
- **Hybrid stats** — **Lethality / Magic Penetration** (same stat, ignore a % of Armor/Magic Protection), **Astral Power** (Arcane+Fire+Frost combined, used by Astral Mages/Spellblades), Physical↔Magic conversion stats, **Ranged Damage & Draw Speed**, **Spell Critical & Spell Haste**, **Movement Speed**.
- **Enchantments** — craft an Enchanting Table; enchants heavily boost gear (full guide on the wiki). See [[Accessories & Enchanting]].
- **Eternal Knowledge: Of Combat & Gear** — completing the reading grants a "Piece of Eternal Knowledge."

### Build guides (tiered, Tier I → IV)
The chapter provides gear-direction guides per playstyle. They are *guides on what to look for*, not fully fleshed builds — check the wiki for complete builds. Difficulty reflects both play and crafting difficulty. Grounded starting points:
- **Protector / Tank** — prioritize Max Health & Armor; Diamond → Netherite → Netherite-Diamond → **Sapphire** armor (+20 Max Health/piece, reflect passive). Fate: **S'kellak's Blessing**. Artifacts: Vaazkar (tank), Fyr'alath (bruiser), Frostmourne (DK sustain).
- **Warrior** — slow but hard-hitting burst melee; **Titan's Shroud** (highest Physical Damage), Fate **Warrior's Devotion** with the **Thunderwrath** artifact (or Hero's Armor for Lightning Power).
- **Rogue (DPS)** — Haste then Physical Damage; fast one-handed weapons to dual-wield. Assassin → **Souldancer** armor; Fate **Shadow Techniques**. Monk alt uses fist weapons (Fury of a Thousand Fists).
- **Mage** — pick an element (Arcane burst / Fire high crit / Frost utility); Wizard Staff + Robes → Magus Set → **Ekavar** (great staff). Astral path via Runic Invocations and **Knowledge of Runes and Stars** fate.
- **Ranger** — Ranged Damage (+ Draw Speed for fire rate); Ranger Armor, shortbows/rapid crossbows (DPS) or longbows/heavy crossbows (burst); Mutant Skeleton chestplate upgrade; artifact **Orion**.
- **Spellblade** — hybrid Rogue+Mage; blade of chosen element + Techniques spellbook; artifacts A'zhar/Ash'edar/Frostmourne/Supernova.
- **Healer** — Holy Power + Holy Book; some defense. **Paladin** path upgrades to the **Apophis** artifact (oath of Protection or Healing).
- **Bard** — no fixed gear; requires the **A Bard's Tale** fated talent and instruments (Immersive Melodies) to buff a party (`/openpac-parties`).
- **Death Knight** — Runeblades give all three DK powers. **Frost** (DPS, Frozen Champion set), **Unholy** (burst/AoE, Plaguebringer set), **Blood** (tank, under the Protector path). Frostmourne is the standout DK artifact.
- **Mariums Guns (sidegrade)** — Hunter Cannon (highest single-target), Blunderbuss (3 projectiles), Pistol (fast fire).

## Tips

- Read, don't skim — the chapter literally gates "Eternal Knowledge" behind reading it.
- These are *direction* guides. Combine with [[Archetypes & Builds]] and the wiki for full builds.

## Anticipated questions

- **What's the difference between Armor and Toughness?** Armor reduces physical damage flatly; Toughness scales its reduction on big hits — the "anti one-shot" stat.
- **What is Astral Power?** The combination of Arcane + Fire + Frost, used by Astral Mages/Spellblades and books like Runic Invocations.
- **Lethality vs Magic Penetration?** Same stat, different names — both ignore a percentage of the target's Armor / Magic Protection.
- **Are these complete builds?** No — they're guides on what gear/stats to look for. Full builds live on rpg.prominence.wiki.
