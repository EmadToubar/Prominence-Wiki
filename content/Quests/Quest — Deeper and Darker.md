# Deeper and Darker — The Otherside

A compact dimension chapter built around the **Deeper and Darker** mod. It takes you from the Deep Dark and the Warden into a new dimension, **The Otherside**, with its own sculk-themed mobs and drops. It's an optional exploration track you can pursue once you're comfortable in the Deep Dark. See [[Dimensions & Space Travel Mods]] and [[Creatures, Bosses & Mobs]].

## Walkthrough

- **Kill a Warden** — Found underground in the Overworld's Deep Dark. Drops the **Heart of the Deep**, your key to the portal.
- **Enter The Otherside** — Before lighting the portal, **remove all Sculk Veins from the inner frame**, then right-click the cleared frame with the **Heart of the Deep** from the Warden.
- **Otherside mobs** (kill quests, each with a themed drop):
  - **Shattered** → Soul Dust.
  - **Stalker** → Soul Crystal. Rare chance to spawn after breaking an **Ancient Vase**.
  - **Shriek Worm** → Sculk Bone. Appears by stepping on **Infested Sculk**.
- **Find a Sculk Snapper** — Can be **tamed with enchanted Netherite Chestplates**; once tamed they occasionally drop enchanted books.

## Tips

- The Warden is a serious fight — come geared and consider ranged/hit-and-run tactics before attempting the Heart of the Deep.
- Don't try to light the portal until every **Sculk Vein** is cleared from the inner frame, or it won't activate.
- Keep spare **enchanted Netherite Chestplates** if you want to tame Sculk Snappers for passive enchanted-book income.

## Anticipated questions

- **How do I open the Otherside portal?** Kill a Warden for the Heart of the Deep, clear all Sculk Veins from the inner frame, then right-click the frame with the Heart.
- **How do I get a Stalker to fight?** Break Ancient Vases — Stalkers have a rare chance to spawn from them.
- **Can I tame anything here?** Yes — Sculk Snappers are tamed with enchanted Netherite Chestplates and then drop enchanted books.
