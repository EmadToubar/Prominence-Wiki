# Industrial Revolution

**[[Industrial Revolution]]** is another full industrial tech tree — tools, weapons, machines, and ways of generating resources. See [[Tech & Automation Mods]].

> [!warning] Industrial Revolution is being removed from Prominence
> The chapter itself announces that **Indrev is leaving the pack**, phased out gradually so players can migrate to **[[Tech Reborn]]** and other tech mods. Reasons given in-quest: the mod was abandoned by its developer, cables don't transfer at their advertised speed, and machines break unbreakable items / kill things they shouldn't. **Phase one** makes all Indrev **cables uncraftable** and lets you convert them to Tech Reborn cables at any time; other features are unaffected for now. If you're starting fresh, prefer [[Tech Reborn]] instead.

## Walkthrough (if you still use it)

1. **Nikolite line** — mine for **Nikolite Dust** (a turquoise dust with useful machine properties). Infuse iron with it for **Nikolite-enhanced ingots**, and combine with Diamond Dust for **Enriched Nikolite**. The **Solid Infuser** is the only machine that can infuse Nikolite — central to the whole tree.
2. **Machine tiers & tools** — Machine blocks come in tiers MK1–MK4; **Circuit** upgrades (MK2/3/4) speed processing and allow more **enhancers**. Use the **Screwdriver** to configure machine input/output sides and the energy cube.
3. **Fluid/Item pipes** — need a **Retriever Servo** (pulls into the machine it points at) or an Output Servo to move contents.
4. **Automation machines** — **Sawmill** (more planks + sawdust), **Chopper** (auto plant + fell trees, needs an axe), **Slaughter** (kills mobs with a supplied sword), **Rancher** (auto-breed and kill animals). Each has a top-tier variant.
5. **Mining Rig** — generate ores using RF: a **Mining Rig Computer** + 1–8 **Mining Rig Drills** + Drill Heads + an **Encoded Ore Data Card** (encode 64 of an ore in a Data Card Encoder; add Enriched Nikolite, 16 per 1%, to raise richness/yield).
6. **Modular armor** — Helmet/Chestplate/Leggings/Boots that accept **modules** (e.g. Color Modules). The **Core of Modularity** is charged in a **Capsule** using **Laser Emitter(s)** placed 3 blocks away; power with LF and pulse redstone. Warning: pulsing an emitter at an empty capsule destroys the capsule, and pulsing one not facing a capsule makes it explode.

## Notable rewards & tips
- **Enhancers** boost machines; the base enhancer adds +25k LF capacity per upgrade.
- The chapter openly flags one weapon as a "meme weapon — please don't use it unironically, it's bad."

## Anticipated questions
- **Should I invest in Industrial Revolution?** Not for the long term — it's being removed. Convert cables to Tech Reborn and build your factory there.
- **My Indrev cables won't craft.** Intended — phase one made them uncraftable; convert existing ones to Tech Reborn cables.
- **What replaces it?** [[Tech Reborn]] is the pack's recommended alternative, with [[Modern Industrialization]] and [[Create]] as other options.
