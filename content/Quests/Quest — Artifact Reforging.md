# Artifact Reforging

This chapter covers the pack's **soulbound artifact weapons** — forged at S'kellak's Forge from the **Molten Core**, each with a unique ability and its own **Upgrade Tree**. It's endgame content: it **requires completion of Chapter I of Void's Invasion** (the main story). See [[Gear Rarity & Reforging]] and the [[simply_swords|Simply Swords]] chapter (whose weapons feed several artifacts).

## Getting started — S'kellak's Forge

- The **Molten Core** is forged from the depths of Cinderstone's volcano, **Vaaz**. With it you craft artifact weapons that are **bound to your soul** — only the forger can use them.
- Make a **Hardening Catalyst / Hardening Crystal** to make artifacts unbreakable, and use **Encased Remnants** to repair/reforge them.

There's also a **molten shield** (symbol of the Living Statue of Vaaz).

## Upgrade Trees

- Forging an artifact unlocks its **Upgrade Tree** — press **N** and click the **Molten Core** icon (top-left).
- Each tree has up to **7 nodes** with branching paths. Unlock nodes by empowering the artifact with **Molten Remnants** (quest rewards, or craftable from **Mythical Essences**).
- **Mastery** — beat **10 Mythical Challenges** with an artifact to complete its Mastery (an Eternal Challenge per weapon).
- **Made a mistake in the skill tree?** You can reset **once for free**: `/puffish_skills skills reset @s puffish_skills:frostmourne` (swap the artifact id).

## Endgame reward
Forging **all** artifact weapons and unlocking their Mastery grants the "Piece of Eternal Knowledge" (Eternal Knowledge: Of the Eternal Forge).

## Anticipated questions
- **Why can't anyone else use my artifact?** They're **soulbound** to whoever forged them.
- **How do I make them unbreakable?** A **Hardening Catalyst/Crystal**; repair/reforge with **Encased Remnants**.
- **Where do Molten Remnants come from?** Quest/campaign rewards, or craft from **Mythical Essences**, to unlock Upgrade Tree nodes.
- **I picked the wrong node.** Use the free one-time reset command shown above.
