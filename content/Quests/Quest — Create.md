# Create

This chapter introduces **[[Create]]**, the showpiece kinetic-automation mod from [[Tech & Automation Mods]]. As the intro itself notes, Create has excellent built-in guides: hover any item in REI/your inventory and hold **W** to "**ponder**" it for an animated explanation. Because of that, the chapter is mostly a **map of what exists** and a rough progression outline rather than step-by-step recipes.

## Progression outline

**1. Generate kinetic power (SU).** Start with the simplest sources and scale up:
- **Hand Crank** — you are the power source; simplest possible.
- **Water Wheel** — same SU at the same RPM as long as one block of *flowing* water is adjacent (still pools don't work). Right-click with any wood plank to reskin it.
- **Large Water Wheel** — twice the SU at slower RPM.
- **Steam Engine** — the densest source, but most complex: needs a steady water + heat supply.

**2. Read your network.** Use the **Goggles** (essential — shows stress impact and block info), plus the **Stressometer** and **Speedometer**.

**3. Brass & processing.** Heat a **Mechanical Mixer** over a **Blaze Burner** (fill an empty burner by right-clicking a blaze or blaze spawner) to mix copper + zinc into brass. The mixer autocrafts shapeless recipes and mixes potions, and needs ≥30 RPM to run.

**4. Core machines to build toward:**
- **Encased Fan** — extremely versatile: wash, haunt, blast, and smoke items. Add **Nozzle** for spherical AoE; front-mounted casings (Haunting/Blasting/Smoking/Splashing) set the mode. Notably sand→soul sand (haunt) and soul sand→nether quartz (wash).
- **Mechanical Press** — bonks ingots into plates.
- **Millstone / Crushing Wheels** — grind to dust (keep both around for different recipes).
- **Mechanical Drill / Saw / Harvester / Plough** — for contraption tools, tunnel bores, and farms.
- **Mechanical Crafter** & **Mechanical Arm** — automatic and oversized crafting, plus advanced item distribution.

**5. Contraptions.** **Super Glue** binds blocks; then **Mechanical Pistons / Bearings / Gantries / Cart Assemblers** move them linearly, rotationally, or along rails. **Rope Pulley** and the fancier **Elevator Pulley** handle vertical movement.

**6. Trains (endgame).** Minimum: 1 **Train Casing**, 1 **Train Station**, and enough **Train Tracks**. Ponder the Train Station to learn assembly. Add **Train Signals**, **Track Switches**, **Schedules**, and a **Display Board** for a real rail network.

## Notable rewards & tips
- The chapter's completion reward is flagged as an **Eternal Challenge** ("One Cookie to Rule Them All").
- **Create: Crafts & Additions** content appears here too: **Electric Motor** (energy→kinetic), **Alternator** (kinetic→energy, caps at 256 E/t), **Rolling Mill**, **Tesla Coil**, and the **Modular Accumulator** — this is your bridge between Create and [[Powah!|electrical]] tech.
- **Infinite fluids:** a Hose Pulley extracts infinitely from any pool of ≥10,000 source blocks (search `$create:bottomless` in REI).
- **Diving kit:** Copper Backtank + Diving Helmet/Boots to walk and breathe underwater.

## Anticipated questions
- **How do I learn a specific block?** Hold **W** to Ponder it — the mod's own tutorial is better than any recipe list.
- **My water wheel won't spin.** It needs genuinely *flowing* water adjacent, not a still source pool.
- **Do I need the Goggles?** Effectively yes — they show stress capacity/impact, which you need to avoid overstressing your network.
