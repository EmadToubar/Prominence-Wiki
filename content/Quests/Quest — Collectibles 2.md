# Collectibles 2

**A Collector's Journey** — the second collection chapter, this one focused on "trinkets and accessories… gathered and admired." Where [[Quest — Collectibles]] is about fish and crops, this chapter is about **Artifact trinkets** (the "Things"), **music discs**, and **mob heads**. It's optional side content: you find and hoard the items as you explore, fight, and dig, ticking off the display sets. See [[Quality-of-Life & UI Mods]] and [[FTB Quests]].

## Walkthrough

1. **Get Hoardin'.** Open the intro quest ("Welcome, young collector…") to start the collection lines.
2. **Collect the "Things" (Trinket Artifacts).** "You must find the Things and collect them for your own benefit… just refer to them as a Thing, nothing more, nothing less." These are a large spread of wearable Artifact trinkets, each with a flavor hint in the quest, for example:
   - **Umbrella** — "strong enough to be a shield and indestructible."
   - **Dimensional Bag** — "basically an ender chest that you can carry around."
   - **Owl's Glasses** — a monocle that "enhances the wearer's vision to even work at night as if they're in the day."
   - **Displacement Tome ("A Book of Teleportation")** — charge it with pearls and save a location to teleport to; it needs crafted **displacement pages** to write down destinations.
   - **Portable Ghast** scepter — shoot fireballs like a Ghast, "but remember to carry ammunition."
   - **Crystallized Hades Fire** — makes the wearer "immune to fire and even lava damage."
   - **Running Shoes** — "press B to run," which makes your sprint faster.
   - **Bedrock Tough** — makes an item indestructible ("don't let it touch cacti though").
   - **Light as a Rabbit**, magnets that "pull in any item," fall-damage-reducing boots, a mining glove, the **Riot Gauntlet**, and the joke-tier **Whoopee Cushion** ("the most legendary artifact").
   - Utility items round it out: the **Recall Potion / "Milk Tea"** (teleports you to your spawnpoint — "a good way to escape or go home quickly"), a **chest key** for locking/unlocking chests, and an inside-out "flipped" bucket that acts as an infinite water source but can't pick anything back up.
   - A **Gleaming Compound** ("Shiny Red Stone") is a rare material used to craft the more powerful Things.
3. **Find the Discs.** "These records are almost lost to time… find them and listen to what they have to offer." Two sets: **Minecraft Discs** (the classic C418 tracks plus Lena Raine's Otherside/Pigstep and other vanilla records) and **Modded Discs** (harder-to-find tracks, including a large block of **Prominence OST** records by SideralisMusic and others).
4. **Find the Mob Heads.** "Finding cool remains is worth showing for display… once you find them and use the brush to dig them out, give your respect." **Get a Brush** first, then recover the mob heads for display.
5. Grab the **"Know Your Thing"** guide item if you want an in-game reference for where to gather the Things.

## Tips
- Most of this chapter is passive completion — trinkets drop, discs are found, and heads are brushed up as you play other chapters, so treat it as a long-running checklist.
- The Displacement Tome and Recall Potion are genuinely useful travel tools, not just display pieces — the Tome saves named locations (needs displacement pages + pearls), while the Recall Potion is a one-shot trip to your spawnpoint.
- Save **Gleaming Compound** — it's flagged as rare and is what the stronger Things are crafted from.
- Mob heads require a **brush** (archaeology-style), so get one before hunting remains.

## Anticipated questions
- **What is this chapter collecting?** Trinket "Artifacts" (the "Things"), music discs, and mob heads — a display/hoarding chapter rather than a progression one.
- **What are the "Things"?** Wearable Artifact trinkets, each with a joke-y hint in the quest — ranging from useful (Dimensional Bag, Owl's Glasses, Crystallized Hades Fire, Displacement Tome) to silly (the Whoopee Cushion).
- **How do I get mob heads for the collection?** Find the remains and dig them out with a **brush**, then the head is yours to display.
- **What discs are there?** Two sets — classic Minecraft records and modded ones, the latter including a big batch of Prominence's own original soundtrack discs.
- **What's the Gleaming Compound for?** It's a rare material used to craft the more powerful Things.
- **Is this required for the main story?** No — it's optional collection/display content you fill in as you play.
