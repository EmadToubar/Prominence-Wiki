# Simply Swords

**[[Simply Swords]]** adds 15+ weapon types plus a **Runic Powers** system (powers slotted into weapon **sockets**) and a large roster of **Unique** weapons. This chapter is mostly about discovering uniques and learning the socket/runic workflow. See [[Core RPG & Combat Mods]].

## Runic Powers workflow

1. **Sockets** — Uniques and runic gear have sockets that hold **Runic Powers**.
2. **Empowered Remnant** — created by melting down a unique weapon. It's the key ingredient for the two runic gems (flagged in-quest as "a pain to make").
3. **Runefused Gem** — combine a **Runic Tablet** + an **Empowered Remnant** in a **Smithing Table**, then click to identify the power you rolled.
4. **Netherfused Gem** — combine an **Empowered Remnant** + a **Netherite Ingot** in a Smithing Table.
5. **Runic Gear** — to make socketed runic gear you need a **Runic Tablet** + a **Netherite weapon**; also keep a **Contained Remnant** on you while exploring.

## Collecting Uniques

Unique weapons drop from strong enemies. The chapter's "old book" gives approximate drop chances:

| Source | Chance |
|---|---|
| Obsidilith | 40% |
| Ender Dragon | 35% |
| Night Lich | 30% |
| Nether Gauntlet | 25% |
| Conjunctivius | 15% |
| Decaying King | 15% |

The Wither and the Ender Dragon are specifically called out as possible sources, and other strong enemies may still carry one.

## Notable weapons & rewards
- **Relic tiers** — the questline funnels toward powerful relics: **Dormant → Righteous / Tainted**, plus **Sunfire** (pure light), **Harbinger** (death), and the **Brimstone Claymore** ("to slay a Dragon").
- **Lichblade line** — a multi-stage weapon: **Slumbering Lichblade → Waking Lichblade → Awakened Lichblade** (fed by kills until it reaches full potential). The **Awakened Lichblade** is a component for the [[artifact_reforging|Frostmourne artifact]].
- **Molten Edge** — a lava blade used to forge artifact weapons in the [[artifact_reforging|Artifact Reforging]] chapter.
- Dozens of flavor-rich uniques (Storm's Edge, Stormbringer, Soulrender, Soulpyre, Frostfall, Mjolnir, Thunderbrand, Caelestis, etc.), each with distinct abilities — check tooltips/EMI for exact stats.

## Anticipated questions
- **How do I get my first Unique?** Kill strong bosses — Obsidilith (40%) and the Ender Dragon (35%) are the best odds. You may already have one from a boss you've fought.
- **What do sockets do?** They hold **Runic Powers** rolled via Runefused/Netherfused Gems for extra weapon effects.
- **Why melt a unique?** To get an **Empowered Remnant**, the base for both runic gems.
- **How do I "wake" the Lichblade?** Keep killing with it — it progresses Slumbering → Waking → Awakened toward full potential.
