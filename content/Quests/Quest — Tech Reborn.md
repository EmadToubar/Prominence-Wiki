# Tech Reborn

**[[Tech Reborn]]** is a complete, self-contained classic tech tree (power generation, power storage, bulk item storage, tools, and making materials from energy + scrap). See [[Tech & Automation Mods]] for how it sits alongside the pack's other tech mods. Note: Prominence is **migrating Industrial Revolution players onto Tech Reborn** (see the [[industrial_revolution|Industrial Revolution]] chapter), so this is the recommended "greener pasture" of the older tech trees.

## Early game — basic components
- **Rubber** — right-click a **Tree Tap** on the orange spots of a rubber tree to extract sap, then smelt into rubber. Later, combine a **Treetap + Drain** next to a rubber tree to fully automate sap collection into an inventory below.
- **Refined Iron** — the foundation for advanced devices and circuits.
- Build the **Electric Furnace**, **Alloy Smelter**, **Compressor** (raw materials under pressure), **Recycler** (any item → scrap via energy), **Wire Mill**, and **Extractor** (efficient rubber use).

## Mid game — the Industrial machines
- **Industrial Centrifuge** — the workhorse: extracts many new resources (tungsten from lava/endstone dust, nickel from raw iron/gold/copper ore, lazurite from lapis, and more).
- **Industrial Blast Furnace** — needs cooling; leads into steel/tungsten alloys.
- **Industrial Grinder** — higher ore yields, especially using **Mercury** instead of water.
- **Scrap Boxes** — craft from 9 scrap for a random loot box; the **Scrapboxinator** auto-opens them at 32 E/t.
- **Chemical Reactor**, **Assembler** (cheaper alternate circuit crafts), **Vacuum Freezer**, **Electrolyzer**.
- Add **Overclocker Upgrades** (−25% time each), **Energy Storage Upgrades**, and (optionally) **Muffler Upgrades**.

## Power generation
Many options, scaling up: **Solid Fuel Generator**, **Thermal/Lava Generator** (16 E/t), **Solar Panels** (basic 0–3 E/t up to advanced 32–512 E/t), **Semi-Fluid Generator** (diesel/nitro diesel, 20 E/t), **Water/Wind Mills**, **Gas Generator** (methane/hydrogen, 16 E/t), a **Lightning Rod** (262 kE per bolt — stack iron fences on top for more strikes), and the endgame **Fusion Reactor** (16 kE/t) fed by Deuterium/Tritium/Helium-3.

## Endgame — UU-Matter
**UU-Matter** (30 kE + 30 scrap each) can be pattern-replicated into almost any material. Feeds into **Iridium**, **Lapotron Crystals**, **Data Orbs**, and the fusion loop.

## Notable rewards & tips
- **Wrench** — instantly break machines (shift+right-click) and rotate them (right-click).
- **Electric tools** — Drill, Chainsaw, Jackhammer, and the **Omni-Tool** (pick/axe/shovel/sword in one); upgraded versions add 3×3 mining and full-tree felling.
- **Interdimensional SU** — an "ender chest for energy": all of them share one storage pool across dimensions.
- **Transformers are cosmetic now** — unlike old Minecraft versions, machines/cables no longer explode from a too-high power tier. Transformers only *restrict* energy flow.

## Machine configuration
Click the wrench icon on the left of any supporting machine's UI, pick a slot, then a side, to cycle input/output/disabled, with auto-input/output checkboxes to push/pull from adjacent inventories.

## Anticipated questions
- **Do cables/machines explode from too much power?** No — that was old (1.12) behavior. Transformers now only limit flow.
- **Fastest early power?** Solid Fuel or Thermal Generator; scale to Solar/Diesel and eventually Fusion.
- **What's scrap good for?** Scrap Boxes (random loot) and UU-Matter production.
