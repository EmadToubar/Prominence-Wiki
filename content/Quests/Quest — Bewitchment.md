# Bewitchment

**[[Bewitchment]]** is the witchcraft-and-rituals mod: brews, poppets, altars, chalk circles, demon-trading, and vampire mechanics. This chapter ("Witch's First Steps") sets you on the witch's path. See [[Magic & Spells Mods]]. The in-game **Book of Shadows** is the deep reference — read it for details.

## Walkthrough

1. **The four magic trees** — Gather from Bewitchment's special trees, each with future uses:
   - **Tree of Blood** — bleeds out; found in **Nether Fortresses**.
   - **Tree of Knowledge** — found scattered in **forests**.
   - **Tree of Death** — smells of death; found in **taigas and swamps**.
   - **Tree of Life** — found in **savannas**.
2. **A Witch's Knife** — essential for rituals and scratching (harvesting) trees; take care of it.
3. **Taglocks & essences** — a **Taglock** binds a target (player) to a ritual; **Ghost Essence** (ghost goop) is another ritual ingredient.
4. **The Altar** — carries the magical energy for rituals. Increase its capacity by decorating it with the right materials and planting more trees around it.
5. **Ritual kit** — a **Brazier** (cursing, incense, rituals), **Chalk** (draw the activation circle — keep one of each type), **Salt** (wards off spirits/demons; good for barriers), and **Garlic**.
6. **Cauldron & brews** — the **Cauldron** brews potions from grown/scraped ingredients (and makes "wine").

## Notable items & rewards
- **Poppets** — curse or protect a target depending on use (multiple types); store them on **Poppet Shelves**.
- **Witch outfits** — the **Besmircher** (disguise to trade with demons), the **Alchemist** clothing (doubles brew durations), the **Hedgewitch** clothing (increases crop bounty).
- **Demon materials** — **Demon Hearts** and **Demon Parts** (from killing demons/demonhounds) craft powerful magical items; **Vampire's Bed** (a coffin) lets a player sleep through the day until night.
- **Utility blocks** — mob-spawn blocker (24-block radius), a teleport wall-light (64-block radius), springboard/firewall/blocking brambles, and **Witchberry** brambles whose fruit restores magical energy.

## Anticipated questions
- **Where's the reference?** The **Book of Shadows** — read it for in-depth mechanics.
- **How do I raise my altar's power?** Decorate it with appropriate materials and plant more magic trees nearby.
- **How do I trade with demons?** Wear the **Besmircher** outfit to disguise yourself; demon materials come from killing demons/demonhounds.
- **What's a Taglock for?** Binding a specific target into a ritual (e.g. curses via poppets).
