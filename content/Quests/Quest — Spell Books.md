# Spell Books

This chapter explains the pack's **spellbook system** (built on Spell Engine and the RPG class mods) — how to craft your first books and what every class book does. See [[Magic & Spells Mods]], [[Accessories & Enchanting]], and [[Archetypes & Builds]] for how books map to builds.

## The Spell Binding Table

- The **Spell Binding Table** creates **Tier I** spellbooks and attaches spells to books of any tier.
- Surround it with **Bookshelves** like an Enchanting Table — you need **18 bookshelves** to unlock all four spells.
- **Tiers ≠ power.** A book's tier reflects how it's *obtained* and its complexity to master, not raw strength. The "difficulty" tag (Easy/Medium/Hard) is about mastery.

## Books by tier

**Tier I (Easy) — craftable starters:**
- **Warrior's Codex** — bruiser/protector, physical damage ("A Berserking Path").
- **Rogue Handbook** — stacking damage, teleports, stuns, stealth ("Lethal Shadows").
- **Archery Manual** — passive shot buffs, crowd control, rapidfire ("The Bullseye").
- **Holy Book** — healing ("A Holy Healer").
- Elemental **Tomes of Arcane / Frost / Fire** — ranged casting: Arcane = high crit damage, Frost = quick constant damage + control, Fire = strong hits + high crit chance.

**Tier II:**
- **Paladin Libram / Oath of Defiance / Oath of Vengeance** (Tank hybrids of Warrior + Priest/Monk) — "A Holy Warrior."
- **Death Knight** tomes (three): **Eternal Winter** (Frost, multi-target DPS), **Unholy Teachings** (single-target DPS, pairs with the Decaying Devotion fate talent), **Vampiric Knowledge** (Blood, self-sustain tank).
- **Spellblade Technique** books (Frost/Fire/Arcane Battlemage) — melee elemental spells ("The Spellblades," Tier II Easy).

**Tier III:**
- **Astral Magic** — combines Arcane, Fire, and Frost; 39 possible spells with an element-shifting ultimate. The **hardest** book.
- **The Three Hunters** archery books: **Mintaka's Fright** (frost/stuns), **Alnilam's Sight** (fast rogue archer), **Alnitak's Might** (fire/burning/explosions).
- **Magister's Knowledge** — evolved technique books: **Echoes Unleashed** (Arcane), **Deathchill Tome** (Frost), **Phoenix Rites** (Fire).

## Tips
- Match your book to your [[Talent Tree & Fate Talents|fate talents]] — e.g. Unholy Death Knight pairs with **Decaying Devotion**.
- Higher tiers aren't upgrades of lower ones; pick by the **element/role** you want, not the number.

## Anticipated questions
- **Is a higher-tier book stronger?** No — tier is about how you get it and mastery difficulty, not power.
- **How do I unlock all spells at the table?** Surround the Spell Binding Table with **18 bookshelves**.
- **Which book for a tank?** Warrior's Codex early; Paladin/Oath books (T2) or the Blood Death Knight tome for sustain.
- **Alternative trophy spellbooks?** Yes — the [[4trophy_collection|Trophy Collection]] chapter offers alternate archer/spellblade books, but they aren't stronger just for being trophy-made.
