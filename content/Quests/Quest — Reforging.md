# Reforging

This chapter teaches **reforging** — changing an item's **rarity/quality**, a core progression loop. The system note [[Gear Rarity & Reforging]] covers the same mechanic in the vault; this guide follows the quest chapter specifically. See also [[artifact_reforging|Artifact Reforging]] for artifact-tier weapons.

## What is reforging?

Tools, armor, and weapons have a **quality** (rarity). Reforging changes it. Do it in the **Reforging tab of an Anvil**, which has three slots:
- **Left — Base Material:** tied to the item itself (e.g. a Holy Wand needs a **Gold Ingot**). Hover the reforge button after placing your item to see what it requires.
- **Middle — Base Item:** the item to reforge. **Only tools, weapons, and armor** are reforgeable.
- **Right — Reforge Material:** determines the quality range you can roll (three tiers below).

## Reforge Material tiers

| Tier | Material | Where / how | Quality range |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | **Limestone Chunk** | Overworld — craft from 4× Limestone Blocks (→4) or 4× Diorite Block (→4) | up to Epic |
| T2 | **Pyrite Chunk** | Nether — craft from 4× Pyrite Dust (→1, dust from Pyrite Ore) or smelt Ancient Debris (→4) | up to Legendary |
| T3 | **Galena Chunk** | End — craft from 2× Galena Dust + 2× Popped Chorus Fruit (→1); End Stone can substitute for the dust. Galena Dust is found in deep Overworld caves | up to Mythic |

## Increasing your chances

It's not all grinding — **Luck** improves your odds at higher qualities. Stack passive luck items, **luck potions**, luck **trinkets**, wine, etc.

## The long grind
- **Become a Mythic Blacksmith** — reforge **100 Mythic items** ("Eternal Challenge") to earn the "Piece of Eternal Knowledge" reward (Eternal Knowledge: Of Blacksmithing).

## Anticipated questions
- **What can I reforge?** Only tools, weapons, and armor.
- **What decides the quality I get?** The **Reforge Material** tier (Limestone → Pyrite → Galena). The Base Material (left slot) is a per-item ingredient, not the quality driver.
- **How do I hit Mythic?** Use **Galena Chunk** (T3) and stack **Luck** to improve the roll.
- **Practical order?** Limestone early, Pyrite mid-game, save Galena for pushing your best gear to Mythic.
