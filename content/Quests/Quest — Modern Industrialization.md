# Modern Industrialization

**[[Modern Industrialization]]** is the pack's heavy multiblock tech tree, running from hand tools through steam power, electricity, and finally fusion. The single most important item is the **MI Guidebook** — the chapter repeatedly insists you read it when you're lost. See [[Tech & Automation Mods]] for context.

## Stage 1 — Hand & Steam
1. **Forge Hammer** (and Iron Hammer) — blacksmith-style tools that let you **triple ores** by hand.
2. **Bronze Ingots → Bronze Compressor** — build the compressor *before* the boiler to save many plates on hammering.
3. **Bronze Boiler + Steam** — burns fuel to heat water into **steam** (send it water or the fuel is wasted). Steam machines accept inputs from any side; lock slots to fix an item/fluid type. Bronze machines run recipes up to **2 EU/t** (1 mB steam = 1 EU), so a max-temp boiler powers ~4 machines.
4. Core bronze machines: **Macerator** (recycle → dust), **Mixer**, **Cutting Machine** (uses lubricant), **Furnace**, **Water Pump**, plus **Fluid/Item Pipes** with hatches.
5. **Coke Oven** (steam-powered multiblock) → **Coke** fuel → **Steam Blast Furnace** → **Steel**. Always carry a **Wrench** to view multiblock shapes.

## Stage 2 — Steel & first electricity
- **Steel Upgrade** your bronze machines. Build a **Steam Quarry** (consumes bronze drills to dig below bedrock for ores) — expensive, but one of your best investments; run several SBFs first.
- Move off steam: **Steam Turbine** (LV) turns steam into electricity; **Analog Circuit → Electronic Circuit**; **Assembler** + **Robot Arm** automate machine parts; **Polarizer**, **Wiremill**, **Electric Blast Furnace** (Cupronickel/Kanthal coils), **Distillery**.

## Stage 3 — Chemistry & oil
- **Chemical Reactor**, **Distillation Tower** (adjustable height via screwdriver), **Vacuum Freezer** (cool hot ingots like stainless steel back to normal), **Electrolyzer**, **Centrifuge**, **Heat Exchanger**, **Pressurizer** (water↔HP water, steam↔HP steam).
- **Oil Drilling Rig** — a huge multiblock pulling Crude Oil from under bedrock. **Electric Quarry** replaces the steam version.
- Upgrades: **Turbo**, **Advanced Machine Hull** (MV), **Advanced/Highly Advanced Upgrades**, **Processing Unit** ("computer brain").

## Stage 4 — Endgame
- **Stainless Steel → Titanium → Iridium** materials; **Implosion Compressor** (uses Industrial TNT), **Blastproof Alloy Plate**, **Quantum Circuit**, **Quantum Upgrade** (the strongest machine upgrade).
- **Fusion Reactor** — the ultimate power source, fusing Deuterium/Tritium/Helium-3 into Helium Plasma, feeding a **Plasma Turbine** (100s× diesel EU/t).
- **Nuclear Reactor** — a deep neutron-physics multiblock (fast vs. thermal neutrons, heat management via hatches). It **cannot explode or emit radiation** and only damages the items inside it, so experiment freely — the guide recommends testing designs in creative first.
- **UU-Matter** via **Singularity** + Electrolyzer ("the fabric of the universe").

## Notable rewards & tips
- **Steel Packer / Unpacker** automate combining and breaking down machine parts.
- **Gravichestplate** — powered chestplate granting creative flight + fall protection (no other defense).
- Tools: **Steam Mining Drill**, then **Diesel** Drill/Chainsaw/**Jetpack**.
- Break machines with a **pickaxe**, not the wrench (wrench is for rotate/config).

## Anticipated questions
- **Why is nothing happening in my boiler?** No water — the boiler wastes fuel burning dry. Feed it water automatically with a Bronze Pump.
- **Bronze machine feels too slow.** Bronze caps at 2 EU/t by design; upgrade to steel and then electric tiers.
- **Is the Nuclear Reactor dangerous to my base?** No — it can't explode or irradiate the world, only ruin the items inside if it overheats.
- **Do I need to understand fusion/nuclear math?** No — the guidebook says to experiment with designs in creative until one works.
