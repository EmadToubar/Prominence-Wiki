# Botania

**[[Botania]]** is the pack's nature-magic tech tree, built entirely on **mana** generated and moved by flowers. This chapter takes you from your first Lexica Botanica through mana generation, Runic Altar crafting, Terrasteel gear, and an endgame boss with a hard mode. See [[Magic & Spells Mods]]. Tip from the chapter: **check the Advancements** for extra hints.

## Walkthrough

1. **Seedling's First Steps** — Craft the **Lexica Botanica** (the in-game guide, essential to progress), a **Flower Pouch** (keeps collected flowers out of your main inventory), and a **Petal Apothecary** (crafts many early Botania items).
2. **Purification** — Use a **Pure Daisy** to transform logs/stone into more sophisticated crafting materials.
3. **Generate mana** — Build the tools of the trade: **Wand of the Forest** (commands/redirects mana blocks — "You're a Druid, Harry!"), **Mana Pool** (stores mana), **Mana Spreader** (transfers mana wirelessly to nearby mana-holding blocks; aim it with the wand), and a **Mana Tablet** (portable mana battery, ~half a pool each). Experiment with the different **generating flowers**.
4. **Functional flowers** — Create a flower with a unique function, then branch into rituals and creations using different flora.
5. **Runic Altar & Runes** — The **Runic Altar** (connected to a mana pool) crafts **Runes** used in many recipes. The **Terrestrial Agglomeration Plate** infuses three mana-dipped objects into a new ingot, consuming half a mana pool's capacity — the ritual is wasted if the items aren't on the plate.
6. **Terrasteel** — Craft the expensive **Terrasteel Ingot**, then work through **Manasteel** gear before it.

## Gear progression & rewards
- **Manasteel Tools / Armor** — better than iron, self-repair using mana.
- **Manaweave Armor** — cheaper mana upkeep than the manasteel set, but weaker defense.
- **Terrasteel Armor** — stronger than manasteel, and passively charges Mana Tablets in your inventory.
- **Terrasteel weapons (3)** — stronger than their manasteel variants; also rank up your **Terra Shatterer** pickaxe. Its highest rank is the **Earthshaker** ("drill through the heavens and the earth").
- **Upgraded Mana Spreaders** — the Elven-tier spreader has higher payout; the best one is slower but pays out the most. Try different **lenses** on your spreaders.

## Boss & hard mode
Defeating the chapter's boss grants rewards and a chance at an **artifact** (collect them all). There is a **hard mode** that re-fights the boss for more rewards — the quest teases doing it **without armor / without creative mode**.

## Anticipated questions
- **Where's the in-game guide?** The **Lexica Botanica** — craft it first, it explains every block.
- **My Agglomeration Plate ritual failed.** The items must be *on the plate*, and you need half a mana pool's worth of mana; otherwise the ritual is wasted.
- **Manasteel vs Manaweave vs Terrasteel?** Manasteel = your first real set; Manaweave = cheaper mana cost, less defense; Terrasteel = strongest and charges tablets passively.
