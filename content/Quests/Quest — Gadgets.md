# Gadgets

A grab-bag chapter about the "unique and quirky items/blocks in Prominence" — many of them useful even for the tech mods. No strict progression; just discover and craft the gadgets you want. See [[Quality-of-Life & UI Mods]] and [[Tech & Automation Mods]].

## Notable gadgets

**Ore & resource tricks**
- **Copper Pan** — an early-game alternative to tech-mod ore farms: put sand/gravel below and water above, and it rarely generates ore nuggets, gravel, emeralds, and misc items.

**Effect & filter blocks**
- **Blank Plates → Effect Plates** — step on an Effect Plate to get its effect. Make plates by striking Blank Plates with **lightning**.
- **Damage Plate** — hurts mobs down to **1 HP** but never kills, perfect for a Looting IV sword ("hurt mobs, but kill them yourself"). Plates don't apply enchants — your weapon does.
- **Mob Filters** — blocks only certain entities can pass through (more than just player filters).
- **Cog Blocks** (Supplementaries) — act like redstone but can be placed in mid-air.

**Movement**
- **Gliders** (Kibe) — affordable, one of the best ways to traverse large areas before the End ("not quite flying, not quite falling").
- **Levitation Block** — a limited zone of creative flight (alternatives: Flügel Tiara, Gravichestplate).
- **Earthdive Spear** — pass through a wall without hurting your head.

**Utility & base blocks**
- **Trash Can** — deletes unwanted items ("apex predator of an unorganized base"); also a handheld version.
- **Animal Feeding Trough** — auto-feeds your animals.
- **Waxing Staff** — wax blocks without doing it by hand.
- **Quiver** (Supplementaries) — store lots of arrows.
- **Mob Ward** (Bosses of Mass Destruction) — stops hostile spawns in a large area.
- Spawn-blocking **torches** — one stops **animal** spawns, another stops **aquatic** spawns (handy for a trident farm).

## Anticipated questions
- **Early ore source without a tech mod?** The **Copper Pan** (sand/gravel below, water above) slowly yields nuggets and odds and ends.
- **How do I make Effect/Damage Plates?** Strike Blank Plates with **lightning**.
- **Best pre-End travel gadget?** A **Glider**.
- **Do the damage plates use my sword's Looting?** Plates don't apply enchants; they just leave mobs at 1 HP so *you* land the killing blow with your enchanted weapon.
