# Collectibles

The **Explorer's Journal** chapter — a collection hunt for "items that you wouldn't actively find." It's framed as three god- and captain-themed collecting quests: fishing up specimens for **Poseidon**, chasing down the drowned **Captain Cornelia's** sea loot, and gathering scattered plant life for **Demeter**. It's optional side content rather than main-story progression; you fill in the sets as you naturally explore, fish, and farm. See [[Quality-of-Life & UI Mods]] and [[FTB Quests]].

## Walkthrough

1. **Begin the Hunt.** Open the chapter's intro quest ("Hello young explorer! Welcome to the Explorer's Journal…") to unlock the three collection lines.
2. **Poseidon's Aquarium — go fishing.** Poseidon "has lost all of his fish for his personal aquarium," so find and fish up new specimens for the God of the Sea. This includes a **Nether Depths' Fish** sub-set — the fish you catch down in the Nether's lava seas (lava pufferfish, obsidianfish, searing cod, bonefish, wither bonefish, magmacube fish, glowdine, soulsucker, fortress grouper, eyeball fish, and the long-lava-vision specimen). Complete the collection to earn the **Sea God's Fisher** reward ("You have done the God of the Sea a huge favor!").
3. **Captain Cornelia's Treasure Hunt.** Cornelia "sailed until she fell into the depths of the freezing oceans" — you have to be "daring enough to find her ship and take her treasures." This line branches into several sets:
   - **Send her to rest** — use a special trinket you find to "call the Captain back from the dead" (the quest warns: "Beware the dead").
   - **Captain Cornelia's Armor** — the set she "wore to survive the freezing depths"; collect the whole set to dive deep yourself.
   - **Armor of a Sea Terror** — a pufferfish-like set "for those who love being reckless and fighting underwater."
   - **Terrors of the Ice Maze** — take down the freezing, massive Ice Maze and its ocean depths, and return with the spoils.
   - **Captain Cornelia's Hideout** — once found, search it for her hidden treasure.
   - **Sea Dog Weaponry** and **Abyssal Sea Weaponry** — the crew's lost weapons ("if you stare at the abyss, the abyss stares back at you").
   - **Minecraft's Aquatics** — a "classic set of aquatic life, from Minecraft itself."
4. **Demeter's Garden — gather crops.** Demeter "has left her plant life scattered across the world" and rewards anyone who gathers it. Fill three sub-collections: **Croptopia Seeds** (a large variety of crops), **Croptopia Trees** (fruit-tree varieties), and **Delight Crops** (a smaller set, but "found fairly quickly").

## Tips
- This is a fishing/exploration/farming completionist chapter — much of it fills itself in as you play the other chapters, so don't grind it in isolation.
- The Cornelia line is tied to cold-ocean and Ice Maze content, so it pairs naturally with underwater/frost exploration; her armor is themed around surviving "the freezing depths."
- Nether Depths fish are caught in the Nether's lava, not overworld water — bring the gear to fish and survive down there.
- Delight Crops is the quickest of Demeter's three sets to finish if you want an early completion.

## Anticipated questions
- **What kind of chapter is this?** A collection chapter — the "Explorer's Journal" for gathering items you wouldn't otherwise seek out, split into Poseidon (fish), Captain Cornelia (sea loot), and Demeter (crops).
- **Who rewards me?** The sea god Poseidon (the "Sea God's Fisher" reward for finishing his aquarium) and Demeter, the Goddess of Harvest and Agriculture, for gathering her plant life.
- **Where do the Nether Depths fish come from?** They're fished up in the Nether's lava seas, not overworld water — a distinct sub-set within Poseidon's Aquarium.
- **What is Captain Cornelia?** A captain who "fell into the depths of the freezing oceans"; her quest line has you find her ship and hideout and collect her armor, weapons, and treasure.
- **Is this required for the main story?** No — it's an optional collecting journal you fill in alongside normal exploration, fishing, and farming.
