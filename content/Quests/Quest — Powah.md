# Powah!

**[[Powah!]]** is the pack's accessible energy mod: generators, batteries, and cables that wire into whichever tech tree you're actually playing (see [[Tech & Automation Mods]]). This chapter takes you from crafting the basic electric parts up through the six machinery tiers and the various generators. There's an in-game **book** that explains the mod if you get stuck.

## Walkthrough

1. **Basic parts** — Craft **Dielectric Paste** (the "glue" for electric parts), **Dielectric Rods**, and the **Wrench** (rotate machines, configure cables, link rods to Energizing Orbs).
2. **Secondary parts** — **Capacitors** (combine two for a large one, or cut one into two tiny ones), **Thermo Plates**, and **Dielectric Casing** — the last is in every machine recipe and upgrade ("heart of the machine").
3. **Energizing setup** — **Energizing Rods** energize items held in an **Energizing Orb**; higher-tier materials need more power. This is Powah's core crafting mechanic.
4. **Tiers** — Machinery upgrades through six tiers via higher-tier capacitors: **Basic → Hardened → Blazing → Niotic → Spirited → Nitro** (final).

## Generators
- **Furnator** — burns fuel (coal/wood); higher tier = more power + efficiency.
- **Thermo Generator** — heat source below, water to cool; higher tier = more power.
- **Magmator** — runs on lava.
- **Solar Panel** — daytime power; needs a **Photoelectric Pane**, and a **Lens of Ender** lets it absorb sunlight through blocks.
- **Reactor** — built from **Uraninite** (needs 36 blocks to form); cooled by water or **Dry Ice**; the most involved generator.

## Storage & transfer
- **Energy Cells** — bulk batteries that buffer and transfer power.
- **Ender Cells** — wireless energy transfer between cells sharing a channel number (each needs an Energy Cell inside).
- **Energy Hoppers** charge items in a chest without an Energizing Orb; **Energy Dischargers** drain charged machinery and redirect via cables.

## Notable rewards & tips
- **Player Transmitter** — charges items in your inventory/armor/trinkets ("walking battery").
- **Binding Cards** link a Player Transmitter to machinery for wireless charging (same dimension); the cross-dimension version requires using a binding card **on an Enderman** first.
- The **Player Aerial Pearl** is made by using an Aerial Pearl on a Zombie/Husk.
- Fun extra: **Charged Snowballs** cause a lightning strike on impact.

## Anticipated questions
- **Which tech tree does Powah work with?** All of them — it's a generic energy provider, meant to power [[Create]], [[Tech Reborn]], [[Modern Industrialization]], etc.
- **Fastest reliable power?** A **Reactor** for scale, or Solar/Thermo/Furnator for simpler setups; upgrade tiers with capacitors as you go.
- **How do I charge my armor/trinkets on the move?** A **Player Transmitter** + Binding Card wirelessly charges your worn gear.
