# The Nether

A dimension chapter that reworks the Nether via Better Nether and adds new bosses, structures, and ores. You tackle it in parallel with the mid-game [[Quest — Main Story|Void's Invasion]] bosses (the Nether Gauntlet, Blackstone Golem, and Decaying King all live here). It also feeds later progression: **Pyrite** for reforging, plus materials for tech and Death Knight builds. Structures and mobs here are from [[Dimensions & Space Travel Mods]] and [[Exploration, Dungeons & Structures Mods]].

## Walkthrough

- **Enter the Nether** — The entry quest.
- **Lesser bosses / mobs** — Kill a **Blaze Guardian** (near Blaze spawners in fortresses / Nether Wastes), a **Necromancer** (fortresses), **Withered Demons** (Crimson Forest — smelt their hearts into **Demonite Chunks** to re-summon the Decaying King), and the **Soul Reaper** (Soul Sand Valley, rides The Nightmare).
- **The Blackstone Golem's Ritual** — Build a **9×9 vanilla Blackstone platform**; the **Polished Blackstone holders** sit one block out from each side, on the same level as the platform. Collect **4 Gilded Blackstone Shards** (Piglin Brutes in Bastions are a good source) and place one on each holder to summon it. (This is also a Main Story herald.)
- **The Decaying King** — Spawns in the remnants of the **Decaying Kingdom** (any Nether biome). Use a **Moonstone Compass** to locate it. Drops the Darkin Blade, a Lord Soul, and the Withered Demon Heart. Can alternatively be summoned via Demon Chunks on a Moonstone Altar.
- **Nether Gauntlet** — Explore near lava level to find the **Gauntlet Arena**; shatter its eye. (Main Story herald, Rec. Lv 15.)
- **Structures to visit** — **Nether City** (in lava lakes; Netherite blocks in chests/drawers — very dangerous), **Black Spiral** (many Piglins and loot; dig down into the towers), **Nether Fortress**, **Bastion Remnant**, **Ghast Hive**, **the Citadel**, **Nether Pyramid**, **Wither Tomb**, and the **Nether Jungle**. A "visit all biomes" quest ("A very hot Vacation") caps the tour.
- **Mob-kill quests** — Piglins, Piglin Brutes, Wither Skeletons, Blazes, plus Bone Llamas / Naga as flavor.
- **Ores** — **Pyrite** (essential for reforging items up tiers — mine lots), **Cinnabar** (for engineers/network designers), **Lapis** (limited here), **Nether Ruby** (nostalgic warm-to-the-touch material), **Sphalerite** (zinc + sulfur), **Cincinnasite** (iron-like with special uses), plus **Ruby**/**Sapphire** gems (Sapphire is the tank's friend — reflect aura).
- **Blood Trinkets** — Found in **Bastion Remnants**; vital for Blood/tank Death Knights (see [[Quest — New Mechanics|New Mechanics]]).
- **Endgame tech quests** — "Tap the Mantle" for an **infinite Lava source**, and moving lava to the Overworld via an **AE2 Quantum Ring** + Hose Pulley + Storage Bus setup (see [[Tech & Automation Mods]]).

## Tips

- **Don't hand-mine for Netherite for hours** — the chapter explicitly says to loot **Nether Cities** and **Black Spirals**, or use tech (Tech Reborn compressor overclocks, UU Matter) for near-infinite Netherite.
- Farm **Pyrite** heavily; you'll want it constantly for [[Gear Rarity & Reforging|reforging]].
- Bastion Piglin Brutes are your reliable source of **Gilded Blackstone Shards** for the Golem ritual.

## Anticipated questions

- **Best way to get Netherite?** Loot Nether Cities and Black Spirals, or use tech multiplication — not hours of hand-mining.
- **What's Pyrite for?** Reforging items to higher tiers; mine as much as you can.
- **How do I summon the Blackstone Golem?** 9×9 Blackstone platform, Polished Blackstone holders one block out per side, a Gilded Blackstone Shard on each (4 total).
- **How do I re-fight the Decaying King?** Kill Withered Demons, smelt hearts into Demonite/Demon Chunks, and use one on a Moonstone Altar (or find the Decaying Kingdom via Moonstone Compass).
