# Talent Tree & Fate Talents

**Press N** to open your Talent Tree — the single most important menu in the pack, and the main tool for actually building your character.

Powered by the mods **[[Pufferfish's Skills]]** and **[[Pufferfish's Attributes]]** (see [[Core RPG & Combat Mods]]).

## How it works

- You earn **1 talent point every 2 character levels** (see [[Progression & Level Caps]]). At the level 65 cap that's roughly 32 points to spend.
- Points spend on stat boosts (the [[Combat & Stats|stats]] your build needs) and unlocked abilities throughout the tree.
- **Fate Talents** are big keystone picks unlocked at level thresholds, near the top of the tree — these are what actually define your build. You commit to your build's Fate once you're high enough level; the quest build-guides call this "now that you've reached enough level to get a Fate Talent, choose X."
- Pick the Fate Talent matching your chosen [[Archetypes & Builds|archetype]].

## Fate Talents by build (from the in-game build guides)

| Build | Fate Talent |
|---|---|
| Fire / Frost / Arcane Mage | **Fire / Frost / Arcane Archmage** |
| Runic / Astral Mage | **Knowledge of Runes and Stars** (grants Astral Power) |
| Haste Rogue / Assassin | **Shadow Techniques** (big Haste boost) |
| Ranger | **Sin'dorei Heritage** |
| Tank / Protector | **S'kellak's Blessing** |
| Warrior (burst melee) | **Warrior's Devotion** (synergizes with the Thunderwrath artifact) |
| Unholy Death Knight | **Decaying Devotion** |
| Bard | **A Bard's Tale** (required to play Bard at all) |
| Spellblade | your element's Archmage, **or** Shadow Techniques if attacking more than casting |

These are grounded in the New Mechanics / build-guide quest chapter; verify the exact node names in-game as the tree is periodically tuned.

## Respeccing

A **Knowledge Scroll** is consumed on use and fully resets your tree, refunding every point spent. Nothing here is a permanent choice — experiment freely, especially early on. It does **not** touch your Player Level or campaign progress, only your talent point allocation.

## Beyond the main tree: weapon / artifact skill trees

Several legendary/artifact weapons have their **own separate skill trees** via [[Pufferfish's Skills|Puffish Skills]], unlocked by obtaining that specific weapon. Categories present in this install: `thunderwrath`, `apophis`, `orion`, `vaazkar`, `supernova`, `azhar`, `fyralath`, `ekavar_staff`, plus the main `prom` tree.

- Open an **artifact's** tree the same way: press **N**, then click the **Molten Core icon** at the top-left.
- Each artifact tree has **up to 7 nodes** with branching paths; you unlock nodes by empowering the artifact with **Molten Remnants** (quest rewards, or crafted from Mythical Essences). See [[Accessories & Enchanting|Artifacts]].
- This is a big chunk of true endgame character power, layered on top of the standard Talent Tree.

## Common mistakes

- **Hoarding talent points.** Spend them — early points are cheap flexibility and a Knowledge Scroll refunds everything anyway.
- **Picking a Fate that doesn't match your gear/runes.** Fate + armor set + [[Damage Types & Runes|rune family]] + stat priority should all point the same way.
- **Confusing the artifact skill tree with the main tree.** They're separate; the artifact tree lives behind the Molten Core icon and uses Molten Remnants, not talent points.

## Anticipated questions

**How do I open my talents?** Press **N**.

**How many points do I get?** One every 2 Player Levels.

**How do I reset my talents?** Use a **Knowledge Scroll** — it's consumed and refunds every point.

**Does respeccing cost me levels or progress?** No — only talent points are refunded; level and campaign progress stay.

**When do I pick a Fate Talent?** When you hit its level threshold near the top of the tree. Match it to your archetype (table above).

**Can I change my Fate later?** Yes — a Knowledge Scroll refunds it along with everything else, so you can re-Fate into a different build.

**What are those extra skill trees on my artifact?** Weapon-specific trees opened via the Molten Core icon (press N), leveled with Molten Remnants — separate from your normal talent points.

**I updated the pack and my artifact tree has a stat I didn't pick — can I fix it?** The Artifact Reforging chapter notes you can reset an artifact skill tree once for free (e.g. `/puffish_skills skills reset @s puffish_skills:frostmourne`). Check the relevant quest for your artifact.
