# Finding Structures & Exploration

The world is packed with hand-built dungeons, villages, ruins and structures — and the pack gives you tools so you don't have to wander blindly. This note covers how to *locate* things. Full mod list in [[Exploration, Dungeons & Structures Mods]].

## The two compasses — your primary tools

- **[[Explorer's Compass]]** — a craftable compass that **locates and points to the nearest instance of any structure type** (village, stronghold, ocean monument, and all the pack's added structures). On this server it's generous: **never breaks**, **zero XP cost per search**, a **10,000-block search radius**, and no structures blacklisted. Use it before any long trek. (Opped/creative players can even teleport to a found structure.)
- **[[Nature's Compass]]** — the sister mod, but for **biomes** instead of structures. Genuinely useful given how many biome mods run here (Regions Unexplored, Geophilic, River Redux, Swampier Swamps…). Same generous config: unlimited durability, no XP cost.

Pair either compass with **[[Xaero's World Map]]** / **[[Xaero's Minimap]]**: search with the compass, drop a **waypoint** on the target, then navigate. Warp to the nearest **[[Fast Travel & Waystones|waystone]]** to cut the walk.

## The big structure mods worth hunting

- **[[When Dungeons Arise]]** — the standout: elegant, dangerous roguelike dungeons and structures scattered worldwide. Reliably "there's something worth finding over there."
- **YUNG's suite** — redesigned, bigger, better-laid-out vanilla structures: **[[YUNG's Better Dungeons|Better Dungeons]]**, Desert/Jungle Temples, Mineshafts, Nether Fortresses, Ocean Monuments, Strongholds, Witch Huts, End Island, plus **[[YUNG's Bridges|Bridges]]**.
- **[[Aquamirae]]** — an ocean ship graveyard with dangerous deep-sea creatures.
- **[[Mine Cells]]** — a Dead Cells-style roguelike dungeon (with its own boss, Conjunctivius).
- **[[The Lost Castle]]** — a large castle structure to raid.
- **[[The Graveyard]]** — graveyard-themed crypts, ruins and a boss (see [[Bosses & Recommended Levels]]).
- **[[Philip's Ruins]]** (200+ structures), **[[Sky Villages]]** (villages in the clouds), **[[Moog's Voyager Structures]]** (80+ hand-built Overworld structures), **[[Towns and Towers]]**, **[[Regions Unexplored]]** (~70 new biomes), plus village overhauls (ChoiceTheorem's, Integrated Villages, Villagers Plus) and more.

Note: **[[YUNG's Better Strongholds|Strongholds]]** matter extra here because the End is entered via a stronghold portal (see [[Dimensions & How to Reach Them]]).

## Loot is personal — Lootr

**[[Lootr]]** makes loot chests **per-player** — every player who opens a loot chest gets their own copy of the contents. So exploring a dungeon as a group, nobody misses out because a friend looted first. Explore together freely.

## Exploration routine

1. **Open Explorer's Compass**, pick the structure type, and get a bearing (or Nature's Compass for a biome).
2. **Drop a waypoint** on the target in Xaero's map.
3. **Warp to the nearest waystone**, then travel the last stretch — tagging any new village waystones on the way (see [[Fast Travel & Waystones]]).
4. Loot freely — Lootr means your haul is your own.
5. Watch the threat level: many structures come with their own mobs/bosses. Go in prepared (see [[Bosses & Recommended Levels]], [[Death, Graves & Recovery]]).

## Anticipated questions

**How do I find a specific structure (stronghold, mansion, a modded dungeon)?** Craft an **Explorer's Compass**, open it, pick the structure — it points you to the nearest one within 10,000 blocks. It never breaks and costs no XP here.

**How do I find a specific biome?** Use **Nature's Compass** — same idea, but for biomes.

**If a friend loots the dungeon chest first, do I get nothing?** No — Lootr gives every player their own copy of each loot chest. Loot as a group without competing.

**Does searching cost anything?** No — both compasses are configured with zero XP cost and unlimited durability on this server.

**How do I find the stronghold to reach the End?** Explorer's Compass → search "Stronghold." Note the End portal itself works differently here — see [[Dimensions & How to Reach Them]].
