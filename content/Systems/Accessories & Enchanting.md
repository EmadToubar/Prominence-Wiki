# Accessories & Enchanting

Don't sleep on these — they carry real passive power on top of your main [[Gear Rarity & Reforging|gear]].

## Accessories

- **[[Jewelry (RPG Series)]]** — rings and necklaces found or crafted from gems mined deep underground, each built to boost a specific [[Archetypes & Builds|archetype]] (a Mage ring, a Rogue ring, etc.). A real power spike, not just flavor — grab the ring/necklace that matches your build's Tier guide.
- **[[Artifacts]]** — powerful, uncraftable trinkets found only while exploring. Rare drops that change how you play, worth detouring for. *(Distinct from **artifact weapons**, below.)*
- **[[Accessories]] / [[Accessories Trinkets Compat Layer]] / [[Trinkets and Things]]** — the invisible slot system Jewelry and Artifacts actually plug into. You interact with the rings and trinkets themselves, never these mods directly.
- **Charms** — craft from **3 of the same Potion** for long-lasting potion effects (worn in a trinket/charm slot). They have durability; keep them alive with Unbreaking+Mending, a **Hardening Catalyst**, or **Infusion** (learned later in the Enchanting chapter).
- **[[Transmog]]** — changes how gear looks without touching its stats, so you're never stuck choosing between "strong" and "looks good."

Full mod list: [[Core RPG & Combat Mods]].

## Artifact weapons (S'kellak's Forge)

The endgame weapon system, separate from normal reforging:
- Unlock the **Molten Core** after **Void's Invasion Chapter I**; it forges powerful **soulbound** artifact weapons (only usable by the one who forged them).
- Each artifact is forged from special essences/components (e.g. Frostmourne = Essence of Wrath + Awakened Lichblade + Molten Core). Artifacts in this install include **Frostmourne, Fyr'alath, A'zhar, Supernova, Fury of a Thousand Fists, Thunderwrath, Ash'edar, Ekavar, Orion, Vaazkar** (shield), **Gh'aj** (dagger) and **Apophis** (paladin hammer).
- **Repair/reforge** with **Encased Remnants**; make one **unbreakable** with a **Hardening Catalyst**.
- **Upgrade Trees:** press **N** → click the **Molten Core icon** (top-left). Each artifact has **up to 7 nodes** with branching paths, unlocked by empowering it with **Molten Remnants** (quest rewards or crafted from Mythical Essences). See [[Talent Tree & Fate Talents]] and the in-game **Artifact Reforging** chapter.

## Enchanting

A deeper, custom enchanting system layered over vanilla — start with an **Enchanting Table**, but the table now has **three bars** you manage:

- **Eterna** — the table's *level*. Higher Eterna = better enchants but higher cost. **Your XP level needed is 2× Eterna** (30 Eterna = 60 levels).
- **Quanta** — randomness/chaos. More = more random results. **Don't max it.**
- **Arcana** — rarity and number of enchants. High Arcana can *block* things like **Protection** or class enchants (e.g. **Sunfire**), so **lower Arcana** when you want those.

### Building your enchanting setup (progression)

1. Surround the table with **Seashelves** and/or **Hellshelves** — ~**15 infused shelves** plus a couple of the higher variants gets you to roughly **30 Eterna**.
2. Move up to **Echoing/Draconic Endshelves** and finally **Endshelves** (End City materials, Ender Dragon Heads) to reach the top of the scale.
3. **Filter** unwanted stats with a **Seashelf of Aquatic Filtration**; keep Arcana tuned for the enchants you want.
4. Endgame: an **Enchanting Library** block stores *all* your enchants in one place — it's even **AE2-compatible** (point a storage bus at it) so you can pull any enchant on demand. See [[Storage & Inventory Mods]] / [[Tech & Automation Mods]].

### Tomes, curses & tricks

- **Tomes** manipulate enchants: **Scrapping/Extraction** tomes pull enchants off items; **gear-specific tomes** grant enchants only possible on certain gear. **Read them first** — some destroy the item you use them on.
- **Anvil enchants:** **Splitting** (split a big enchanted book into its components — a key way to extract just some enchants) and **Obliteration**.
- **Curses** can't be removed normally — you need a **Special Cobweb** to strip them.
- **Getting XP levels faster** (you'll need a lot): accessories like the **Golden Hook** and **Superstitious Hat**, the **Lapis Backpack**, and enchants **Looting / Scavenger / Soul Devourer / Bag of Souls**. **Knowledge of the Ages** converts items to XP — **don't use it on bosses.**

Use JEI/EMI (press **U** on an item) and the in-game **Enchanting** quest chapter for exact recipes rather than assuming vanilla rules apply.

## Spell Books (for casters)

Casters cast from **Spell Books** ([[Spell Engine]] / the RPG-series class mods):
- Craft Tier I books at a **Spell Binding Table**, surrounded by **18 Bookshelves** to unlock all four spell slots; the table also **attaches spells to books of any tier**.
- **A higher tier doesn't mean stronger** — it means obtained differently and harder to master. Tier I: Warrior's Codex, Rogue Handbook, Archery Manual, Holy Book, Tomes of Arcane/Frost/Fire. Tier II: Paladin/Oath books, Death Knight tomes, Spellblade Techniques. Tier III: **Astral Invocations** (39 spells, the hardest), the Three Hunters archery books, and Magister's Knowledge spellblade evolutions.
- Match your book to your [[Archetypes & Builds|archetype]] and element.

## Common mistakes

- **Maxing Quanta or Arcana.** Chaos and over-high Arcana ruin targeted enchanting — lower Arcana for Protection/class enchants.
- **Under-building shelves.** You need ~15 infused shelves before the good enchants appear; overshoot on shelf crafting.
- **Using a destructive Tome blind.** Read the tome; some consume the item.
- **Trying to scrub a curse in an anvil/grindstone.** Only a Special Cobweb removes curses.
- **Ignoring jewelry/charms.** They're a genuine power spike, not decoration.
- **Confusing found "Artifacts" (trinkets) with forged "artifact weapons."** Different systems.

## Anticipated questions

**What do Eterna, Quanta and Arcana mean?** Eterna = table level (XP cost is 2× Eterna); Quanta = randomness (keep it low); Arcana = rarity/number of enchants (lower it for Protection or class enchants like Sunfire).

**How do I power up my Enchanting Table?** Surround it with Seashelves/Hellshelves (~15 for ~30 Eterna), then Draconic Endshelves/Endshelves for the top end.

**How do I remove a curse?** Use a **Special Cobweb** — normal methods won't work.

**How do I take an enchant off one item and put it on another?** Use Extraction/Scrapping tomes, or an **anvil enchanted with Splitting** to break an enchanted book into parts.

**What's the best way to store enchants?** An **Enchanting Library** block — holds everything and works with AE2 via a storage bus.

**How do I level up fast for enchanting?** Golden Hook, Superstitious Hat, Lapis Backpack, and enchants like Looting/Scavenger/Soul Devourer/Bag of Souls. Avoid Knowledge of the Ages on bosses.

**How do I get an artifact weapon?** Forge one with the **Molten Core** at S'kellak's Forge after Void's Invasion Chapter I; it's soulbound to you.

**How do I upgrade an artifact?** Press **N**, click the **Molten Core icon**, and spend **Molten Remnants** on its up-to-7-node upgrade tree.

**How do I make a charm or artifact unbreakable?** A **Hardening Catalyst** (or Unbreaking+Mending, or Infusion for charms).

**Do I need 18 bookshelves for spell books?** Yes — that's what a Spell Binding Table needs to unlock all four spell slots.
