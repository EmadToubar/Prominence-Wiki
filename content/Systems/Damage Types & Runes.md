# Damage Types & Runes

Damage in Prominence II comes in three families ("powers"), tied to **Rune** items you collect as you play. The quest book ranks them by strength: **Primordial** (secondary/strong), **Elemental** (tertiary), and **Decaying** (powers of Death).

| Family | Runes | Role | Source mod |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Elemental Powers** *(tertiary)* | Arcane, Fire, Frost, Lightning | Mage spell schools; Lightning drives weapons like Thunderwrath | [[Runes]] (under [[Magic & Spells Mods]]) |
| **Primordial Powers** *(secondary)* | Healing (Holy), Soul | Holy heals/supports (Priests, Paladins); Soul combines with elements | [[Runes]] |
| **Decaying Powers** *(Death)* | Unholy, Blood | Death Knight powers (Unholy bruiser/disease, Blood tank/sustain) | [[Death Knights]] (under [[Core RPG & Combat Mods]]) — *not* the Runes mod |

Building around **one family** — matching runes, gear, and talents together — is generally stronger than spreading thin across all of them.

## How the powers combine

- **Astral Power** = **Arcane + Fire + Frost** together — the endgame hybrid mage/spellblade path (Runic Invocations, Ekavar, Supernova). Take the *Knowledge of Runes and Stars* [[Talent Tree & Fate Talents|Fate]].
- **Soul + element = hybrids:** **Soulfire** and **Soulfrost**, used mainly by some artifacts (e.g. A'zhar is a Soulfire weapon, Frostmourne is Soulfrost).
- **Holy** is the support element — healing and temporary shields cast from the Holy Book by Healers/Paladins.
- **Lightning** is a specialist elemental power tied to weapons like the **Thunderwrath** artifact.

These families map directly onto the [[Archetypes & Builds|Mage archetype's]] three elemental schools (Fire/Frost/Arcane), while Primordial feeds Healers/Paladins and Decaying feeds Death Knights.

## Where runes come from

You obtain runes as drops/rewards while playing (the New Mechanics chapter has quests to *obtain* an Arcane/Fire/Frost/Healing/Soul rune, and an Unholy/Blood rune). Use the recipe viewer (**U** on a rune) to see exactly what accepts it. The **Runes** mod itself ships the **six** Elemental + Primordial runes (Arcane/Fire/Frost/Lightning/Healing/Soul); the **Unholy** and **Blood** runes are separate items from the **Death Knights** mod (`unholy_stone` / `blood_stone`).

Correction from earlier: the Decaying family's Unholy and Blood runes are added by the separate **Death Knights** class mod, not by the Runes mod itself.

## Common mistakes

- **Spreading across families.** Pick one power family and stack it — mixing Fire spell power with Blood gear helps neither.
- **Assuming Unholy/Blood come from the Runes mod.** They're Death Knight items.
- **Ignoring Holy as "just healing."** Holy shields and support are a full role (Healer/Paladin), and Smite-flavored damage matters against the undead Hasturian bosses.

## Anticipated questions

**How many damage families are there?** Three: Elemental, Primordial, Decaying.

**What is Astral Power?** The fusion of Arcane + Fire + Frost — the endgame hybrid-mage resource. See [[Combat & Stats]].

**What are Soulfire and Soulfrost?** Hybrid powers made by combining the Soul power with Fire or Frost — used by certain artifacts (A'zhar, Frostmourne).

**Which family should my build use?** Mage → Elemental (pick one of Fire/Frost/Arcane, or all three for Astral). Healer/Paladin → Primordial (Holy). Death Knight → Decaying (Unholy/Blood).

**Where do I get runes?** As drops/quest rewards while playing; hover a rune and press **U** to see what uses it. Elemental/Primordial come from the Runes mod, Unholy/Blood from Death Knights.

**Is Lightning its own school?** Yes — an Elemental power, mostly relevant to Lightning weapons like Thunderwrath (and thematically to Valkyries).
