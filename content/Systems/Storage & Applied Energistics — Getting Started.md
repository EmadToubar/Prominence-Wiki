# Storage & Applied Energistics — Getting Started

Storage in Prominence II is its own progression track — you climb from a few chests up to a fully auto-sorting digital network. This note lays out that path and the beginner route into **[[Applied Energistics 2]]** (AE2), the pack's real answer to "how do I stop drowning in chests." See [[Storage & Inventory Mods]] and [[Tech & Automation Mods]] for the full mod lists.

## The storage progression (chests → drawers → AE2)

There is **no auto-categorize-into-separate-chests mod** installed (no Sophisticated Storage equivalent). The real end-state is AE2. The sensible climb:

1. **Chests, upgraded.** Start with a Chest and upgrade it via **Chest Upgrade** items (a Blank Chest Upgrade + copper to begin) — see [[Getting Started]]. **[[Iron Chests: Restocked]]** adds a tiered line of much larger chests, and **[[Nether Chested]]** is a single chest holding 8× the usual capacity.
2. **On-the-go storage.** **[[Traveler's Backpack]]** is one of the best early upgrades — wearable, upgradeable packs with a weapon/tool rack and installable Upgrades (Crafting, Void-with-filter, etc.). **[[Carry On]]** lets you pick up and move a full chest or furnace in your empty hands instead of emptying it first.
3. **Bulk single-item storage.** If you want mass storage of individual item types before AE2, the Create/tech side and drawer-style blocks bridge the gap. (Note: the pack leans on AE2 rather than a dedicated drawers mod as the mid-tier — flagged as general guidance; check EMI/JEI for what's craftable.)
4. **Digital storage — AE2.** One **ME network** that holds, sorts, and auto-crafts thousands of item types from a terminal instead of a wall of chests. Steep, but the payoff is the whole reason the other tiers are "temporary."

## The beginner path into AE2

AE2 has its own Quest Book chapter — **follow it**, it walks the exact recipes. The early arc, grounded in that chapter:

1. **Build a Charger**, powered manually with a **Wooden Crank** to start (no power infrastructure needed yet). It charges crystals and makes a **Meteorite Compass** from a normal compass.
2. **Craft & charge a Meteorite Compass.** It points to the nearest **Meteorite** within ~2,700 blocks — spinning *fast* means Sky Stone is in your chunk; spinning *slow* means keep exploring. Bring an **Iron Pickaxe or better** (Sky Stone needs it).
3. **Loot the meteorite.** Mine to the center for **Certus Quartz Crystals/Blocks** and, in the central Sky Chest, your **Inscriber Presses** — you need these presses to make processors, and they only come from meteorites.
4. **Charge Certus Quartz** in the Charger, then craft **Fluix Crystal** (Charged Certus + Nether Quartz + Redstone Dust dropped in water — it reacts on its own). Fluix is the foundation of all ME tech.
5. **Build an Inscriber** and make the processors your machines need (each press = one processor type; each operation needs ~1k AE charged up).
6. Grow into a **Controller + ME Drive + cells + terminal**, then Import/Export/Storage Buses to automate.

## The big caveat: AE2 is hand-assembled

**Cables, terminals, and buses are *items you attach by hand*, not blocks you can place normally.** The machine blocks (drive, controller, energy acceptor) place like any block, but the fluix cables, ME/crafting terminal, and import/export/storage buses **must be crafted and physically attached to a cable** in-world — they cannot be conjured pre-built. A **Certus Quartz or Nether Quartz Wrench** rotates blocks and removes individual parts from cables. Plan on assembling the network by hand, part by part.

## AE2 add-ons in this pack

Once your network runs, these extend it (see [[Tech & Automation Mods]]): **[[MEGA Cells]]** (enormous storage cells), **[[ME Requester]]** (auto-restocking), **[[AE2WTLib]]** (wireless terminals), **[[ExtendedAE]]** (QoL content), **[[Applied Botanics]]** (feeds [[Magic & Spells Mods|Botania]] mana into AE2), and **[[Polymorphic Energistics]]** (AE2 recipes play nicely with the Polymorph recipe-conflict system).

## Anticipated questions

**Is there a mod that auto-sorts items into separate chests for me?** No — nothing like Sophisticated Storage is installed. The closest options are AE2's Storage Bus + item filters, or a classic vanilla hopper/comparator sorter.

**Do I need AE2 to enjoy the pack?** No. Plenty of players run on Iron Chests + a Traveler's Backpack for a long time. AE2 is the endgame convenience, not a requirement.

**Why can't I just place the ME cables and terminal down?** Because they're cable *parts*, not full blocks — you craft them and attach them to a cable in-world by hand. This is a known AE2 quirk, not a bug.

**Where do processor presses come from?** Only from **meteorites** — loot the Sky Chest at a meteorite's center. Use the Meteorite Compass to find one.

**Do I need a power plant before starting AE2?** No — the first steps (Charger, crystal charging) run off a hand-cranked Wooden Crank. You'll want real power (see [[Tech & Automation Mods]], [[Powah!]]) once the network grows.
