# Best Gear — Armor & Weapon Recommendations

Straight answers to "what's the **best** ___?" questions. Short version: there is **no single best armor or weapon** — the best gear depends on your **build** and how far you've **progressed** — but there are clear top picks for each goal, and one rule that matters more than the set you pick.

> The most important thing: **reforging any gear up its rarity ladder (toward Mythic) and matching its affixes/runes to your build matters more than the base set.** A Mythic-reforged mid set with the right stats beats a Common top-tier set. See [[Gear Rarity & Reforging]].

## What is the best armor for pure protection / tankiness?

For raw survivability, the **Tank armor ladder** is: Diamond → Netherite → **Netherite-Diamond** → **Sapphire**.

- **Sapphire armor is the top defensive set** — it trades a little raw armor for **+20 Max Health per piece** and a **damage-reflect passive (~30% less damage taken)**. This is the answer to "best protecting armor" if you just want to not die.
- **Bone Armor** is the tank's utility option — it lets you **taunt** enemies (pull aggro off squishier friends), which is what you want as the party's designated tank.
- Stat priority for any tank: **Max Health + Armor + Toughness**. Fate Talent: *S'kellak's Blessing*.

See the Tank section of [[Archetypes & Builds]].

## What is the best armor for MY build?

"Best" armor is build-specific — each build wants the set that boosts **its** key stat. Your in-game **New Mechanics** Tier guide always names the exact next set for you, but here's the endgame target per build:

| Build | Best / endgame armor | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Tank** | **Sapphire** (or Bone Armor to taunt) | Max Health + damage reduction |
| **Rogue (DPS)** | **Assassin** → **Souldancer** | +5% Haste per piece; Souldancer = highest Haste of its tier |
| **Warrior (Burst)** | **Titan's Shroud** | Highest Physical Damage stat (Hero's Armor for Lightning-Power builds) |
| **Ranger** | **Ranger Armor** | Best Ranged Damage |
| **Healer** | **Prior set** | Boosts Holy Power by a percentage |
| **Spellblade** | **Magus set** (~lvl 50, from the Magus boss) or **Mageseeker Hat** for crit | Hybrid physical + magic |
| **Death Knight — Frost** | **Frozen Champion** | DPS runeblade set |
| **Death Knight — Unholy** | **Plaguebringer** | Burst/AoE set |
| **Death Knight — Blood** | **Crimson Knight** | Self-sustain / tanky |
| **Mage** | Element-appropriate robes + **Mageseeker Hat** for crit | Stack your element's key stat |

Full details and stat priorities in [[Archetypes & Builds]].

## What is the best weapon?

Again, per build — the **endgame artifact weapons** are the top of each tree, and each has its **own skill tree** (see [[Talent Tree & Fate Talents]]):

- **Warrior:** **Thunderwrath** (pairs with the *Warrior's Devotion* Fate).
- **Ranger:** **Orion** — the only bow artifact.
- **Spellblade:** **A'zhar** (Soulfire), **Ash'edar** (Fire & Arcane), **Frostmourne** (Frost), or **Supernova** (Astral).
- **Astral / Runic Mage:** the **Ekavar** staff — the true endgame, strongest and hardest to pilot.
- Other legendary/artifact weapons with their own skill trees include **Apophis**, **Vaazkar**, and **Fyra'lath**.

Getting an artifact *and* leveling its personal skill tree is a multi-session chase goal.

## What's the "best build" for a new player?

Rated **Easy** in the in-game build guides: **Fire Mage**, **Ranger**, or **Tank**. **Fast Rogue** is very rewarding but rated Medium. The strongest mage path is **Runic/Astral** (Knowledge of Runes and Stars + Ekavar) but it's the hardest to play. See [[Archetypes & Builds]].

## The rule that beats picking the "best" set

1. **Match the set to your build's key stat** (above) — a Rogue in tank armor underperforms.
2. **Reforge it up the rarity ladder** toward **Mythic**, and use **Luck** to improve rolls — see [[Gear Rarity & Reforging]].
3. **Match your runes and Fate Talent** to the same damage family — see [[Damage Types & Runes]] and [[Talent Tree & Fate Talents]].
4. In **Hasturian Era**, **Corruption Infusions** (add corruption for corruption-scaled abilities) and **Etyr Infusions** (add corruption resistance) further tune armor — see [[Progression & Level Caps]].

Do those four and your gear is "the best" for you, whatever the base set.

## Anticipated questions

**What is the best armor in the modpack?** There isn't one universal best — it depends on your build. For pure defense/tankiness, **Sapphire** (+20 Max Health per piece, ~30% damage reduction) is the top set. For your specific build, see the table above; your in-game Tier guide names the exact next set.

**What's the best protective / defensive armor?** **Sapphire armor**, the top of the tank ladder (Diamond → Netherite → Netherite-Diamond → Sapphire). Use **Bone Armor** if you want to taunt as the party tank.

**What's the best armor for a mage / rogue / warrior / ranger?** Mage: element robes + Mageseeker Hat; Rogue: Assassin → Souldancer; Warrior: Titan's Shroud; Ranger: Ranger Armor. See the table.

**What's the best weapon in the pack?** The endgame **artifact weapons** — Thunderwrath (warrior), Orion (ranger bow), A'zhar/Ash'edar/Frostmourne/Supernova (spellblade), Ekavar staff (astral mage). Each has its own skill tree.

**Is netherite the best armor?** No — netherite is a *step* on the tank ladder; **Netherite-Diamond** and then **Sapphire** are above it, and every build has a better set that boosts its key stat.

**Does the armor set matter more than reforging?** No — **reforging to a higher rarity (up to Mythic) with the right affixes and runes matters more** than which base set you wear. A well-reforged set beats a raw top-tier one.

**How do I make my armor the best it can be?** Match set → your build's key stat, reforge toward Mythic (use Luck), match runes + Fate to your damage family, and add Corruption/Etyr infusions in Hasturian Era.
