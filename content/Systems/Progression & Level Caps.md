# Progression & Level Caps

## Player Level ≠ vanilla XP

Two completely separate bars:
- **Vanilla XP** (green bar on the hotbar) — works like normal Minecraft: mining, smelting, enchanting.
- **Player Level** (shown top-left of your inventory screen) — your real RPG level. Grows **only from combat** (killing enemies and bosses). Each level gives more max health and lets enemies scale up to your level.

Many bosses have a **recommended (or required) level** shown in their quest description — under-leveled means you get crushed. See [[Combat & Stats]].

## Campaigns & Level Caps

Your maximum possible level is gated by **story progress**, not by how much you grind — this is the single most common point of confusion:

| Level range | Unlocked by |
|---|---|
| 1–10 | Available from the start |
| 11–50 | Defeating the **first boss of the Void's Invasion** campaign (the **Night Lich**) |
| 51–65 | **Starting the Hasturian Era** campaign (after finishing all of Void's Invasion) |

**Level 65 is the current hard cap.** If you feel stuck at a level with nothing giving XP, you haven't cleared the next story gate yet — advance the Main Story quest chapter, that's the fix, not more grinding.

## The two campaigns, in order

1. **Void's Invasion** — the first campaign (Main Story chapters). Its early bosses include the **Night Lich** (recommended level 10, ranged-friendly; find him on a broken tower in cold biomes using **Soul Stars** dropped by undead — killing him **unlocks 11–50 leveling**), the **Nether Gauntlet** (rec. level 15), and the **Blackstone Golem** (rec. level 25, summoned by a Blackstone ritual). Void's Invasion has multiple chapters (Chapter I unlocks the **Molten Core** for [[Accessories & Enchanting|artifact weapons]]).
2. **Hasturian Era** — unlocked only after **finishing every chapter of Void's Invasion**. Story: S'kellak once imprisoned C'thulhu, but his half-brother **Hastur, the King in Yellow**, broke free and now threatens all realms. You're summoned by beacon to **Vaaz**. Its bosses are level **60–65 required** and its enemies are **undead — enchant weapons with Smite** (the questline explicitly tells you this).

## The progression stages in order

1. **Stage 0 — First hour.** See [[Getting Started]].
2. **Stage 1 — Early game (levels 1–10).** Explore, fight, start eyeing an [[Archetypes & Builds|archetype]], spend early [[Talent Tree & Fate Talents|talent points]] as they come in. You're capped at 10 regardless, so there's no rush.
3. **Stage 2 — Breaking the first cap.** Beat the first Void's Invasion boss (Night Lich). Gear up first via [[Gear Rarity & Reforging|reforging]] with Limestone Chunks before the fight; bring **4+ Soul Stars** and consider a ranged build for this one.
4. **Stage 3 — Mid game (levels 11–50).** Commit to an archetype and its Fate Talent, keep reforging with Pyrite Chunks, start a parallel tech/magic track (see [[Tech & Automation Mods]], [[Magic & Spells Mods]]), explore overhauled dimensions, and unlock your first **Fate Talent** for your build.
5. **Stage 4 — Unlocking the final bracket (51–65).** Finish all of Void's Invasion, then simply **starting the Hasturian Era chapter** unlocks this bracket. Harder fights, better rewards; "The Scarred Night" and "Mastering Fate" chapters open up.
6. **Stage 5 — Endgame.** Legendary/artifact weapon skill trees, Mythic-tier gear via Galena Chunks, Ad Astra space travel, Runic/Astral endgame magic, Transmogrification, and the level-60–65 Hasturian bosses culminating against Hastur's forces.

## Common mistakes

- **"I'm level 10 and can't level up — the pack is broken."** It isn't. You must beat the Night Lich to lift the cap. Read his Main Story quest.
- **Skipping recommended levels.** A "recommended level 25" boss at level 15 will delete you. Level and reforge first.
- **Trying to jump into Hasturian Era early.** It stays locked until *every* Void's Invasion chapter is done.
- **Forgetting Smite in the Hasturian Era.** Its bosses/mobs are undead and some have barriers you break faster with Smite/spell power.

## Anticipated questions

**What's the max level?** 65, and it's the current hard cap.

**How do I raise the level cap?** By story progress, not grinding: beat the Night Lich (→50), then start Hasturian Era (→65).

**Why won't I gain levels from mining or farming?** Player Level is combat-only. Mining fills the separate vanilla XP bar.

**Which boss breaks the first cap and how do I find it?** The **Night Lich**. Kill undead to collect **Soul Stars**, use 4+ of them to be guided to his broken tower in a cold biome, then defeat him.

**What order do the campaigns go?** Void's Invasion first (all chapters), then Hasturian Era.

**A boss says "Required Level 60" — is that recommended or mandatory?** "Required" gates the fight; "Recommended" is advisory but ignoring it usually means death. Both appear in quest descriptions.

**Do levels reset if I respec talents?** No. A **Knowledge Scroll** only refunds *talent points*; your Player Level and campaign progress are untouched. See [[Talent Tree & Fate Talents]].
