# Fast Travel & Waystones

Prominence II is a huge world, and you are not meant to walk everywhere. The intended fast-travel backbone is **[[Fabric Waystones]]**, backed up by the waypoint system in **[[Xaero's Minimap]]** / **[[Xaero's World Map]]**. See [[Getting Started]] for the first-hour version of this.

## Waystones — the main system

A waystone is a tall carved block. You **bind** to one by walking up to it and right-clicking, which discovers it and adds it to your network. From then on you can open any waystone (or a linked device) and **warp** to any waystone you've already discovered.

- **Finding them:** on this server waystones **generate naturally in villages** — one per village — wired into dozens of the pack's custom village variants. So exploring toward any village is a reliable way to grow your network. They're also craftable if you'd rather place your own at a base.
- **You must visit a waystone in person to bind it** — discovering waystones from a map item is turned off here. No shortcut; go there once, then it's a warp target forever.
- **Cost:** every jump costs a flat **1 XP level**, no matter how far you're travelling (distance is free; it's a flat fee). Keep a level or two banked before a long-distance hop.
- **Anyone can break a waystone** on this server, so if you place private ones, put them somewhere claimed (see [[Getting Started]] on Open Parties and Claims).

## Waypoints — the map layer

[[Xaero's Minimap]] and [[Xaero's World Map]] (default map key `M`) let you drop **waypoints** anywhere and see them on both the corner minimap and the full map. These don't teleport you — they're navigation markers — but they pair naturally with waystones: mark a dungeon or ore vein you found, warp to the nearest waystone, then walk the last stretch to your waypoint.

- **Death points** are automatic here — a waypoint is dropped where you last died, which works hand-in-hand with your grave (see [[Death, Graves & Recovery]]).
- Waypoints and death points are **shared between the minimap and world map**.

## Sharing & multiplayer

- Waystone networks are **per player** — you each discover your own. Everyone binding the same village waystones effectively gives the whole group a shared warp hub, so it's worth agreeing on a few "home" waystones.
- Join a party (FTB Teams) early so quest progress and group play line up — see [[Getting Started]].

## Practical routine

1. Every time you pass a village, tag its waystone (right-click) so it's on your network.
2. Keep 1–2 XP levels spare for warping home in a hurry.
3. Drop a waypoint on anything you'll want to return to.
4. Build a personal waystone at your base (in claimed land) so you always have a way home.

## Anticipated questions

**Do I lose the waystone if someone breaks it?** Your *discovered* network is stored on you, but you can only warp *to* waystones that still exist. Anyone can break waystones on this server, so protect ones you rely on with a land claim.

**How much does teleporting cost?** A flat 1 XP level per jump, regardless of distance.

**Can I teleport straight to a waystone I've only seen on the map?** No — map-based discovery is disabled here. You have to stand at a waystone and right-click it once to bind it.

**Is there any other fast travel?** Nether portals still work as the vanilla long-distance shortcut, and some dimensions have their own transit — but for the Overworld, waystones plus waypoints are the system. There's no `/tp` for regular players.

**Waystones vs. waypoints — what's the difference?** Waystones physically teleport you (for 1 level). Xaero's waypoints are just map markers you navigate toward; they don't move you.
