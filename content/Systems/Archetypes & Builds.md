# Archetypes & Builds

There's no hard class lock in Prominence II — you *build* a class out of [[Talent Tree & Fate Talents|talents]], gear, and weapon type. Pick a rough direction and commit your [[Gear Rarity & Reforging|gear]] and [[Damage Types & Runes|runes]] toward it. Nothing is permanent: a Knowledge Scroll respecs you any time.

The **New Mechanics** quest chapter contains full **build guides** organized as Tier I → IV, each telling you the next armor set, weapon, stat priority, [[Talent Tree & Fate Talents|Fate Talent]] and endgame artifact for that build. Everything below is grounded in those guides — always open the in-game guide for your build for the exact next step.

## How to think about stats

Every build is really a **stat priority**. Match your gear affixes, runes and talents to the 1–2 stats that matter for you (see [[Combat & Stats]]):
- **Melee DPS (Rogue):** Haste, then Physical Damage.
- **Melee Burst (Warrior):** Physical Damage.
- **Ranged (Ranger):** Ranged Damage, then Draw Speed.
- **Mage:** the element's spell power + Spell Crit/Haste.
- **Tank:** Max Health + Armor/Toughness.
- **Healer:** Holy Power.

## Mage (Elemental)

Powered by the **[[Wizards (RPG Series)]]** mod and [[Spell Engine]]. A Mage deals ranged damage with spells cast from a **Spell Book** (see [[Accessories & Enchanting|spell books]]). Start with a **Wizard Staff** (or an element-specific staff) + **Wizard Robes**.

- **Fire** *(Easy)* — high constant damage, no utility. Priority **Crit Chance** (crits as often as possible), then Crit Damage. Fate: *Fire Archmage*.
- **Frost** *(Easy/Medium)* — moderate damage + utility (slows enemies). Priority **Spell Haste**; Crit Chance/Damage secondary. Fate: *Frost Archmage*.
- **Arcane** *(Easy)* — burstiest; slow, hard-hitting spells. Priority **Crit Damage**, then Crit Chance. Fate: *Arcane Archmage*.
- **Elemental Wizard** *(Hard)* — uses all three schools; more versatile but weaker single-target. Path toward Astral.
- **Runic Invocations / Astral Magic** *(Hard)* — true endgame; blends all three schools via **Astral Power**. Strongest, hardest to pilot. Fate: *Knowledge of Runes and Stars*.
- **Endgame weapon:** **Ekavar, the Great Cinderstone Staff**, which evolves via its upgrade tree into Arcane/Fire/Frost/Holy/Astral versions.

## Rogue

Powered by **[[Rogues & Warriors (RPG Series)]]**. Fast, one-handed, dual-wieldable weapons; the "DPS" (constant damage) class. Stat priority: **Haste**, then Physical Damage.

- **Assassin / Haste Rogue** — the **Assassin set gives +5% Haste per piece**; upgrade later to the **Souldancer set** (highest Haste of its tier). Fate: *Shadow Techniques*.
- **Monk** — fist-weapon variant (e.g. **Fist of Fury**), endgame artifact **Fury of a Thousand Fists**.
- **Swordsman Rogue** — endgame artifact **Ash'edar**.

## Warrior (melee burst)

Powered by **[[Rogues & Warriors (RPG Series)]]**. Swings slow but hard — the "Burst" class (high damage, not constant). Priority: **Physical Damage**.

- Endgame armor **Titan's Shroud** (highest Physical Damage stat); endgame artifact **Thunderwrath**, which synergizes with the **Warrior's Devotion** Fate. Alternative **Hero's Armor** grants Lightning Power for certain Thunderwrath upgrades.

## Ranger (ranged)

Powered by **[[Archers (RPG Series)]]** + **[[Archers Expansion]]**. Priority: **Ranged Damage**, then **Draw Speed**. Note rangers are squishier — keep your distance.

- **DPS route:** Shortbows / Rapid Crossbows — low draw speed, high sustained DPS.
- **Burst route:** Longbows / Heavy Crossbows — high per-shot. **Veiled Crossbow** is the middle ground.
- Wear **Ranger Armor** for the best Ranged Damage. Fate: *Sin'dorei Heritage*.
- **Upgrade path:** kill a **Mutant Skeleton** for its chestplate → **Crystal Longbow / Ruby Heavy Crossbow** → the **Orion** artifact bow (the only bow artifact), which pairs with the *Mintaka's Fright / Alnilam's Sight / Alnitak's Might* spell books.

## Paladin / Holy & Healer

Powered by **[[Paladins & Priests (RPG Series)]]**.

- **Healer** *(Medium/Hard)* — priority **Holy Power**; cast heals from the **Holy Book**. Damage is low; add some Max Health/Movement Speed for survival. Upgrade the **Holy Staff**, then the **Prior set** (boosts holy power by a *percentage*).
- **Paladin** — a Warrior + Priest protector; uses **Oaths** (Defiance/Vengeance are defensive playstyle choices). Endgame artifact **Apophis** (the Divine Hand), with an oath of Protection (defensive) or Healing (offensive) branch.
- **Bard** *(Medium/Hard)* — a support sub-path. Requires the **A Bard's Tale** Fate, plays instruments (flute, bagpipe, lute, trumpet, etc.) to buff **party members** (you must be partied). Bards have no fixed gear — mix with any build (archery, mage, healer).

## Spellblade

Powered by **[[Spellblade Next]]**. Hybrid melee + magic — wears **Runic** armor sets, uses spellblade **Techniques** spellbooks alongside an elemental blade. Priority: a mix of **Physical Damage + your chosen element**. Fate: your element's Archmage, or Shadow Techniques if you attack more than you cast. Endgame armor **Magus set** (level ~50, from the Magus boss) or **Mageseeker Hat** for crit; artifacts **A'zhar** (Soulfire), **Ash'edar** (Fire & Arcane), **Frostmourne** (Frost), or **Supernova** (Astral). *Ash'edar* path.

## Death Knight

A darker, dark-magic-and-melee hybrid inspired by World of Warcraft, added by the **[[Death Knights]]** mod. Weapons are **Runeblades** (they carry all three DK powers). Three specs:
- **Frost** *(DPS)* — one-handed Runeblades + **Frozen Champion** armor.
- **Unholy** *(Burst/AoE)* — two-handed Great Runeblades + **Plaguebringer** armor; loves the *Decaying Devotion* Fate.
- **Blood** *(Tank)* — self-sustain via the **Tome of Vampiric Knowledge** + **Crimson Knight** armor. (Go the Protector's path if you want pure tanking.)
- Shared endgame artifact: **Frostmourne**, purpose-built for Death Knights.

## Tank / Protector

Priority: **Max Health + Armor + Toughness**; damage is deliberately mediocre. Fate: *S'kellak's Blessing*. Taunt with **Bone Armor**, or take **Righteous Hammers** for a DPS lean.
- **Armor ladder:** Diamond → Netherite → **Netherite-Diamond** → **Sapphire** (less raw armor but **+20 Max Health per piece** and a damage-reflect passive, ~30% less damage taken).
- Endgame artifacts: **Vaazkar** (tank shield), **Fyr'alath** (soulfire bruiser), **Frostmourne** (Death Knight sustain).

## Guns (side-grade)

From **[[Marium's Soulslike Weaponry]]** — a ranged side-grade: **Gatling Gun** (spins up, decent DPS, slows you), **Hunter Cannon** (highest single-target, long cooldown), **Blunderbuss** (3 fast projectiles, high velocity), **Hunter Pistol** (fast, low commitment).

## Common mistakes

- **Mixing stat priorities.** A Rogue stacking Physical Damage over Haste, or a mage ignoring its element's key stat, underperforms badly.
- **Wrong Fate for your gear.** Follow the table in [[Talent Tree & Fate Talents]].
- **Skipping the in-game build guide.** Each Tier quest hands you the exact next armor/weapon — don't guess.
- **Playing Bard without the Fate or a party.** Its buffs literally require both.

## Anticipated questions

**Is there a class system?** No hard classes — you build a role from talents + gear + weapon type, and can respec with a Knowledge Scroll.

**What's the easiest build for a new player?** Fire Mage, Ranger, or a Tank are all rated Easy in the build guides. Fast Rogue is rewarding but Medium.

**Best DPS vs best burst melee?** Rogue = constant DPS (stack Haste); Warrior = burst (stack Physical Damage).

**Which armor set for my build?** Assassin/Souldancer (Rogue), Ranger Armor (Ranger), Titan's Shroud (Warrior), Sapphire (Tank), Prior (Healer), Magus/Runic (Spellblade), Plaguebringer/Frozen Champion/Crimson (Death Knight). Your Tier guide names the exact next one.

**What's the strongest mage path?** Runic/Astral (Knowledge of Runes and Stars + Ekavar), but it's the hardest to play.

**Can I be a hybrid?** Yes — Spellblade (melee+magic) and Bard (support+anything) are built-in hybrids.

**How do I get an artifact weapon for my build?** Forge one at S'kellak's Forge using the **Molten Core** (unlocked in Void's Invasion Chapter I). See [[Accessories & Enchanting]].

**Do I have to follow the "recommended" build exactly?** No — they're guides, not rules. But matching stat priority + Fate + armor + rune family is what makes a build actually strong.
