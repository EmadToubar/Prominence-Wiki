# Gear Rarity & Reforging

## Rarity ladder

Weapons, tools, and armor have a quality, ascending:

**Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary → Mythic**

Higher rarity means better stats and bonus "affix" effects. This system is run by the **[[Zenith Renewed]]** mod (a Fabric port of Apotheosis) — see [[Core RPG & Combat Mods]]. A separate mod, **[[Tierify]]**, layers its own per-item tier scale on top, which is why two copies of the "same" sword can feel very different in your hands. When you craft or obtain gear it rolls a **random rarity** — reforging is how you improve it.

## Reforging

Reforging **changes an item's quality** and is a core progression loop, done in the **Reforging tab of an Anvil**, which has three slots:

- **Left = Base Material** — tied to the *specific item*. Example: a Holy Wand needs a **Gold Ingot** as its base. **Hover the reforge button after placing your item** to see exactly which base material it wants.
- **Middle = the Item to reforge** — only **tools, weapons and armor** are reforgeable (not other items).
- **Right = Reforge Material** — its tier sets the quality range you can roll.

### The three reforge materials

| Reforge Material | Where / how to get it | Quality range |
|---|---|---|
| **Limestone Chunk** (T1) | Overworld — craft from 4× Limestone Block *or* 4× Diorite Block (yields 4) | Common → Uncommon (through Epic) |
| **Pyrite Chunk** (T2) | Nether — craft from 4× Pyrite Dust (mine Pyrite Ore) for 1, or smelt Ancient Debris for 4 | Uncommon → Legendary |
| **Galena Chunk** (T3) | End — craft from 2× Galena Dust (deep Overworld caves; End Stone also works) + 2× Popped Chorus Fruit | Legendary → Mythic |

*(The in-game Reforging chapter lists each material's guaranteed range; higher-tier chunks can roll the top rarities but not every roll lands there.)*

### Increasing your odds — use Luck

Reforging is a **roll**, and **Luck** improves your chances at higher qualities. It's not pure grind: stack passive Luck before reforging your best gear. Sources include **luck potions, luck-granting trinkets, and even wine** — grab whatever Luck you can, then reforge.

**Practical rule:** reforge with **Limestone early, Pyrite mid-game, and save Galena** for pushing your best gear to Mythic. Reforging 100 items to Mythic is even an "Eternal Challenge" (Mythic Blacksmith).

## Artifact reforging is separate

Artifact weapons (Frostmourne, Ekavar, etc.) don't use these chunks — they're forged and maintained at **S'kellak's Forge** with **Encased Remnants** (repair/reforge) and a **Hardening Catalyst** to make them unbreakable. See [[Accessories & Enchanting|Artifacts]] and the in-game **Artifact Reforging** chapter.

## Armor sets & tiers

Progression is organized around **tiered armor sets** tuned to each [[Archetypes & Builds|archetype]] (Assassin, Souldancer, Ranger, Prior, Runic, Sapphire, Plaguebringer, etc.), obtained by crafting or killing bosses. Each "Tier II/III/IV Guide" quest tells you the next set and weapon for your build — follow it rather than guessing.

## Common mistakes

- **Missing the Base Material.** The left slot is item-specific; if reforge won't proceed, hover the button to read what base it needs.
- **Trying to reforge non-gear.** Only tools, weapons and armor qualify.
- **Wasting Galena on early gear.** Save the End-tier chunk for the gear you'll actually keep.
- **Reforging with no Luck.** Pop luck potions/trinkets/wine first, especially for Pyrite→Legendary and Galena→Mythic attempts.
- **Chasing rarity over affixes.** A high rarity with the wrong stats for your [[Combat & Stats|build]] can be worse than a lower one with the right ones.

## Anticipated questions

**How do I improve a weapon's rarity?** Use the **Reforging tab of an Anvil**: base material (left), the item (middle), a reforge chunk (right).

**Where's the reforging menu?** It's the second/Reforging tab inside a normal Anvil.

**Which chunk do I need for Mythic?** **Galena Chunk** (End tier). Pyrite tops out at Legendary; Limestone is the early-game chunk.

**What's the left slot for?** An item-specific **base material** (e.g. Gold Ingot for a Holy Wand). Hover the reforge button to see it.

**Reforging keeps giving low rarity — am I unlucky?** Partly — it's a random roll. Stack **Luck** (potions, trinkets, wine) to bias toward higher qualities, and use a higher-tier chunk.

**Can I reforge armor, or just weapons?** Both — any tool, weapon or armor. Not other item types.

**How do I make gear unbreakable?** For artifacts, a **Hardening Catalyst** at S'kellak's Forge; for charms/normal gear, Unbreaking+Mending or a Hardening Catalyst via [[Accessories & Enchanting|enchanting]].

**Where do I get Pyrite/Galena Dust?** Pyrite Ore is mined in the **Nether**; Galena Dust is found in **deep Overworld caves** (or substitute End Stone in the Galena Chunk recipe).
