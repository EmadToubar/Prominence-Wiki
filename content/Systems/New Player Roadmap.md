# New Player Roadmap

A concrete, ordered checklist for your first ~10 hours, grounded in the **Getting Started** and **New Mechanics** quest chapters. The single golden rule underneath all of it: **open the Quest Book (book icon, top-left of inventory) and let it lead** — this list is the spine, the Quest Book is the detail. You're capped at level 10 until you beat your first Void's Invasion boss, so there's no rush to over-optimize early.

## Hour 0–1: Land, eat, orient

1. **Open the Quest Book** and read the *First Steps* / *How To: Questing!* quests. Left = tasks, right = rewards. *(Getting Started chapter.)*
2. **Eat something** — grab a fruit or vegetable. Hunger drains faster than vanilla and food gives real buffs. See [[Food, Farming & Cooking Mods]].
3. **Learn EMI** — click items in the right-hand list, or hover and press **R** (recipe) / **U** (uses). This answers almost every "how do I make X" from here on. See [[Quality-of-Life & UI Mods]].
4. **Craft a Chest**, then upgrade it: mine copper and use a **Blank Chest Upgrade** to make a copper upgrade. Storage is its own progression track. See [[Storage & Inventory Mods]].

## Hour 1–2: Kit out and secure a base

5. **Get a Traveler's Backpack** — equip it, open with **B**, and search `@traveler` in EMI to see variants. Big early inventory boost. See [[Storage & Inventory Mods]].
6. **Claim your base**: press **M** for the world map, right-click a chunk, choose "Claim Selected." You get up to 500 chunks — claim what your base needs. See [[Common Problems & Fixes]].
7. **Multiplayer:** form a party now — `/openpac-parties create`, then `/openpac-parties member invite [Name]`. Shared quest progress and teleports depend on it. See [[Getting Started]].

## Hour 2–3: Fast travel and the map

8. **Find or craft a Waystone**, right-click to bind it. This is your fast-travel anchor — keep a little vanilla XP on hand, since teleports cost **1 XP level**. See [[Common Problems & Fixes]].
9. **Set up navigation** — Xaero's minimap is already running; press **M** for the full map and drop waypoints at your base and each Waystone. See [[Quality-of-Life & UI Mods]].

## Hour 3–5: Learn the combat systems

10. **Work through the New Mechanics chapter** — it's the in-game combat tutorial and the most important reading in the pack. Cover:
    - **Better Combat** — weapons have movesets, hold to attack, consecutive hits combo. See [[Combat & Stats]].
    - **Combat Roll** — bind the dodge; it costs stamina and gives i-frames.
    - **Shield Overhaul** — bash while blocking to stun normal enemies for 1 second (not bosses).
11. **Learn the stats** from the same chapter: Physical Damage, Armor & Toughness (Toughness is the "anti one-shot" stat), Health & Stamina, and **Haste** (attack/cast speed — huge for almost everyone). See [[Combat & Stats]].
12. **Open the Talent Tree (N)** — you earn 1 point every 2 levels. Spend early points as they come; nothing's permanent (a **Knowledge Scroll** fully respecs you). See [[Talent Tree & Fate Talents]].

## Hour 5–7: Pick a direction and gear toward it

13. **Choose a rough archetype** using the New Mechanics build guides (Tier I): Protector/Tank, Warrior, Rogue, Ranger, Mage, Healer, Spellblade, or Death Knight. Each guide names the gear and stats to chase. See [[Archetypes & Builds]].
14. **Start collecting your damage family's runes** — Elemental (Arcane/Fire/Frost/Lightning), Primordial (Healing/Soul), or Decaying (Blood/Unholy). Commit to one family. See [[Damage Types & Runes]].
15. **If you're a caster:** build a **Spell Binding Table** and surround it with bookshelves (18 for all four spell slots) to make your first spellbook. See [[Magic & Spells Mods]].

## Hour 7–9: Reforge and prepare for the first boss

16. **Get an Anvil and learn Reforging** (second tab): Base Material (left) + your item (middle) + **Limestone Chunk** (right) to roll better rarity. Craft Limestone Chunks from 4× Limestone or Diorite. Reforge your main weapon and armor up. See [[Gear Rarity & Reforging]].
17. **Optionally start enchanting** — an Enchanting Table plus special shelves (Seashelves/Hellshelves). Keep Quanta low, manage Arcana. This can wait if you're eager to fight. See [[Accessories & Enchanting]].
18. **Push toward level 10** by fighting, and read your target boss's quest for its recommended level and how to find it.

## Hour 9–10: Break the first level cap

19. **Beat your first Void's Invasion boss** to unlock levels 11–50 — the two entry bosses are both recommended level 10:
    - **The Decaying King** (Nether, melee-friendly) — locate the Decaying Kingdom with a Moonstone Compass.
    - **The Night Lich** (Overworld cold biomes, ranged/magic-friendly) — use 4+ Soul Stars (dropped by undead) to find its tower.
    Gear up and hit the recommended level *first* — under-leveling means you deal 10% less damage per level below the boss. See [[Progression & Level Caps]].
20. **From here the game opens up:** commit to your Fate Talent, start reforging with Pyrite Chunks, and pick a parallel tech or magic track. Follow the **Main Story / Hasturian Era** chapters for the campaign. See [[Progression & Level Caps]].

## Quick reference — key binds & commands

- **Quest Book:** book icon, top-left of inventory
- **N** — Talent Tree · **M** — World Map · **B** — Backpack
- **R / U** (hover item) — Recipe / Uses in EMI
- **Party:** `/openpac-parties create` · `/openpac-parties member invite [Name]` · `/opacpvp` (PvP, both must enable)
