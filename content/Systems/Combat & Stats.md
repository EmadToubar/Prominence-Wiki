# Combat & Stats

Combat uses **[[Better Combat]]** (directional attacks, proper weapon movesets — closer to Minecraft Dungeons than vanilla's "click and pray") plus a stamina-based dodge roll from **[[Combat Roll]]** (bind/spam it to i-frame out of attacks). See [[Core RPG & Combat Mods]]. The **New Mechanics** quest chapter is the in-game tutorial for all of this — it's mostly reading, and it's essential.

## How combat actually plays

- **Each weapon has its own attack animation and timing** (Better Combat) — no more uniform vanilla swing.
- **Combos:** consecutive attacks chain into combos; you can **hold the attack button** to keep attacking.
- **Shields** are overhauled (Shield Overhaul): **bash while blocking** to perform a shield bash that **stuns the target for 1 second** (bosses are immune to the stun).
- **Dodge roll** (Combat Roll) costs stamina and gives i-frames — your main way to survive telegraphed hits. Movement Speed even scales your rolling.

## The stat system

Gear and talents grant Diablo/WoW-style stats ("attributes"), not vanilla armor points. Other things scale off them (e.g. Haste defines how fast you attack).

**Core stats (everyone uses these):**
- **Physical Damage** — increases melee swing/punch damage; the main melee stat, scales all physical weapons/abilities.
- **Armor & Toughness** — Armor reduces incoming physical damage; **Toughness** reduces it more on *big* hits (the "anti one-shot" stat). Enemies and bosses have Armor too.
- **Health & Stamina** — Health is your damage buffer; **Stamina** reduces how fast you lose Energy (hunger), fueling dodging/abilities.
- **Haste** — attack/cast speed (also switch-weapon speed). Huge for almost every build, top priority for Rogues.
- **Movement Speed** — how fast you move; also scales rolling.

**Ranged & magic stats:**
- **Ranged Damage** — scales projectile weapons (bows/crossbows/guns). From talents, [[Accessories & Enchanting|jewelry]] and the weapons themselves.
- **Draw Speed** — draw rate for bows/crossbows, fire rate for guns.
- **Spell Critical Chance / Spell Critical Damage / Spell Haste** — the caster's crit and cast-speed stats (Spell Haste shortens casts).

**Penetration & hybrid stats:**
- **Lethality (physical) & Magic Penetration** — the *same* stat, different names: they ignore a % of the target's Armor / Magic Protection. **Worked example:** 16% Lethality vs an enemy with 100 Armor makes you hit as if they had **84** Armor.
- **Astral Power** — the combination of Arcane + Fire + Frost; fuels the hybrid endgame elemental-mage path and artifacts like Supernova and Ekavar's Singularity.
- **Physical-Magic (Hybrid) conversion** — "*Attack to X*" adds spell power as a % of your Physical Damage; "*X to Attack*" adds Physical Damage as a % of a spell power. For Spellblade-style builds.

## Where these numbers come from

- **[[Zenith Renewed]] / [[Zenith Attributes Renewed]]** — the affix engine behind gear rarity and its bonus stats. See [[Gear Rarity & Reforging]].
- **[[Tierify]]** — a separate tier scale (Common/Uncommon/Rare/etc. per item) layered on top.
- **[[AttributeFix]], [[Custom Entity Attributes]], [[Custom Item Attributes]]** and friends — the invisible plumbing that widens how high these stats can go without breaking the game.

Full mod list: [[Core RPG & Combat Mods]].

## Reading your stats

Your attributes show on the character/inventory screens and on gear tooltips. When comparing two items, compare the *stats that matter for your [[Archetypes & Builds|build]]* — a "better rarity" item with the wrong affixes can be a downgrade for you.

## Common mistakes

- **Ignoring Toughness against bursty bosses.** Armor alone won't stop a one-shot; Toughness is the anti-one-shot stat.
- **Never binding/using the dodge roll.** Rolling through attacks is often more valuable than more armor.
- **Stacking a stat your build doesn't scale.** e.g. Ranged Damage does nothing for a melee build; Spell Haste does nothing for a bow.
- **Forgetting penetration on tanky targets.** Lethality/Magic Pen cut through high-armor enemies far better than raw damage.

## Anticipated questions

**Why do numbers feel so different from vanilla?** The pack replaces vanilla armor points with a full attribute system (Zenith + Tierify + attribute mods). Gear grants stats like Physical Damage, Haste, Crit, etc.

**What's the difference between Armor and Toughness?** Armor reduces all physical damage; Toughness scales harder against big hits — it's your anti-one-shot stat.

**What does Haste do?** Speeds up attacks, casts and weapon-switching. It's a top stat for almost every build and the #1 stat for Rogues.

**Are Lethality and Magic Penetration different?** Same mechanic, different names — each ignores a % of the target's Armor / Magic Protection.

**How do I dodge?** Bind and use the Combat Roll — it costs stamina and gives brief invulnerability. Keep Stamina/Energy up.

**How do I stun an enemy with a shield?** Bash while blocking (Shield Overhaul) — 1-second stun on non-bosses.

**What's Astral Power?** Arcane + Fire + Frost combined; the endgame hybrid-mage resource used by Astral builds and artifacts like Supernova.

**Do I need crit on everything?** No — Fire mages and some builds prioritize Crit Chance, Arcane leans Crit Damage, but Rogues want Haste and Tanks want Health/Armor first. Match crit to your build.
