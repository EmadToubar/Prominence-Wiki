# Farming & Food

Food matters more here than in vanilla — cooked dishes give real stat buffs, not just hunger refill, so it's worth doing early (see [[Getting Started]]). This note covers eating well and brewing drinks. Full mod list in [[Food, Farming & Cooking Mods]].

## Read your food — AppleSkin

**[[AppleSkin]]** is turned fully on here: food tooltips show exact **hunger + saturation** values automatically (no need to hold Shift), and holding a food previews exactly how much it'll restore before you eat. Use it to compare dishes — a proper cooked meal beats raw ingredients by a wide margin.

## Grow more — Croptopia + Farmer's Delight crops

- **[[Croptopia]]** adds a huge range of crops and foods, giving farming real depth beyond wheat-and-carrots.
- **[[Farmer's Delight]]** is the anchor of the whole category — it adds tomato, cabbage, onion, and rice, plus **Rich Soil** farmland that speeds growth. Six other mods in the pack are add-ons built on top of it (Chef's, End's, Expanded, Ocean's Delight, Farmer's Knives, Animal Feeding Trough).

## Cook well — the Farmer's Delight kitchen

Farmer's Delight adds the cooking furniture that turns raw crops into buff food:

- **Cooking Pot** — multi-serving stews and soups, with a recipe-book UI. Your workhorse for hearty meals.
- **Stove** — passive cooking (replaces the furnace+campfire shuffle).
- **Cutting Board** — knife-based butchering/slicing of meats and crops (boosted by Fortune). Turns raw meat into cuts for composed dishes.
- **Skillet** and **Basket** round it out.
- Knives come in Flint → Iron → Golden → Diamond → Netherite tiers, extended further by the add-on mods.

From there you build sandwiches, wraps, rolls, pies, stews and soups from cuts (minced beef, chicken cuts, bacon, fish slices, mutton chops). On this server vanilla soups/stews are also configured to grant Farmer's-Delight-style buffs, and villagers can trade FD crops.

## Brew drinks — the Let's Do series

Three "Let's Do" mods add proper drink-crafting (all built on the shared **[[[Let's Do] API]]**):

- **[[Let's Do: Brewery]]** — Oktoberfest-themed **beer & whiskey**. Grow hops and barley, feed them into a **Brewingstation** (Wooden → Copper → Netherite tiers) with **Silos** for grain, and brew Wheat/Barley/Hops beers served in Beer Mugs, plus named whiskeys aged in Big Barrels. Also adds pretzels, sausage, pork knuckle and other tavern food, plus tavern furniture.
- **[[Let's Do: Vinery]]** — grapes and **wine**.
- **[[Let's Do: HerbalBrews]]** — **tea**.

Drinks are their own little progression: crops → brewing station → aged/served product. Great for a tavern build and for group play.

## How to eat well — quick routine

1. Turn your hunger read-out into a habit: check the AppleSkin tooltip and prefer high-saturation cooked dishes.
2. Plant a Croptopia + Farmer's Delight garden early; add Rich Soil for speed.
3. Build a Cooking Pot and a Cutting Board — that's 90% of good food.
4. For drinks, pick a Let's Do path (Brewery/Vinery/HerbalBrews) and grow its crop.

## Anticipated questions

**Does food do anything beyond filling the hunger bar here?** Yes — cooked dishes give real stat buffs, and saturation (visible via AppleSkin) determines how long a meal lasts. It's worth cooking properly early.

**What's the single most useful thing to build?** A Farmer's Delight **Cooking Pot** — multi-serving buff meals from cheap crops.

**How do I make beer / wine / tea?** Beer & whiskey via Let's Do: Brewery (Brewingstation + hops/barley), wine via Let's Do: Vinery (grapes), tea via Let's Do: HerbalBrews. Each needs its own crop and station.

**Can I sell or buy crops from villagers?** Yes — Farmer's Delight is configured so villagers trade FD crops (see [[Economy & Trading]]).

**Is farming required to progress?** Not strictly, but good food is a cheap, permanent combat advantage — under-eating in a combat-focused pack is a real handicap.
