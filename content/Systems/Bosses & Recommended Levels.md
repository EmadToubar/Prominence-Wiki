# Bosses & Recommended Levels

Prominence II is built around boss fights, and **most bosses have a recommended Player Level** — walk in under-leveled and you get crushed. This note explains the level-gating and how to prepare. See [[Progression & Level Caps]] for how levels work and [[Creatures, Bosses & Mobs]] for the mob roster.

## Player Level, not vanilla XP

Bosses scale to your **Player Level** (shown top-left of your inventory), which grows **only from combat** — not the green vanilla XP bar. Your max level is gated by *story progress*, so if you're stuck, advance the Main Story chapter rather than grinding (full detail in [[Progression & Level Caps]]). Many boss quest descriptions list a **recommended level** — treat it as a real warning.

## The boss-gating system

The pack wires many bosses into a shared level-definition system (`boss_level_definitions.json5`) — a structured difficulty curve rather than scattered one-off fights. The gates are **soft** (a strong recommendation, not a hard lock). Confirmed examples from **[[Bosses of Mass Destruction]]** (BOMD):

| Boss | Recommended level | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Night Lich** | ~10 | 650 HP, summons undead, drops a Soul Star after 50 summon-kills |
| **Nether Gauntlet** | ~15 | 900 HP, self-heals, drops Ancient Debris |
| **Obsidilith** | ~40 | Tankiest — 2000 HP, 20 armor, heals; End-themed |
| **Void Blossom** | (optional) | 1250 HP; sits outside the level-gate list |

The same gated roster also includes vanilla's **Ender Dragon**, **[[AdventureZ]]**'s Blackstone Golem and The Eye, **Souls Weapons'** Accursed Lord/Draugr, **[[Mine Cells]]'** Conjunctivius, **[[Archon]]'s** Null, Spellblade Next's Magus, **[[Eldritch End]]'s** The Faceless (X'al), and the pack's own **Hastur Magus** finale.

## The pack's boss mods

- **[[Bosses of Mass Destruction]]** — four high-threat standalone bosses (above). Built to be fought with real gear, not stumbled into.
- **[[Marium's Soulslike Weaponry]]** — a soulslike arc with its own quest chapter and four named bosses: **The Decaying King**, **Old Champion's Remains**, **Fallen Icon → Harbinger of Moonlight**, and **Monarch of Chaos** (pedestal-summoned via a "Shard of Uncertainty"). Beating them yields soul materials for its legendary weapon roster.
- **[[Archon]]** — a rune/mana magic tree; bosses are **summoned on a Pedestal** using ingredients listed in your **Grimoire** (right-click the ingredients onto the pedestal — there's no GUI; shift-click to clear). Its gated boss is **Null**.
- **[[Mutant Monsters]]** — oversized, dangerous versions of familiar mobs; a step up from vanilla, good for leveling toward the real bosses.
- **[[The Graveyard]]** — the **Corrupted Champion**, a multiphase fight (400 HP casting → 200 HP hunting phase, summons minions) summoned from staff fragments, plus themed hordes.
- **[[Eldritch End]]** — **X'al, The Faceless Eye**, a summonable End-dimension horror with an area void attack.
- Plus dimension/structure bosses: **[[The Bumblezone]]'s** Beehemoth / Queen Beehemoth ("Bee Queen" fight), **[[Mine Cells]]'** Conjunctivius, and the Main Story's **Hastur Magus** finale.

## How to prepare for a boss

1. **Check the recommended level** in the boss's quest, and make sure your **Player Level** clears it (level up via combat — see [[Progression & Level Caps]]).
2. **Reforge your gear up a tier first** — Limestone early, Pyrite mid, Galena for endgame Mythic gear (see [[Gear Rarity & Reforging]]).
3. **Eat a buff meal** before the fight — cooked food gives real stat buffs here (see [[Farming & Food]]).
4. **Bring the summon ingredients** if it's a summoned boss (Archon Grimoire recipes, Marium's pedestal Shard, Graveyard staff fragments).
5. **Set a waystone/waypoint nearby** so a death doesn't cost you the trek back (see [[Fast Travel & Waystones]] and [[Death, Graves & Recovery]]).
6. Match your **[[Archetypes & Builds|archetype]]** gear and talents to the fight.

## Anticipated questions

**How do I know a boss's recommended level?** It's written in the boss's Quest Book entry. Check before engaging.

**The gate is "soft" — can I fight above my level anyway?** Technically yes, gates are recommendations not hard locks — but bosses scale and hit hard, so under-leveling is how you die fast. Bring the level.

**Why am I stuck at a level and can't get stronger?** Your max level is capped by story progress, not grinding. Advance the Main Story / Hasturian Era chapter to raise the cap (see [[Progression & Level Caps]]).

**How do I summon a boss?** Depends on the mod: Archon uses a Pedestal + Grimoire ingredients (right-click, no GUI), Marium's Monarch of Chaos uses a pedestal + Shard of Uncertainty, The Graveyard's Corrupted Champion uses staff fragments. Others spawn in the world or their dimension.

**Do I keep my stuff if a boss kills me?** Yes — your items go into a recoverable grave (item loss is disabled on this server). See [[Death, Graves & Recovery]]. But recovering it from a boss arena can be dangerous, so prepare properly.
