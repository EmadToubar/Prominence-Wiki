# Prominence II MOC

**Prominence II: Hasturian Era** — a hardcore-leaning fantasy RPG modpack on Minecraft 1.20.1 (Fabric). This vault maps its systems and mods as a linked network: open Graph View to see it, or navigate from here.

Core loop: quest → level up → pick talents & gear for your build → beat the recommended-level boss → unlock the next campaign tier → repeat, with tech/magic automation as parallel long-term goals.

Three sources always beat this vault for exact specifics: the in-game **Quest Book**, the official wiki at **rpg.prominence.wiki**, and the **JEI/EMI** recipe viewer in-game.
